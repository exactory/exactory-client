1. As an author whose study cannot obtain one source, I want an audited
   decision to continue without it, so that the study keeps the gap visible

   evidence and nothing private, so that my review is independent.
4. As an author who pinned a manuscript under 0.43.0, I want it to stay
   current after the upgrade, so that I do not repeat the reviews.

## Coverage and known gaps

- The privacy check does not decode escape sequences in text that no JSON
  decoder reads, and a substring check cannot find private text split across
  several strings.
- UTF-16 or UTF-32 text without a byte-order mark that begins with a
  non-ASCII character is not recognized by `json.detect_encoding` and keeps
  only the raw-byte check (review probe on `c0ec02f`).
- Whether JSON with an integer beyond the interpreter's digit limit is
  refused depends on the interpreter; both outcomes keep private text out.
- v7 R9 (delivered packet evidence in `round-review`) and R10 (stage after a
  user-authorized deposit) are not part of this release.
