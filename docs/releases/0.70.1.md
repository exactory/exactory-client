# Exactory 0.70.1

This patch extends the history bound of 0.69.20. When a problem result turn does not fit, the harness now also replaces
the part outputs of an earlier series that ended because the problem evidence changed, so that the slot can plan its
final turn.

## Why

0.69.20 replaces, in the replayed history of a result slot, the part outputs of an earlier series that closed with a
recorded final review. A series can also end without a final: when the problem evidence changes, the next turn of the
slot starts a new series on the new evidence. The part outputs of such a series stayed verbatim.

A math-conjecturer record (on 0.69.20) met this. Its slot B had three series: five part turns that the evidence change
ended, a series that closed with a recorded final, and the current refresh. Planning the current final was refused
after the 0.69.20 fallback had run:

```text
review_input_too_large {"stage": "final", "turn": 7, "input_characters": 1129709, "input_limit_characters": 1300000,
 "frame_characters": 1129709, "unit": null}
```

The final also has to attach the bodies of its objection evidence (207,214 bytes), and the five part outputs of the
first series held about 115,000 characters.

## What changed

In `review_problem_history.py`, the fallback also replaces the part outputs of an earlier series when all of these
hold:

- the series is complete in the replayed ancestry and fully observed through its last turn;
- it has no recorded final review;
- the next series of the same slot has different problem evidence;
- a later series of the same slot closed with a recorded final review that is eligible under the 0.69.20 rule;
- every turn passes the slot, role, subject, route, session, reviewer, author and readiness checks.

The placeholder names the first such later review in `review_id`, carries `superseded: true` and a note saying that
the series ended without a recorded final review when the problem evidence changed, and that the named review does not
summarize the omitted output. A later fallback validates these placeholders against the same rule; the placeholders of
0.69.20 keep their strict same-series check.

These stay as they were: the fallback runs only after the unchanged planner refuses with `review_input_too_large`, so
a request that fits is unchanged and every recorded request rebuilds byte for byte; user messages, the bar session, the
latest recorded result, the current series and final outputs stay verbatim; delivery credit (0.69.19) and coverage do
not change. No module that a route pins by its bytes changes.

## Upgrade

No action is necessary.

## Checks

The new tests in `tests/test_problem_result_history.py` reproduce the three-series refusal and its recovery, and cover
missing or altered historical delivery, incomplete observation, identity boundaries, the explicit evidence change, a
continuation without an evidence change, a return to an older digest, several abandoned series, the deterministic
choice of the named review, a second fallback, forged references, fitting requests and request rebuilds. On the
record's store, the final turn of slot B, refused on 0.69.20, is planned with 253,656 characters of replayed outputs.
The module and the related review modules pass on Python 3.12.8 and 3.9.6, with `codex/generate.py --check` and
`git diff --check`. The full suite runs in CI on the pull request.
