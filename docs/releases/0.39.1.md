# Exactory 0.39.1

This release corrects the documentation of the development rounds of
[0.39.0](0.39.0.md) and adds tests. It changes no harness code, record, gate or
obligation. Tasks 11 and 12 of 0.39.0 (the CLI wiring, the example catalog, the
documents, the constitution version and the skills) shipped without a review.
Two independent reviewers then read them against the code and the
[design](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-12-development-rounds-design.md). A third
reviewer then read their findings and the documents for defects that the two
reviews missed. Before the fix, a separate verifier checked against the code every
finding of the first two reviewers and the four major findings of the third
reviewer. The author checked the seven minor findings of the third reviewer
against the code. Two more reviewers then checked the fixes.

## Corrected guidance

- Upgrade from 0.38.0. The 0.39.0 note listed only
  `constitution_revalidation_required` and `round_decision_missing`. Version 3
  changes the constitution digest, so an existing study also reports the
  staleness of its synthesis, cycle assessments, checkpoint and readiness
  review. A study at `evaluate` also pins its manuscript again before it decides
  with `round`. The [0.39.0 note](0.39.0.md) now gives the recovery steps.
- Carried developments. The next `round` decision disposes of a `next_round`
  development in its `carried` array. A candidate alone does not dispose of it.
  The experiment skill and the round gate procedure of the ai-science loop now
  say so.
- The blind cohort prediction. The evaluate rubric and skill now give the
  payload that `manuscript-prediction` accepts. They name the `reasons`, the
  fields that the author adds, and the cohort that the author gives each
  reviewer. They also define the "top X%" scale, where 1 is the strongest
  position.
- The round review. The evaluate skill has a new section, "The round review".
  It gives the question of each check and the fields that the assessor returns.
- The admitted goal. No report shows the admitted goal while a round runs. The
  skills now tell the author to save the `round-admit` output. The author takes
  the success criterion and stop condition ids for `round-assess` from that
  output.
- Blind reviews in a round. The manuscript packet carries the claims ledger as
  pinned, with its `revised` and `superseded` markers. The evaluate and write
  skills now state this.
- The CLI reference. These statements now match the code:
  - where each round obligation appears, and when it clears
  - the transition rules of `evaluate`
  - the goal repetition and direction rules
  - where a field change is checked
  - the error codes of malformed payloads
  - what `round-assess` derives and what the round packet contains
  - the claim marker refusal of `manuscript`
  - the `exemplar` purpose of `require-fulltext`
  - the units of the `development` budget
- The workflow document, the ideate and literature-review skills, `STUDY.md`,
  the README and the Codex README follow the same rules.

## Tests

- The CLI test checks the function of each round command. A new test runs
  `export --kind round` through the CLI, before and after a decision.
- Validation case R24 (`ConstitutionUpgradeTests`): a study prepared under the
  0.38.0 constitution is opened under Version 3.
- Validation case R25 (`test_a_field_change_collects_only_the_category_difference`):
  a field change collection keeps the reading of a member that the study
  already read.

The findings of the review and their fixes are in the
[progress record](../superpowers/plans/2026-09-12-development-rounds-progress.md),
section "Review of Tasks 11 and 12". The tests and the full-suite result are in
[the testing record](../testing/development-rounds.md).

## Known limits

This release documents these limits of 0.39.0 and does not change them.

- `exactory-draft deposit` checks readiness and the publication gate. The
  harness checks the approved `stop` of `gate round` when the study enters
  `deposit`.
- The manuscript packet delivers the pinned claims ledger unchanged, so the blind
  reviewers see its `revised` and `superseded` markers.
- `status` and `gate round` do not report the admitted goal.

## Upgrade and resume

A study that runs 0.39.0 continues unchanged. A study that upgrades from 0.38.0
follows the upgrade list of the [0.39.0 note](0.39.0.md).
