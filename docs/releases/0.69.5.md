# Exactory 0.69.5

This patch cites each paper once in a problem export when a record read the paper both under its arXiv id and under
its DataCite DOI. It also corrects one paragraph of the 0.69.4 release note.

## Why

The export of the unknotting problem record cited two papers twice: once as `2601.18757v1` and once as
`10.48550/arxiv.2601.18757`, and the same for `2609.14520`. The record had read each paper as an arXiv version and as
an OpenAlex work whose only alias is the DataCite DOI that arXiv registers for the paper. The citation projection
skipped a reading only when the family of its own identifier was already cited, and an OpenAlex work forms a family of
its own.

## What changed

- `identities.resolve_arxiv_family` returns the arXiv family of an arXiv identifier. It also returns the family of a
  DataCite arXiv DOI `10.48550/arXiv.<id>` in any case, when `<id>` is an arXiv identifier without a version. Other
  identifiers give no family.
- The citation projection of a problem skips a reading when its work resolves to the arXiv family of an earlier cited
  reading and both works have the same author list. The earlier reading supplies the citation text and the locator.
  Works with different authors, works without authors, and works whose identifiers name two arXiv families stay
  separate. Versions of one arXiv id are cited once, as before.
- The manuscript citation check matches a bibliography entry that carries only the arXiv id to a work that is known
  only by its DataCite DOI. Before, this match needed equal titles.
- `docs/releases/0.69.4.md`: one paragraph said that the new rules for a record reference without a digest apply to
  all three frozen callers. Focused adjudication refuses such a reference before resolution, so the rules apply to the
  other two.

## Upgrade

The citation list is part of the problem evidence digest. If a problem record cites one paper under both forms, its
citation list and its evidence digest change. If such a record has recorded result reviews and is not posted, run both
result reviews again, record a new post decision and export again. A record without such a pair keeps its digest.
Posted problems, stored reviews and exports do not change.

## What stays the same

The reading order, the locator rule, the openness minimum, the reviewer prompts, the recorded packets, the
route-pinned transport and cost modules and `review_state_evaluation.py` are unchanged.

## Checks

The new tests ran first against the old code and failed as expected: six citations instead of four, and no arXiv
token for a DataCite DOI. After the change, `test_research_problem_citations`, `test_research_citation_accounting` and
`test_research_providers` (54 tests) and `test_codex`, `test_manifest` and `test_research_guidance` passed on Python
3.12.8 and 3.9.6. With `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=tests`, 27 modules (699 tests) passed on both versions
before the last review edits, which renamed two variables, removed an exception handler for input that records never
hold, and moved the README line.

On a copy of the unknotting record at revision 888, the projection gives 22 citations instead of 24. The two DataCite
repeats are gone, and every other entry keeps its text, its locator and its place. On a copy of the KMP record, the 8
citations and their digest are unchanged. `codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` pass.

## Corrections in 0.69.6

The independent review of this release found that two statements above are incomplete. The projection also linked a
work through a plain arXiv alias, so a journal article and its preprint with the same authors became one citation.
The new arXiv token could match inside an unrelated identifier such as `10.9999/2609.14520`, and an accounting item
for a work that the token now cites is refused as `already_cited`. Releases 0.69.6 to 0.69.8 correct the first two
points (the matcher in 0.69.8), and 0.69.6 documents the third.
