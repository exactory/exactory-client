# Exactory 0.69.14

This patch closes four more defects of the manuscript citation check, which the independent review of 0.69.13 found.

## Why

- Inside a braced value, the closing quote of an identifier ignored brace depth. With an unrelated title,
  `doi={"10.9999/x{"arxiv:2609.14520"}"}` therefore cited `arxiv:2609.14520v1`.
- A line that looked like an entry head inside a field value started a new entry. `@misc{fake,title={Recorded paper
  title}}` inside the `note` of another entry therefore cited the work titled "Recorded paper title".
- Unicode normalization took quadratic time on long runs of combining marks, in titles, in URL hosts and in plain-text
  bibliographies. 32,000 pairs of marks took about 1 second in a title, and 53 seconds for 50 works in a plain-text
  bibliography.
- The plain-text reference `Another publication. "Available at https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14520"` no longer cited the
  work, because the closing quote stayed in the URL.

## What changed

- The closer of a bracket or quote that opened an identifier ends it only outside the braces of that identifier.
- Matching braces are found once for the whole file. An entry written with braces ends at its matching brace, and an
  entry head inside it is skipped, also inside `@comment`, `@string` and `@preamble` entries. An entry whose brace has
  no match ends at the next entry head, as before. An entry written with parentheses keeps the next head as its limit.
- A run of combining marks is cut to 16 marks before a title is normalized and before a URL is parsed. ASCII text does
  not change.
- A trailing `"` or `'` is removed while the identifier holds an odd number of them.
- A plain-text bibliography is normalized once for title matching, not once for each work.

## Upgrade

- A line that starts like an entry head inside a braced value no longer starts an entry. Inside an entry written with
  parentheses, it still does.
- A title or a URL with more than 16 combining marks in a row is read with the first 16 marks of that run.
- The notes on BibTeX keys (since 0.69.8), on DOIs in publisher URLs (since 0.69.10), on Markdown link texts (since
  0.69.11) and on `already_cited` items (since 0.69.5) still apply. Publication bundles that are already recorded do not
  change.

## What stays the same

The field reading of 0.69.13, title matching, `find_citation_tokens`, the problem citation projection, the reviewer
prompts, the route-pinned transport and cost modules and `review_state_evaluation.py` are unchanged.

## Checks

The new tests ran first against 0.69.13 and failed as expected (60 failures in 8 test methods). After the change, with
`PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=tests`, the modules `test_research_citation_accounting`,
`test_research_problem_citations`, `test_research_providers`, `test_research_publication`,
`test_research_publication_records`, `test_research_publication_files`, `test_research_problem_delivery` and
`test_math_problems_cli` (388 tests) passed on Python 3.12.8 and 3.9.6, and so did `test_codex`, `test_manifest` and
`test_research_guidance` at 0.69.14. Every new performance case took less than 0.2 seconds and every earlier one less
than one second. `codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` pass.
