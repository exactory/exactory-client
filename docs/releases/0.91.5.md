# Exactory 0.91.5

This release corrects the payload of a captured reviewer call. `record-check` now authenticates the capture of a
capacity probe, and the step of a captured call holds the payload of its original command. The release also
corrects three texts of 0.91.4 and adds one sentence to the CLI reference. The release follows 0.91.4. Its record
is `misc/harness-improvement/20261119165105-record-check-refuses-a-committed-call-observation` of the exactory
repository. The corrected texts come from the record
`misc/harness-improvement/20261113110022-every-open-record-and-review-capacity`, and the new sentence comes from
the record `misc/harness-improvement/20261119131613-retire-phenomenon-and-thought-experiment-entries`.

## What changed

The payload of a captured reviewer call:

- When a reviewer call returns, the harness commits a capture of the call (`review_call_observation`). The capture
  binds the digest of the payload of the command. `record-check` builds that payload again from the invocation
  record, to authenticate the capture. A captured call is a call with a capture and no recorded result. The step
  of a captured call holds the same payload.
- Since 0.89.2 the payload of `review-probe` can hold `capacity`, for a capacity probe. Before this release,
  `record-check` built the payload of a captured probe from `id` and `route_id` only. For a record that holds the
  capture of a capacity probe, the step `authentication` then failed with `review_invocation_unverified`, and the
  record was not ready. Now `record-check` builds the payload with every member that the command received.
- On a copy of one live store, `record-check` of 0.91.4 failed at `authentication`. With this change, all six
  steps pass there, all 34 captures authenticate, and the report gives `ready: true`.
- The step of a captured call is the original command with its original request ID. This step records the result
  from the capture without a reviewer call. Before this release, the step of a captured capacity probe did not
  hold `capacity`. The store refused the replay of that step with `request_id_conflict`, and the CLI reference
  said to restore the missing members first. Now the step holds the payload of the original command, and the
  payload needs no change before the step runs.
- The payload of no other call changes. The payloads of `review-run` and `review-output-correction` have no
  optional member. So their steps, the `resume` plans of `review-series` and the `review-run` of a staged task
  send the same payload as before. On copies of 35 live stores, the payload built for each of the 1,740 recorded
  reviewer calls equals the payload of its command. Before this release, five of them differed: the capacity
  probes of one store.
- Each kind of reviewer call (`review-run`, `review-probe`, `review-output-correction`) now names the members of
  its payload in one definition. The start check of the call, `record-check`, the step, the `resume` plan and the
  `review-run` of a staged task read that definition. New tests add a member to each definition and check that
  `record-check`, the step, the plan and the staged `review-run` carry it.
- The CLI reference describes the step with its whole payload, in the states of a stopped call and in the calls
  of a closed chain.

The corrected texts of 0.91.4:

- The message of `review_refusal_replacement_mismatch` for a replacement on another route record ended with a list
  of the plans that `review-series` can give. The list did not include one case. When the route record of the
  refused assignment is retired, `review-series` ends with the handoff `review_route_retired`. The message now
  ends with "Run review-series on the head and take the step that it plans."
- The CLI reference and step 6b of the `math-conjecturer` skill say when `unavailable_chains` holds a row for an
  earlier chain. In 0.91.4 both texts did not state one condition. The row appears only when that chain has the
  statement and the formulations of the new chain. Both texts now state this condition. The skill also names the
  artifact to restore after the refusal of a first assignment: the artifact that `details.path` names.
- In the `math-conjecturer` skill, a sentence on an exhausted turn position said that "the code" names the step of
  the role of the head. Six sentences on three other codes stood between that sentence and `review_slot_exhausted`,
  the code that it meant. The sentence now names `review_slot_exhausted`.

The CLI reference now says, next to the refusals that concern forming records, when a record holds a retired kind
(`phenomenon` or `thought_experiment`). A record holds such a kind when its start, one of its candidates or one of
its forming calls already has it.

One test failed in the CI of 0.91.4. `test_review_request_parity` compares the reports and requests of fixed cases
with a frozen export of an earlier release. 0.91.4 added one sentence on output corrections to the explanation of
`review_context_contaminated`, and the test still expected the earlier explanation. The test now expects the new
sentence. The requests of every case are unchanged.

## Compatibility

No file of a reviewer route's runtime identity changes, and no reviewer prompt changes, so no route becomes stale.
Stored records, requests and digests keep their bytes. The corrected texts change no command, no payload and no
error code.

A record that holds the capture of a capacity probe stayed on the version that it ran, because its `record-check`
was not ready. With this release the check of such a record can be ready, and the record can then activate the
release.
