# Exactory 0.74.0

This release closes open items that the 0.73 releases left: three research records that `record-check` refused, the
missing maintenance of the CI Lean cache, false refusals and repeated Stop notices of the math-search hook, the
rebuilt bundle index of large studies, the cost limit of measurement campaigns, and the recovery of a problem that
was posted outside the post gate. The release follows 0.73.6. The work is recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261105155433-open-items-after-0-73-6` of the exactory repository. Each decision was made
in consultation with GPT 6 Astra at effort `ultra`.

## Why

- `record-check` refused the extremal, sidorenko and stretched-lr records with `record_request_changed`, so they could
  not move to a 0.73 release. Ten failed attempts and probes in them hold requests that older builders wrote and the
  current builders no longer produce.
- GitHub removes a cache entry that no run uses for seven days, and CI saves the Lean cache of `main` only when
  `main` changes.
- The math-search hook refused some read-only shell commands (an environment prefix, a list of read commands, a copy
  that reads a discovery pointer), and a passive session in a parent workspace received a Stop notice on every Stop.
- On a study with 2,526 cohort versions the bundle index weighed 96 MB, more than the 64 MiB evaluation arena, so
  every untargeted reading lookup built it again.
- The measurement runner could continue after a usage limit and lost its call count on a restart.
- The graph rigidity problem was posted on exactory.ai without the post gate and without its local post record, and
  the `math-conjecturer` skill did not say how to record such a post.

## What changed

- `record-check` rebuilds a request that no supported fit order rebuilds in one of five historical forms, only for a
  failed attempt or probe with a null output whose bindings match, and only when the whole request matches byte for
  byte. The step reports the form as `historical_profile`. The attempt stays failed and authenticates nothing.
  Dispatch, authentication and the record fingerprint do not change. On copies of the three records, `record-check`
  is now `ready`.
- A daily and manual workflow, `.github/workflows/lean-cache.yml`, restores, installs, builds and saves the Lean
  cache of `main` with the key, the path and the installer of CI.
- The math-search hook understands literal environment prefixes, lists of read-only commands, `du`, and the source and
  the destination of `cp`. A copy only reads its sources, and a move still changes both ends. Every form that the hook
  cannot check is refused as before. A passive parent-workspace session with no binding and no direct root gets no
  Stop action.
- The bundle index holds bundle ids instead of bundle payloads, and readings build it only for an exact-target lookup
  or a main full-text candidate. On a copy of a study with 2,526 cohort versions, the CPU time of `status` fell from
  271 s to 29 s. Reports do not change.
- The measurement tools are in `tools/review_measurement/` with tests. A campaign counts every admitted call, also an
  interrupted one, and stops at the first usage limit of Claude or Codex, also after a restart. `run_live.py` runs the
  real `review-series` driver on a disposable copy under a separate output directory only after `record-check` is
  ready, refuses a staged worker, never writes the source copy, and does not copy the credential files of the Claude
  and Codex CLIs.
- The `math-conjecturer` skill tells an agent to record an existing server post with `problem-post-external` and the
  server's `createdAt` as `posted_at`, instead of posting again. A graph-shaped test guards this path.
- The regression ledger has nine new `real_shape` entries, and three entries of the math-search hook become
  `real_shape`.

Not in this release: `status` still takes minutes on review-heavy records, where the authentication of recorded
requests rebuilds each request after every reduction step. The fix of that cost, the per-statement planning after a
post, and the managed Codex hooks follow in 0.75.0.

No stored record changes, and no migration is necessary.

## Checks

- The 14 new and changed test modules pass on Python 3.12.8 and 3.9.6 with 259 tests and no skipped test. The
  version, manifest, guidance, release-records and reference modules pass on both Pythons.
- On copies of the extremal, sidorenko and stretched-lr records, `record-check` of this release is `ready`, with the
  matched historical forms reported, and `ci/record_fingerprint.py` gives the same fingerprints as 0.73.6.
- On a copy of a study with 2,526 cohort versions, two interleaved runs of `status` take 271 s and 272 s of CPU time
  with 0.73.6 and 28 s and 29 s with this release, with equal reports.
- The texts were written by Fable and reviewed once by Astra 6 at `max`; its four findings are applied.
- Compile, `codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` pass. The eleven protected modules are unchanged.

The test modules that import the changed modules run after the pull request opens, with CI on Python 3.9, 3.12 and
3.14.
