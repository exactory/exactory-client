# Exactory 0.84.0

This release adds the accounts of a statement to the value evidence of a math-conjecturer record, and it gives the
reviewers the text that reads them. The release follows 0.83.0. It is stage B of the work recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261109153246-abc-type-conjecture-creation` of the exactory repository.

## Why

0.83.0 records how a statement was formed. It left the judgment of such a statement to the bar and the reviewer
texts of 0.82.0. Those texts did not ask three things. They did not ask which statement, among its nearest
alternatives, the evidence supports. They did not keep the worth of a question apart from the evidence for an
answer. They did not tell a test from an agreement that suggested the statement. A value record held paragraphs
with evidence, and each reviewer worked these points out from prose.

The principle that the owner accepted on 2026-11-09 says that the one thing worth being right about is the choice
of the statement. The owner also decided, on the same day, that this reading holds for every problem, and that a
statement posed in place of a refuted one is admitted under three necessary conditions, with the purpose test of
rubric item A7 deciding. This release builds both decisions.

## What changed

- `problem-value` requires `accounts` in every new value record (`problem_accounts_required`). The accounts keep
  the conjectured answer apart from the question, and they enter each fact once, with its scope and its date. The
  first tier holds for every problem: the conjectured answer (`none`, `forecast` or `conjectured`), the evidence
  for or against the answer, the evidence that locates boundaries, the evidence of explanatory use, the nearest
  alternatives with their roles, the parts of the strength under four heads, the relations of the statement, and
  the refuted conjectures that the question concerns. A weaker alternative carries the surplus, which is what the
  statement asserts beyond it, with the status `supported`, `used`, `supported_and_used` or `neither`. A
  consequence is credited to the weakest named alternative that gives it. The second tier is required when the
  record claims a gain or enters a passed test: the checks with what each can show, the gain, and the search
  coverage. The replacement part is required when the statement is posed in place of a refuted one.
- A value paragraph cites entries of the accounts, as `{kind: "account", id}`, and the harness derives its delivered
  evidence from those entries. A paragraph that rests only on expected relations is refused
  (`problem_value_expected_only`). A paragraph cites a full reading directly only with `field_fact: true`, for a
  fact about the field (`problem_value_account_required`).
- A plan of a problem record can carry a test declaration, `test_declaration`, which `cycle` freezes with the plan
  before the execution. It holds the alternative that the test tells against (a declared neighbour or a recorded
  form), the outcome that this alternative excludes, the account of what was known about the outcome, by source,
  and the search for the outcome in print. The new codes are `problem_test_alternative_missing` and
  `problem_test_search_missing`. A plan without the declaration keeps its bytes and its digest.
- Only a run whose plan carried the declaration can be entered in the accounts as a check with the standing
  `test`. The harness checks the order of the records, the text that the plan names, the named alternative, the
  assessed failure signals and the earlier questions of the record (`problem_test_run_invalid`). A passed test is
  experimental support against the alternative that its plan named. Every other computed agreement is an
  observation or the origin of the statement. In 0.83.0 no record made a computation a test.
- `problem-statement` takes an optional `revises_statement_id`, the statement record that the new one revises. A
  revision that keeps the statement and the resolution criteria keeps the standing of every check.
- The harness derives three things for a value record with accounts, and the author does not enter them: every
  run on the current text and on the earlier texts of its lineage, with the assessed status of each failure
  signal; the history of the forms from which the statement descends; and the counts of forming calls, forms,
  comparisons, probes and runs. A failed test therefore stays with its text.
- A value record with accounts becomes stale when the record gains a later run on the current text, a later
  outcome of a form in the history, or a later delivered snapshot of a cited objective. The gate lists one
  obligation, `problem_value_stale`, with the changes.
- The result packet delivers the accounts whole, the derived parts, and the records that the entries cite: the
  plan of each run with its test declaration, the cited searches, and the forming calls with their outcomes. The
  result coverage check requires them in the request of each review.
- Three reviewer paragraphs ship, each with a version id, and a prompt carries one only when its packet carries
  the material. The accounts paragraph (`exactory-problem-accounts-v1`) gives a result reviewer the reading of the
  rubric with the accounts, the questions on the gain, and what stays unestablished about a test. An adjudicator
  of A3, priority, calibration or significance receives it with the definition of the item. The slate paragraph
  (`exactory-problem-slate-forming-v1`) gives a slate reviewer the questions for a candidate that the record
  formed. The replacement paragraph (`exactory-problem-replacement-v1`) gives a result reviewer, and an
  adjudicator of A7, the three necessary conditions of a statement posed in place of a refuted one.
- New command `exactory-research problem-post-fact`. It records a fact about the place of a posted statement that
  arrived after the post: the post, the fact, its source, its date, and what it bears on. `status` lists the fact
  under `after_post`. The record needs no value record, and it changes no reviewed evidence.
- The math-conjecturer skill gains "The accounts" and "A run as a test of the statement" in "Value and the
  rubric", and "The rubric" states the reading. "Formulation" states the reference to the revised statement and
  the rule for an alternative that is named later. "Reviews and the decision" states what the result reviewers
  receive and the stale value record. "Compose and post a problem" states the fact after a post. The three
  conditions of 0.83.0 stay in the skill, and the skill now names the reviewers' replacement paragraph as the
  text that judges them.
- Each refusal of the new code names the repair in its message. `docs/research-tools.md` gains the section "Run".
  `docs/research-cli.md` gains "The test declaration of a plan" and "The accounts of a value record", with every
  part, the rule of a test, the derived parts and the refusal table.

## What an ordinary problem needs now

The first tier of the accounts holds for every problem, also for one that claims no gain. The reading that the
reviewers receive is stricter than the rubric anchors in one place that matters for the post. Priority reaches
level 2 against a weaker alternative only when the surplus over that alternative is supported by evidence for the
conjectured answer or used by a consequence that is credited to the statement. A statement whose exact constant,
exponent or range is neither supported nor used is revised to the weaker alternative that the evidence supports.

A regrade on paper measured this before the release. It graded the nine posted value cases under the reading, with
no reviewer call. Four of the nine fell to priority 1, and each of the four conjectures no answer. Five stayed at
level 2, three of them on thin grounds. The regrade is recorded in the folder named above. No posted statement
changes: the reading binds a statement when its next value record is recorded.

## What stays the same

- No rubric item, level or threshold changes. The response shapes of the bar, slate, result and adjudicator roles
  are unchanged. The assurance `problem_value` covers the accounts.
- A prompt whose packet calls for no paragraph keeps the bytes of 0.82.0. The carry paragraph of 0.82.0 keeps its
  bytes, and a recorded assignment rebuilds its prompt from its own envelope.
- The bar prompt is unchanged.
- A plan without a test declaration keeps the bytes, the digest and the meaning of 0.82.0, in a problem record and
  in a study.
- The absolute principle of problem selection and rubric item A7 are unchanged.

## What stored records see

- A value record that an earlier release recorded has no accounts. It validates, digests, projects and gates as
  before, and its reviews stay current. Fixture tests pin this.
- A posted statement gets no new obligation. A fact after a post is a record of its own.
- A record in progress keeps its state while its value record stands. Its next `problem-value` needs the accounts.
  The new record changes the evidence digest, so the result reviews are refreshed, with the accounts paragraph.
- For a value record without accounts, a later delivery of a cited objective is still listed as
  `problem_result_superseded`. For a value record with accounts it is one of the changes of `problem_value_stale`.
- Records of other orchestrations do not change, and no migration is necessary.

## Known limits

- The harness does not establish that the outcome of a test was unknown to the model that planned the run. It
  compares the questions of earlier probes and runs with the question of the plan character for character, and it
  does not read what the search for the outcome found. The reviewers judge these points, and the accounts
  paragraph says so.
- The account of prior knowledge in a test declaration is the performer's report. The harness accepts any list of
  sources, an empty one included. The skill tells the agent to enter five named sources.
- A gain claim and a passed test need a candidate that cites a form recorded before the evidence. A statement
  whose forming work left no forming record can claim no gain.
- A slate reviewer receives the references to the forming records of a candidate, and not their content. The
  reviewer reads the source reference and the observation.
- The areas paragraph is a placeholder. No record names a further area before stage C, so no prompt carries it.
- The report of an exploration, further areas and the command that carries forming records to another record
  follow in stage C.
- A staged review still cannot post before its quality measurement.
