# Exactory 0.74.1

This release adds an entry to math-conjecturer for open problems that start from a simple thought experiment.
The release follows 0.74.0. The work is recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261105113333-thought-experiment-entry` of the exactory repository. The design and every
review were made with GPT 6 Astra at effort `ultra`.

## Why

Until now an exploration started from a field and the questions of its literature. Some deep problems started
from a simple situation instead. The Kakeya needle problem started from turning a unit segment around in the
plane. The owner asked for a path that starts from such a situation. A problem from this path meets the same
posting bar as every other problem.

## What changed

- `problem-candidate` accepts the source kind `thought_experiment`. Use it for a question that the exploration
  derived from its own thought experiment. The other five kinds and their rules do not change. No gate changes.
- The math-conjecturer skill has a new subsection of "Exploration", "An exploration from a thought experiment".
  It gives the research steps: the seed, its precise interpretations, the expectations stated before the tests,
  the tests, and the candidates derived from each outcome. It applies the absolute principle of problem selection
  to the record's own expectations. It gives the openness searches for an elementary situation.
- A thought-experiment exploration keeps all reviewed work in one record. It can restart in another subject class
  only before its first review request. After that, a favoured candidate of another class is selected and waits
  for a release whose handoff to another record carries the review objections. The reason: a bar or slate
  objection binds only inside its own record. The shared handoff (Formulation, step 5) does not change in this
  release. Its repair is recorded in `misc/harness-improvement/20261105171520-objections-lost-on-area-handoff`.
- The skill description names the new entry, and the Codex entry is generated again.
- `docs/research-cli.md` lists the new kind in the `problem-candidate` row.

No stored record changes, and no migration is necessary.
