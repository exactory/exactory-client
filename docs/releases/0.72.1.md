# Exactory 0.72.1

This release adds the protocol `exactory-openness-reading-v5` for new problem openness reading chains. Search fields
that several bound searches share arrive once, and the harness rebuilds every original search from the delivered
parts before it gives credit. A v5 final turn can withhold a selected source text that earlier turns of the same chain
delivered completely. Each `review-assignment` of a v5 chain reports the final-input headroom. The release follows
0.72.0. The work is recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261103142106-openness-chain-repeats-identical-search-records` of the exactory repository.

## Why

A problem binds one search per formulation: the statement and each declared neighbour. Every material search stays
bound, so a record that records its searches again adds a full copy of each. The reading chain delivered each bound
search record whole.

Measured on 2026-11-03 on the bunkbed record, on the code of 0.71.7:

- The openness minimum bound 15 search records: three generations for each of five formulations. The records held
  4,301,964 characters of canonical JSON. They held only two distinct `dispositions` lists: ten records shared one
  list of 339 judgments, and five shared one list of 271.
- The chain needed about 45 turns of at most 120,000 characters of new text. About 36 of those turns read search
  records, almost all of it repeated text.
- Another chain of the same record bound `native_reviews` in its export. Its packet then protected the selected text
  of every one of the twelve source versions in the final turn. At turn 19, `review-assignment` refused the chain with
  `review_input_too_large` (`phase: final_preflight`): the mandatory floor was 1,303,063 characters against the limit
  of 1,300,000. The protected floor was already about 1.08 million characters at turn 1.

## What changed

New problem chains record `exactory-openness-reading-v5`. A chain that an earlier release started continues under its
own protocol: a v4 chain stays v4, and an older chain continues to v4 as before. Math reading chains keep their
protocol.

- Shared search fields. The first assignment freezes a manifest, `reading_record.search_delivery`. The exact canonical
  values of `dispositions`, `queries`, `responses` and `scope` that two or more bound search records share travel once
  as `search_shared_field` units. Each bound search travels as a `search_residual` unit with its other fields and the
  digests of the shared bodies that it uses. A search that shares no value travels whole.
- Credit. A bound search counts as delivered only when the authenticated reading turns of the same chain delivered its
  residual and every part of every shared body that it uses. The harness then rebuilds the exact original bytes and
  digest. A manifest, a saved artifact, a receipt or a reviewer statement gives no credit. Every bound search keeps its
  digest in the minimum and the finding, and the openness audit visits each of them. Excerpts and passage requests cite
  the original `literature_search` record.
- The final turn. A v5 final turn omits the factored searches with `delivery: "search-rebuilt-from-reading-turns"`.
  It can withhold a selected source text of an `openness_delivery` record when earlier reading turns of the same chain
  delivered that text completely and its digest has no other protected role. The withheld text keeps its descriptor
  with `delivery: "body-delivered-in-reading-turns"` and a note that names the delivering turns. Required proof bytes,
  screening evidence, originals, inspection assets and declared figure, table, equation and supplement units keep
  their rule.
  `validate_delivery` derives the coverage again from the actual requests.
- Headroom. The result of each `review-assignment` of a v5 chain carries `diagnostics.final_input`: the route limit,
  the measured mandatory floor, the headroom, the mandatory components, the selected texts that the final turn can
  withhold with their verified deliveries, the remaining excerpt and passage allowances, and `capacity_warning`. The
  record does not store these diagnostics. A floor above the limit is refused as before.

`problem-openness` and the later assessments verify the v5 chain before the openness audit. The result reviews, the
result evidence parts of 0.72.0, adjudication and the citation projection receive the complete original search rows
as before. The route-pinned transport modules are unchanged. The skill `math-conjecturer` and `docs/research-cli.md`
describe the v5 chain.

## Upgrade

No record migration is necessary. After the update, a new openness chain uses v5. A chain that runs during the update
continues under the protocol of its first turn.

## Checks

The new tests failed on the code before each change and pass after it. They cover the measured shape (15 bound
searches in three generations of five formulations, ten records with one list of 339 judgments and five with one list
of 271, rebuilt byte for byte from two shared disposition bodies), exact field identity, credit only from authenticated
complete delivery (missing, altered or unsent parts, swapped references and forged receipts give none), chain
boundaries, original citations of excerpts and passages, the final search representation, whole and multipart
selected-text credit, two source versions with identical text, indirect asset overlaps inside and outside text-only
delivery, the headroom report, the incident-shaped final (twelve selected texts and 224 retained excerpts), the
end-to-end finding, and the replay of stored v4 chains built by 0.71.8 and by 0.72.0.

On the measured shape, the 4,270,450 characters of the 15 search records become 558,311 characters of shared bodies
and residuals and a manifest of 24,108 characters.

On the release commit, on Python 3.12.8 and 3.9.6: 220 tests in 17 modules pass (the new and changed modules, the
openness, source delivery, native review, screening, minimum and preflight modules, and the manifest, Codex and
research-guidance tests), and the 22 math-solver openness tests pass. The changed modules compile on both versions.
`codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` pass. The route-pinned modules are unchanged. The implementation
lanes also ran the excerpt and parts modules (304 tests) on both versions, with no failure.
