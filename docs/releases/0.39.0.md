# Exactory 0.39.0

This release lets one managed study develop its paper across independently
approved research rounds. It keeps the proportionate literature preparation of
0.38.0, the three-review manuscript measurement, the two-review publication
gate, the five search purposes, and the five to ten external innovation cases.
The design is recorded in
[the development rounds design](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-12-development-rounds-design.md).

## Research workflow

- Five operations: `round` closes the current round on the exact publication
  bundle and decides `continue` with one pursued candidate and a goal, or
  `stop`; `round-review` records the independent judgment of one decision, by
  an assessor who is not a cycle author, with one judgment per check;
  `round-admit` opens the round of an approved `continue`, charges one round to
  the `development` account and stores the opening state the round is judged
  against; `round-assess` judges the admitted round on the exact bundle
  against its success criteria and stop conditions; `manuscript-prediction`
  records one blind assessor's cohort prediction on the exact bundle.
- The `round` gate walks the current bundle, the closing round's assessment,
  productivity and claims continuity, the decision, its approving review and,
  for `continue`, the `development` budget and the admission. Two transition
  rules read it: `evaluate -> literature` needs an admitted `continue`, and
  `evaluate -> deposit` needs the publication gate and an approved `stop`.
- In a round of number 2 or more, the literature stage requires a selected
  search for each consequence purpose (`downstream`, `next_step`, `exemplars`,
  `changes`) recorded after the admission, judged like the five foundation
  purposes, and one `require-fulltext` with the purpose `exemplar` read in
  full (`round_search_missing`, `round_exemplar_missing`).
- A cycle assessment's `next_round` disposition carries a useful development
  that exceeds the round's scope or resources: it leaves no readiness
  obligation, and the next decision disposes of it
  (`carried_development_missing`).
- Objective lineage: an admission can widen the configured objective; the
  earlier objective stays as an ancestor (`research_objective`,
  `objective_lineage`), a narrower or unlinked objective is `objective_locked`,
  and successor cycles inherit checkpoints of earlier rounds.
- Claims continuity: the manuscript keeps every claim id of the round's
  opening bundle; a changed claim carries `revised: {previous, reason}`, a
  withdrawn one `superseded: {reason}`; a dropped claim is
  `round_claims_dropped` and a round without a new claim `round_claim_missing`,
  both at the round gate.
- Each blind measurement reviewer returns the rubric core and, separately, a
  cohort prediction in the market's shape, bound to the study's collection and
  refused for an author or a second prediction on the same bundle. The
  measurement summary (review and prediction medians and spreads) is reported
  by `status --summary`, stored with each round assessment and delivered in
  the round packet; no gate rule and no goal reads it.
- `budget` accepts the `development` purpose with the `rounds` unit; every
  budget payload names the `rounds` unit; a recorded limit at its count is
  `resource_budget_exhausted` for the decision and the admission.
- `status` carries `round` (number, active, assessed, decision, obligations,
  progress, measurement, limits, budget, usage); at the `evaluate` stage the
  status obligations also carry the publication and round obligations, in
  priority order.
- `export --kind round` delivers the latest decision on the current bundle to
  the independent round assessor: the manuscript packet, the bundle's reviews
  and predictions with their measurement, the decision, every earlier round's
  goal, assessment and decision, the closing round's carried developments, the
  synthesis sections, the selected searches and the literature account, with
  no author, request id, token or revision label at any depth.
- `RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION.md` is Version 3 with a section on development across
  rounds.

## Distribution and validation

The shared `bin/`, `skills/` and `hooks/` serve Claude Code and Codex; the Codex
entrypoints are regenerated. New tests cover the round records and the gate
(`test_research_rounds`), the `next_round` disposition and objective lineage
(`test_research_development`), the `development` budget
(`test_research_resources`), the claim markers and the shared assessor check
(`test_research_publication`), the round packet and the neutral manuscript
packet (`test_research_review_packets`), the status view
(`test_research_report_views`), the consequence purposes
(`test_research_literature`) and the CLI registration (`test_research_cli`).
The per-task evidence, the validation-case mapping and the full-suite result
are in [the testing record](../testing/development-rounds.md).

## Upgrade and resume

An existing study keeps every record. On first use under 0.39.0 it reports:

- `constitution_revalidation_required` for every study: run `constitution` with
  the previous SHA-256 to adopt Version 3.
- `round_decision_missing` for a study at `evaluate` with a pinned, accepted
  bundle: decide with `round` before `deposit`.
- Budget payloads now name the `rounds` unit; an account recorded with seven
  units keeps its credit.
- A study before `evaluate` sees no other change until it reaches the round
  gate.

Read `status --summary` and `next --summary`, then the `obligations` page for
the code in hand. An upgrade does not resume paused work, reset a budget, or
turn a historical receipt into current readiness.
