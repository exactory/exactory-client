# Exactory 0.39.0

This release lets one managed study develop its paper across independently
approved research rounds. It keeps the proportionate literature preparation of
0.38.0 and the three-review manuscript measurement. It also keeps the two-review
publication gate, the five search purposes, and the five to ten external
innovation cases. The design is recorded in
[the development rounds design](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-12-development-rounds-design.md).

Release 0.39.1 corrected this note after an independent review of the release
documentation. [The 0.39.1 note](0.39.1.md) lists the corrections.

## Research workflow

- Five new operations:
  - `round` closes the current round on the exact publication bundle. It
    decides `continue` with one pursued candidate and a goal, or `stop`.
  - `round-review` records the independent judgment of one decision, with one
    judgment per check. The assessor is not a cycle author.
  - `round-admit` opens the round of an approved `continue`. It charges one
    round to the `development` account and stores the opening state of the
    round. While the round runs, its output is the only report of the admitted
    goal.
  - `round-assess` judges the admitted round on the exact bundle against its
    success criteria and stop conditions.
  - `manuscript-prediction` records the cohort prediction of one blind assessor
    on the exact bundle.
- The `round` gate checks, in order, the current bundle, the assessment of the
  closing round, its productivity and the continuity of its claims. Then it
  checks the decision, its approving review and, for `continue`, the admission.
  An exhausted `development` budget is reported beside each pending decision
  step.
- Two transition rules use the rounds. `evaluate -> literature` needs a round
  that `round-admit` opened and that has no assessment yet. `evaluate -> deposit`
  needs the publication gate and an approved `stop`.
- In a round of number 2 or more, the literature stage requires a selected
  search for each consequence purpose (`downstream`, `next_step`, `exemplars`,
  `changes`). Each search is recorded after the admission and judged like the
  five foundation purposes (`round_search_missing`). The stage also requires one
  `require-fulltext` with the purpose `exemplar` (`round_exemplar_missing`). The
  full reading of the exemplar is then owed under `fulltext_reading_missing`.
- A cycle assessment can give a useful development the `next_round`
  disposition when it exceeds the scope or resources of the round. This
  development leaves no readiness obligation. The next round decision must
  dispose of it in `carried` (`carried_development_missing`).
- An admission can widen the configured objective. The earlier objective stays
  as an ancestor (`research_objective`, `objective_lineage`). A `previous_id`
  that is not the configured objective's id, an unchanged statement or a reused
  objective id is `objective_locked`. Containment is the author's recorded
  assertion, judged by the round reviewer. The harness does not check it. A
  successor cycle can inherit a checkpoint of an earlier round.
- The manuscript keeps every claim id of the opening bundle of the round. A
  changed claim carries `revised: {previous, reason}`. A withdrawn claim carries
  `superseded: {reason}`. A missing claim, or a changed text without a marker,
  is `round_claims_dropped`. A round without a new claim is
  `round_claim_missing`. The round gate reports both.
- Each blind measurement reviewer returns the rubric core and, in a separate
  file, a cohort prediction with its reasons. The prediction has the shape of
  the market verdict and binds the cohort of the study. The harness refuses a
  prediction by a cycle author, and a second prediction by the same assessor on
  the same bundle.
- The measurement summary gives the medians and spreads of the reviews and the
  predictions. `status --summary` reports it, each round assessment stores it,
  and the round packet delivers it. No gate rule and no goal reads it.
- `budget` accepts the `development` purpose with the `rounds` unit. Every
  budget payload names the `rounds` unit. A `rounds` limit without room refuses
  the decision and the admission (`resource_budget_exhausted`). Any other unit of
  the `development` budget at its limit also refuses the decision.
- `status` carries `round`: number, active, assessed, decision, the count of
  round gate obligations, progress counts, measurement, limits, budget and
  usage. At the `evaluate` stage, the status obligations also carry the
  publication and round obligations, in priority order.
- `export --kind round` delivers the latest decision on the current bundle to
  the independent round assessor. The packet contains:
  - the manuscript packet, and the reviews and predictions of the bundle with
    their measurement
  - the decision, and the goal, assessment and decision of every earlier round
  - the `development` blocks of the cycle assessments of the closing round
  - the `context` and `innovation` synthesis sections, and the selected
    `downstream` and `next_step` searches
  - the research resource accounts with their budgets

  The packet contains no author, request id, token or revision label at any
  depth.
- `RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION.md` is Version 3, with a section on development across
  rounds.

## Distribution and validation

The shared `bin/`, `skills/` and `hooks/` serve Claude Code and Codex. The Codex
entrypoints are regenerated. New tests cover these areas:

- the round records and the gate (`test_research_rounds`)
- the `next_round` disposition and objective lineage (`test_research_development`)
- the `development` budget (`test_research_resources`)
- the claim markers and the shared assessor check (`test_research_publication`)
- the round packet and the neutral manuscript packet (`test_research_review_packets`)
- the status view (`test_research_report_views`)
- the consequence purposes (`test_research_literature`)
- the CLI registration (`test_research_cli`)

The per-task evidence, the validation-case mapping and the full-suite result are
in [the testing record](../testing/development-rounds.md).

## Upgrade and resume

An existing study keeps every record. Version 3 changes the constitution digest.
On first use under 0.39.0, a study reports these obligations:

- `constitution_revalidation_required` for every study. Run `constitution` with
  the previous SHA-256 to adopt Version 3. Adoption clears only this code. It
  does not revalidate earlier decisions.
- For each record that the study already has, the staleness that a policy change
  causes:
  - `synthesis_dependencies_stale`: record the four sections again under new ids.
  - `development_dependencies_stale`, and `branch_resolution_unverified` for a
    declared resolved branch: assess each cycle again.
  - `candidate_checkpoint_stale`: checkpoint the reassessed candidate.
  - `readiness_review_stale`: record a fresh readiness review.

  A study without these records reports only the constitution code.
- A study at `evaluate` also reports `readiness_required`, and `round` refuses a
  decision until readiness is current. The pinned bundle then reports
  `publication_readiness_stale`. Pin the manuscript again under a new id and
  obtain two accepting blind reviews. Then decide with `round` before `deposit`
  (`round_decision_missing`).
- Budget payloads now name the `rounds` unit. An account recorded with seven
  units keeps its credit.

Read `status --summary` and `next --summary`, then the `obligations` page for the
code in hand. An upgrade does not resume paused work, reset a budget, or turn a
historical receipt into current readiness.
