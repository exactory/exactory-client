# Exactory 0.41.0

This release adds two bounded preparation policies. `lineage-v1` prepares an
author study from the line of results the paper continues. `sampled-v1` prepares
a verification from a random sample of the population. Each one replaces a
complete reading of the cohort with a reading list of a known size.

## The two policies

`lineage-v1` is the research default. It reads the lineage and the classics in
full, and it runs a bounded loop over the five search purposes. It asks for no
cohort member reading. The loop closes with `loop-close` once every purpose is
covered or recorded as a gap.

`sampled-v1` is the verification default. It draws one stratified random sample
of the frozen population. The verifier reads each sampled abstract and records a
placement of the target against that member. The percentile and the band of the
verdict come from those placements.

`exhaustive-v1` and `screened-v1` stay selectable on both profiles. An existing
workspace keeps the policy its configuration records, and a configuration
written before this field existed stays `exhaustive-v1`.

## Limits

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Abstract readings in the loop | 100 per study |
| New abstract readings per development round | 40 |
| Hits kept per captured query | 10 |
| Innovation candidates read at abstract depth | 10 |
| External innovation cases read in full | exactly 5 |
| Sampled members per verification | at most 100 |
| Abstract readings from targeted searches | 20 per verification |
| Core papers beyond the target, read in full | 10 |
| Unplaced sampled members that make the band widen | more than 20 |

## New commands

`sample` draws and records the verification's sample of one frozen population.
The draw is stratified by month with equal allocation. The record keeps the
seed, the per-month counts and the drawn members. The enumeration must be
complete before the draw.

`loop-close` closes the bounded loop of `lineage-v1`. It names all five search
purposes, each one `covered` or `gap`, and it keeps the queries each purpose
tried.

`population-query` is read-only. It ranks the enumerated population by how many
of the given terms appear in the title or the saved abstract.

`batches --loop [--candidates FILE]` exports the loop's unread abstracts instead
of the unread cohort members. `--candidates` reads a `population-query` result or
a plain list of version ids.

## New fields and codes

`read-batch` accepts four extras. `loop` and `innovation_candidate` belong to
`lineage-v1`. `placement` and `search_hit` belong to `sampled-v1`. An extra of
the other policy fails with `invalid_batch`.

`require-fulltext` accepts four new purposes: `lineage`, `classic`, `core` and
`contradiction`. The first three name the claim, lineage entry or finding they
serve in `depends_on`.

`policy-report --policy` takes any of the four policies. For the two bounded
policies it reports the recorded workspace's counts against their own limits.

`status` now carries `limits`, the counts of the recorded policy against its
bounds. Before this release the field held a prose caveat about the readiness
gate's scope, and no consumer read it. The gate reports still carry that caveat.

Under `lineage-v1` the manuscript gate reads the pinned bibliography for every
`lineage` and `classic` entry. It reports `lineage_citation_missing` for an entry
that the bibliography matches by no arXiv family, no DOI and no title.

`bind-verdict` checks the verdict body's prediction against the sample under
`sampled-v1`, and refuses with `prediction_mismatch`. The refusal carries both
the sample prediction and the claimed one.

A second draw against an unchanged population is refused with `sample_exists`.

## Upgrade

The research constitution is Version 4. An existing workspace reports
`constitution_revalidation_required` at its next `status`. Run `constitution`
with the previous SHA-256 to adopt Version 4, as after 0.38.0.

`exactory-lab init` records `lineage-v1` when `--preparation-policy` is absent,
and `exactory-draft init` always records it. An `init` payload without
`preparation_policy` records the profile's default.

## Repairs

Preparing and running the two acceptance runs found three faults. All three are
corrected.

- The five search purposes are optional under `sampled-v1`, because its searches
  are targeted. A purpose with no recorded search owes nothing, and the
  foundation gate no longer reports `search_purpose_missing` for it.
- A main document served as `application/octet-stream` is accepted when its
  bytes are a PDF. Zenodo serves every file URL with that media type.
- The arXiv Atom API caps a query at 10,000 results. A partition whose cursor
  reaches that ceiling now splits into two date halves that restart at offset
  zero.

## What did not change

The store schema remains version 1, so an upgrade from 0.40.0 needs no
migration. `exhaustive-v1` and `screened-v1` behave as before. The writing and
development round flows are unchanged, except for the citation gate on lineage
and classic entries and the cap of 40 loop readings per round.

## Acceptance

Two runs on existing workspaces measured the policies. The author run used
`lineage-v1` on the haar-purity objective at commit `455ec1a`. The verification
run used `sampled-v1` on 10.5281/zenodo.22773593 at commit `6f49d6f`.

Author run:

| Measurement | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Loop abstract readings | 52 of 100 |
| Innovation candidates and external cases | 10 candidates, 5 cases in full |
| Lineage and classic entries read in full | 4 of 4 |
| Tensor papers surfaced by `population-query` | 3 of 3 |
| Preparation gate | passed, no obligations, no harness repair |
| Time from `init` to the gate | 41 minutes 23 seconds |

Verification run:

| Measurement | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Sample | 100 of 8,064 members, 17/17/17/17/16/16 by month |
| Registered abstract readings | 100, all of them sampled members |
| Core papers read in full | 4 of 10 |
| Prediction | percentile 49, band 44 to 54, 98 of 100 placed |
| Preparation gate | passed, zero obligations |
| Time from enumeration complete to the gate | 21 minutes 34 seconds |

suite results and the full measurements of both runs.
