# Exactory 0.89.0

This release carries the first units of Stage 1 of the capacity, continuity and openness designs. It follows 0.88.1.
The records, the designs, the rulings and the reviews are in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261113110022-every-open-record-and-review-capacity` of the exactory repository, and each
change below names the record that it answers.

## Why

Stage 1 gives every size failure of a review a next step, and it stops a review that the construction does not
cover before a paid call. It changes no protocol and no prompt of a running chain. This release ships the units of
Stage 1 that are merged and tested. The other units of the stage follow in 0.89.1 and later patch releases, and the
records of the stage close with the release that completes their units.

## What changed

Citations of a session (record `20261111235418-bar-final-refused-for-text-delivered-in-reduced-history`):

- The audit of a final credits each citation to the verified turn of the session that delivered the cited text, and
  no longer reads the request of the final attempt alone. A text that no turn delivered is still refused with
  `review_evidence_undelivered`.
- A continuation whose fitted input lost the body of an earlier delivered text names that text, the turn that
  delivered it, the missing characters and the verified excerpts that it keeps, under the delivery notice
  `exactory-review-delivery-notice-v1`. Continuations that keep every body, and every stored input, keep their bytes.

- A final whose citation lacks a reading of a full text that the chain delivered keeps `reading_missing`. Its plan
  now names `read` for each version, and the selected output stays the selected output. A session delivery is not a
  reading.
- A source link whose artifact is neither the original nor the extracted text of a full-text capture of its version
  (for example an abstract that a session delivered) is refused as an output with `source_link_not_fulltext`, before
  any reading is checked, and the refusal record is written and rechecked when it is read. Until the refusal units of
  0.90.0 ship, the same request runs once more, and a second refusal leads to the step of the role for an exhausted
  slot.

Outcomes of a reviewer call and the next step of every stop (records `20261112043216`, `20261112135015`,
`20261112072628`, `20261113074500`):

- An interrupted call has the outcome `interrupted`, and a reading turn that the harness reconciled after an
  interruption stays runnable. An explicitly stopped call spends no failure allowance.
- Every outcome has a row with its attempt limit and what follows when the limit is spent. One counter counts the
  failures of a position across its replacements, its task levels and its sequences. A sequence that ends waits 30
  minutes, or until the provider's reset time, and a position has three sequences; an owner grant adds one.
- A turn over the output limit with no smaller task gets one identical retry, then the decision stop
  `review_output_over_cap`, whose steps name the route choices of the role. A reading turn keeps its three attempts
  after a provider refusal and then waits for a new sequence.
- One registry in `stop_classes.py` holds the class, the owner and the next step of every stop code. The class of
  every existing code is unchanged. A harness defect needs its guard test.
- A stop names its step: the command, the payload that the harness knows, the choices that stay open and the
  prerequisites, for each role.
- An exhausted bar head frees its slot for a new eligible blind reviewer. An exhausted focused adjudication admits
  another eligible reviewer. Earlier reviewers stay excluded.

The capacity of a route and the output profile (records `20261112043216-bar-turn-over-output-cap-at-effort-max` and
`20261112235500-openness-chain-final-turn-over-route-limit-after-57-turns`):

- `research_harness/review_route_capacity.py` measures a request against the character and token limits of its route,
  with the source of each limit, and conservative defaults for a model without a row. A calibrated Claude request
  fits up to 1,141,040 text characters (1.36 characters for each token).
- The output profile of a route counts every stored attempt by its kind and task level, those at or over the output
  limit included, with the CLI version and the dates of the attempts. Route and probe receipts, `review-route-assess`
  and `status` report the effective output limit and the measured state of each kind. Reviewer efforts and stored
  request bindings do not change.
- A capacity warning never changes a stop: a stored packet that does not decode keeps the stop
  `review_format_unsupported` and its exit status.

The verification and the capacity signals (records `20261112043216-bar-turn-over-output-cap-at-effort-max` and
`20261113110022-every-open-record-and-review-capacity`):

- `ci/route_capacity_profile.py` judges the predeclared verification of the bar chains at `max`: every started
  chain is counted, at least 20 calls that reached a generation, at most one attempt at the output limit for each 20
  calls and one in a position, and no size stop, owner step or chain without a plan.
- A response over the limit of the HTTPS transport is `response_over_limit`: one identical retry, then the decision
  stop `review_response_too_large` with the serving routes and the steps of the role. The message "Prompt is too
  long", a Codex context-window error and a compaction inside a Claude call are `input_over_limit`. The output limit
  message of a request sent without streaming is `execution_incomplete`. None of these attempts enters the output
  profile.

Overrides and their conditions (record `20261113074500-post-on-user-order-instruction-condition-export-and-chain-stop`):

- `gate-override` accepts an instruction in the parts form: the owner's own words, a selection that the owner made
  and the agent's description, each kept as given and labelled by its author. A stored instruction in the text form
  keeps its digest and its views.
- An override holds the condition of its instruction and the conditions that it lifts. Every public write and
  every reader of an override assess the conditions of the applicable instructions, in their recorded order. A write
  is refused with `gate_override_condition_unmet` while a condition stands without met evidence, and
  `gate-override` reports the write authorities of the instruction that it records.
- An override export holds the authority that it was written under. A new post keeps the exact authority of its
  export, or a recording-time observation when no export names it, and `after_post` reports its unmet conditions.
- An override export uses the schema `exactory-problem-export-v3`. It cites the works of the read search records,
  with a chosen version and order (`export --spec`), counts its citations, lists the relevant works that it leaves
  uncited, and moves the citations above the shared limit of 50 to `citations-omitted.json`.

The minimum of an openness export (record `20261112143000-openness-chain-ends-unresolved-on-search-integrity-gaps`):

- The minimum of version 2 is built and validated with all its lists. Exports still write version 1; version 2 is
  exported with protocol v6 in 0.90.0.

## Compatibility

No file of a reviewer route's runtime identity changes, so no route becomes stale. Stored records, packets, prompts,
exports and posts keep their meaning and their digests. The controller digest of the math-solver writer changes, as
it does with every release that changes a module of `research_harness/`.

New codes: `gate_override_condition_unmet` and the wait `review_sequence_wait`. The transport's existing
`review_response_too_large` becomes the decision stop of the outcome `response_over_limit`. New stop class:
`requested` (for the stop command of a later 0.89 release).

Incident ledger: nineteen entries set to 0.89.0.
