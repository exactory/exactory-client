# Exactory 0.70.0

This release performs a problem result review or a focused adjudication whose request does not fit one input of a
local text route as a staged review: tasks of one frozen epoch, each a fresh call with an empty history, that read
the originals, write grounded cards, assess the cards together, read selected originals again, integrate the
findings and write the review. An assessment that cannot be completed is recorded as a distinct outcome that
occupies its slot. A packet that fits one input and delivers every body is built as before. The work is recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261031190348-part-delivery-quality-equivalence` of the exactory repository.

## Why

0.69.0 to 0.69.4 deliver an oversized result packet in part turns of one session. A part turn returns one free-text
note per unit, checked for shape only, and the final turn holds the earlier units only through those notes, the
verified excerpts and the earlier packets that the replayed history still carries. On 2026-10-31 the first part
series of the unknotting record delivered about 2.1 million characters of units in three part turns per slot, and
both final reviews were `insufficient` on calibration; the reviewer wrote that its review rested on the frame of the
final turn, its notes and its excerpts. The replayed history grew about 30,000 characters per turn, and the evening's
requests were close to the Claude limit of 1,300,000 characters: 1,299,415 and 1,294,633 for part turn 4 of the two
unknotting slots, 1,289,020 for the slot A final of the KMP record. No measurement compared a review in parts with a
review of the same evidence in one input.

The owner asked for a split that keeps the quality of a single input, with no regression, at scale and in every case:
chunked reading of the originals, critical statements kept verbatim, structured summaries grounded in verified
excerpts, a stage that assesses combinations of evidence, further calls that read the originals of the flagged
combinations, and a final turn that can return to any original. GPT 6 Astra at effort xhigh designed the mechanism in
four drafts and two independent reviews; the supervisor's decisions are section 18 of the design. The
[decision guide](../research-decisions.md#staged-problem-reviews) states the contract, and the
[CLI reference](../research-cli.md#staged-problem-reviews) the commands, the records and the codes.

## What changed

- Entry. A problem `result` or `adjudicator` assignment on `claude_cli_v1`, `codex_cli_v1` or `codex_cli_framed_v1`
  stays ordinary when the request that the transport builds fits the route limit and delivers every item of the
  packet and every artifact body of its `evidence` list; the packet, the prompt and the request are then built as
  before, byte for byte. Otherwise, also below the limit when the fitted request withholds a body, the assignment
  becomes the controller of a staged review (`staged_review: {protocol: "exactory-review-staged-v2", kind:
  "controller", epoch_id, task_ids}`). An initial assignment, a refresh, a continuation, a context repair and a
  focused adjudication enter this way; a `source_delivery` continuation and a session that is already a part series
  do not.
- The epoch. `review-assignment` freezes `review_epoch/<id>` in the same write: the subject, the evidence currency,
  the role, the slot, the reviewer, the route and the author; a manifest of every source as text with its length and
  SHA-256, every occurrence of the evidence lists with the bodies it owns, every claim of the frame with its context,
  the assurance dependencies and the inherited material objections; the material objections with their evidence
  resolved; the permitted value of every decision; and the budgets (10,000 calls, one retry per task, two rereads of
  an unchanged question, 10,000 subdivisions, two successor epochs, 86,400 seconds). It installs the slot head
  `review_slot_head/<slot id>` as `pending`, so an earlier review of the slot leaves the current pair before any call.
- The tasks. Seven task types, `frame`, `read`, `combine`, `original_check`, `integrate`, `answer` and `final`, each
  with an explicit scope (`joint_original`, `segmented_original`, `original_segment`, `joint_card`, `segmented_card`,
  `card_segment` or `unresolved`), frozen inputs, claims, dependencies and measurement. A task's prompt is the role's
  current template text followed by the staged texts of its type under the id
  `exactory-review-staged-v2/<role>/<type>`; its packet carries the originals as exact text spans, the accepted
  records of its dependencies and nothing replayed. The harness plans the frame tasks when it records the controller,
  in groups of at most 16,000 characters of frame fields. Every later task is supplied as a sealed envelope in
  `context.task` of its child `review-assignment`, built with `make_task` or `plan_tasks` of
  `research_harness.review_staged`, which partitions the originals by claims and by source spans, plans the
  combination calls (all compact cards in one call when they fit together, otherwise blocks that meet pairwise), the
  original checks and, for a paged final, the answer pages and the terminal.
- The operator's commands are unchanged. A child runs one task: `review-assignment` with
  `context: {controller_assignment_id, task_id}` and the controller's role, reviewer, author, route and dossier;
  `review-run {assignment_id, epoch_id, task_id}` runs it, with `action: "accept"` to resume an acceptance and
  `action: "reconcile"` to reconcile a worker. `next` lists the exact payload of every step and never a repeat of an
  exhausted task. `value-review` records the controller's effective result.
- Capacity. One reviewer call at a time per store by default, counted together with the pending invocations of
  ordinary reviews on the covered adapters. `review-assignment` with `scheduler: {store_limit, route_limits}` records
  a higher capacity (`review_capacity_policy/store`). Admission is one store transaction; `review_capacity_wait`
  names the reservations to reconcile.
- Capture and acceptance. `review-run` reserves the task, launches an owned worker, waits for its capture and accepts
  it. The worker commits the observation through the store (`review_observation/<reservation id>`); that commit is
  the capture, and no file outside the store is an observation. Acceptance (`review_task_acceptance/<reservation
  id>`) authenticates the capture against the frozen request, binds every quotation to its source span, checks the
  output against the task's shape and records the task's actual input. A run interrupted after the capture resumes
  the acceptance without a second call; a worker that dies before the capture leaves an unknown outcome, one retry,
  then `review_task_exhausted`. A reservation is released only after confirmed termination of the worker and its
  process group. Capture and acceptance commit through task-scoped writes (`Store.mutate_scoped`,
  `prepared_task_mutation`), so a commit of another task does not invalidate them.
- Raw observation and effective result. `assignment_state` exposes `raw_output` and `raw_output_digest`, the exact
  object a call returned, and `effective`, the scientific result assembled from the accepted tasks, of kind
  `ordinary`, `paged`, `incomplete` or `pending`; `read_raw_observation` returns the raw observation of an attempt.
  Authentication, refusal classification and output correction read the raw observation; recording, independence,
  coverage, objections, context repair and export read the effective result. `value-review` writes
  `effective_binding` on the review record. A paged final is answer pages and a terminal bound under a composition
  root (`review_composition.py`): one owner per field, lists by frozen ordinals, string segments joined in order, no
  last-write-wins, and a terminal that authorizes the exact root and chooses only the scalar values the record
  permits.
- Incomplete assessments. A final task that cannot complete a required assessment returns `assessment_incomplete
  {unresolved_scope, recovery_work}`; the harness derives the outcome record `assessment-incomplete:<digest>` with
  its origin, its roots, its resource ledger and its unresolved entries. The slot stays occupied, the earlier adverse
  findings stay in force, and the gate lists `assessment_incomplete` and `review_assessment_incomplete` with the
  recovery work instead of asking for another reviewer.
- The post gate. `assess_problem_reviews` reads the slot outcomes: pending and incomplete slots are obligations in
  their own slots, only completed slots form the pair, and the result coverage check reads the accepted task graph.
  Every staged slot also lists `review_quality_measurement_pending`, so the gate is not ready for a staged review in
  this release. The gate report carries `slot_outcomes`, `status --summary` carries `review_slots`, and the
  obligations of a staged review are ordered before the scientific shortfalls.
- Recorded sessions. `review-assignment` with `context: {staged_upgrade: {assignment_id}}` opens a staged review in
  the slot of a recorded result or adjudicator assignment, binding the predecessor and its last attempt.
- Views and exports. `intermediate/reviews/staged/<kind>/` holds the new record kinds, `final/reviews/incomplete/`
  the incomplete outcomes, and a controller's turn writes its effective output beside its raw turns. The problem
  export adds `reviews.json` when a review of the decision is staged. The renderer waits at most one second for its
  lock and otherwise leaves a dirty marker and the advisory reason `render_deferred`.
- Context repair. A repair of a staged slot waits for pending workers (`review_context_repair_pending`), carries the
  predecessor's raw observations, intermediate findings and effective binding, and no longer needs a recorded final
  for a part turn of a 0.69.x series.

## What stays as it was

- A packet whose fitted request fits and delivers every item and every body is built, measured and recorded exactly
  as before, with the same prompt and request bytes.
- No module that a route pins by its bytes changed: `review_transport.py`, `review_claude_transport.py`,
  `review_codex_transport.py`, `review_codex_framed_transport.py`, `review_codex_visual_transport.py`,
  `review_verbatim_text.py`, `review_exact_text.py`, `review_exact_text_v2.py`, `review_exact_text_cost_v1.py`,
  `review_visual_inputs.py` and `review_pdf_pages.py` are as in 0.69.4. Every recorded route stays valid and needs no
  new probe, and the reduction orders do not change.
- The legacy part prompts, the part response shape, the part planner and the series coverage check are unchanged.
  `Store.mutate`, `prepared_mutation` and the full-history validation of the store are unchanged.
- The Responses route, the visual route, bar and slate reviews, study, manuscript and verification reviews and the
  native math reviews are not staged.

## Existing records

- A recorded 0.69.0 to 0.69.4 part or final turn authenticates as recorded, and a session that is a series continues
  in parts under its recorded contract. A continuation whose evidence digest changed and a refresh of a series start a
  new series as before. Only an explicit `staged_upgrade` moves such a slot to a staged review.
- Installation alone changes neither active record. The unknotting and KMP records run their result reviews on 0.69.2
  to 0.69.4; the owner decides whether they are reviewed again in the staged mode.
- The store is append-only and needs no migration. The new record kinds (`review_epoch`, `review_task`,
  `review_execution_plan`, `review_task_execution`, `review_reservation`, `review_pool_membership`,
  `review_observation`, `review_task_acceptance`, `review_capacity_policy`, `review_slot_head`,
  `review_task_dispatch`, `review_composition_plan` and `assessment_incomplete`) appear only in a record that opens
  a staged review.

## Codes

- New obligations and states: `review_task_pending`, `review_capacity_wait`,
  `review_worker_reconciliation_required`, `review_observation_acceptance_pending`,
  `review_task_dependency_changed`, `review_epoch_stale`, `review_composition_pending`,
  `review_composition_conflict`, `review_assurance_dependency_pending`, `assessment_incomplete`,
  `review_assessment_incomplete` and `review_quality_measurement_pending`.
- New refusals: `review_task_exhausted`, `review_task_failed`, `review_instrumented_capture_required`,
  `review_context_repair_pending` and `invalid_review_staged`; `review_resampling_forbidden` also refuses a second
  child of one task and a rerun under another attempt id of a captured reservation.
- Completion issues inside a report: `review_terminal_pending`, `review_finding_answer_pending`,
  `review_original_check_pending`.
- Unchanged in meaning: `review_input_too_large`, `problem_evidence_part_pending`, `problem_evidence_unassessed`,
  `review_evidence_undelivered`, `review_invocation_unverified`, `review_context_contaminated`.

## Known limits

- The quality-equivalence measurement has not run. The release claims no equal quality between a staged review and a
  review of the same evidence in one input. The staged mode decides no posting: every staged slot lists
  `review_quality_measurement_pending`, and no author flag lifts it, until an authenticated passing study (option A,
  then option C of the design: 150 independent held-out fixtures, zero composite regressions, one-sided 95% upper
  bound 1.98%) binds the protocol and the reviewer configuration. The issuer of that qualification record is not in
  this release. The owner's confirmation of the measurement was pending when this note was written.
- Full-history storage validation is unchanged: every authoritative read still validates the whole history, measured
  at 2.041 seconds for a copy of the unknotting record and 1.707 for a copy of the KMP record. No history-independent
  command latency is claimed.
- The harness plans only the frame tasks itself. Every later task is a sealed envelope that the operator or a record
  tool builds with `make_task` or `plan_tasks` and passes in `context.task`; `next` names that step as
  `review_task_pending` with the prerequisite `remaining_required_tasks`. The planning profiles are not calibrated
  (`unqualified-planning-v1`), and the frame tasks are cut at 16,000 characters rather than at the route's measured
  room.
- A task whose required original is a binary body (a PDF, an image) cannot be planned (`review_task_pending`); its
  epoch cannot complete in this release.
- An output that fails its task check is not accepted, and the task has no second call: its capture stays the raw
  observation, and the work goes to a new task envelope with another partition.
- The execution-origin `assessment_incomplete` record (an exhausted task, an unknown outcome) is defined and
  validated but no command writes it; `next` reports such a task from its task state. The slot head record stays
  `pending` until a successor epoch replaces it; the complete and incomplete states of a slot are derived from the
  effective result, not read from the head.
- The entry and the prompts of a focused adjudication are in place, and `record_adjudication` reads the controller's
  effective result, but the adjudication functions of `review_protocol.py` were not changed and the recording of a
  staged adjudication has no test.
- The turn counts and wall times of the design (18 to 56 calls per slot, 7.63 serial hours at the Opus max mean) are
  projections under stated assumptions, not measurements of this release.
- The staged logic that lane A2 added to `review_protocol.py` (about 1,000 lines) is to move into its own module, and
  the deferred handoffs of the lanes (reconciliation ids and record roots in packets, replanning inputs, the view
  registry, a body shared by original and current evidence) are open work of lane G1 at the time of this note.
