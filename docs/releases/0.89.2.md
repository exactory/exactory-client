# Exactory 0.89.2

This release completes Stage 1 of the capacity, continuity and openness designs, and it fixes the four tests that
the CI of 0.89.1 failed. It follows 0.89.1. The records, the designs, the rulings and the reviews are in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261113110022-every-open-record-and-review-capacity` of the exactory repository.

## What changed

The stop of a review chain (record `20261113074500-post-on-user-order-instruction-condition-export-and-chain-stop`):

- `review-stop` stops the reviewer calls of one chain or of the whole store, and releases that stop later. The mode
  `now` ends the running calls; the mode `after_turn` lets them finish. In both modes no new call of the scope
  starts until the stop is released.
- A process that a call owns gets 10 seconds after SIGTERM before SIGKILL, and then up to 120 seconds for its
  settlement. When a call is not settled in that time, the result names a closing step, and a fresh request
  continues the stop. The same request id replays its committed response.
- A `review-series` driver reads the stops of its scope after each plan, before each action and every 5 seconds
  while it sleeps. A run that meets a stop ends with `review_chain_stopped` and exit status 3, and SIGTERM or SIGINT
  to the command stops its chain in the same way.
- A post closes the calls of its stopped chains. `status` lists each call under `after_post` with its state and its
  administrative step, and a settled call has the code `review_call_closed`.

The queue of reviewer calls:

- `review-run`, a probe, a correction and a staged dispatch wait for the reviewer capacity of their route by
  default. Eligible waits start in the order of their requests. A wait whose process is gone is abandoned, a wait
  has a bounded timeout, and a stop withdraws the waits of its scope. `status` lists the live waits under
  `review.queue`.
- A wait plan and `review.queue` name the kinds of limit that hold a call and propose a scheduler for the verified
  routes that can take part.

The capacity of a review that the construction does not cover (closure requirements C4 and C10):

- The measure of a request bounds its binary inputs by the documented token rules of the provider. An input without
  a bound, for example an image in an encoding other than PNG, makes the request not fit with the reason
  `binary_input_without_bound`.
- `review-assignment` measures the request of a new assignment against the capacity of its route before it records
  the assignment. A request that does not fit, or a packet with an evidence body that has no text form, is refused
  with `review_route_capacity_below_floor`, with no assignment and no call. The code is a decision stop with its
  answers. `review-route-assess` and `status` list, for each role, the reviews that no route can serve.
- `review-probe` takes an optional `capacity: {input_tokens}`. A passed capacity probe is evidence of the input limit
  of its route under its key; a failed one leaves the route unverified until a later probe passes.

A single review that its route cannot answer:

- An ordinary request above the `single` start of its route class enters a series with `entry: "task"`.
- After an output-limit attempt of a stored single review, the first read of a series reuses its frozen packet with
  the same route, reviewer, author and slot. A native review is authenticated through the attestation of the
  original. `review-assignment --check` reports the delivery form and its reason.

The forecast of a session, and the size reports:

- A `bar`, `slate` or `result` assignment whose packet holds a source inventory has a forecast of the source
  delivery that its session still owes, from the capacity and the output profile of its route. Plans, the response
  of `review-assignment`, `review-series` lines and `status` carry `capacity_forecast`. When
  the pending delivery is estimated at more than 32 turns, or the projected request exceeds the input limit of the
  route, the plan and `review-run` stop with `review_pending_delivery_over_plan` and name the steps: a compact
  capture for each source with pending parts, or a serving route for the preserved slot.
- `forming-import --check` and a new `problem-value` report the size of the material they measure under
  `size_report`, with a measure against the input limit of each registered route. No stored receipt holds the
  report.

The guards of Stage 1:

- `ci/capacity_guard.py` checks the closure matrix, the source and transport inventories and the packet schema at
  every run, and `ci/continuity_guard.py` executes every outcome kind of the continuity design.

The CI of 0.89.1:

- The historical v1 result fixture is built under the session rules v2 and refreshed under v3.
- The capacity classification table is a tuple, so the scan of record writers no longer reads it as writes.
- The stub routes of the measurement tools have an endpoint and a timeout.

The texts that an agent reads for these units were written for this release and reviewed at effort max.

## Compatibility

No file of a reviewer route's runtime identity changes, so no route becomes stale. Stored records, packets, prompts
and requests keep their meaning and their digests. A chain that a store stop holds stays stopped until
`review-stop` releases it.
