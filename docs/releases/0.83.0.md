# Exactory 0.83.0

This release lets math-conjecturer record how it forms a statement. An exploration can start from a phenomenon
that appears in several places, and the Ideate tool gains two commands for a problem record. The release follows
0.82.0. It is stage A of the work recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261109153246-abc-type-conjecture-creation` of the exactory repository.

## Why

The owner asked whether math-conjecturer creates open problems like the abc conjecture. The posting bar does not
exclude such a problem, but no step of the harness was built to form one. In the nine problem records that hold
a post, 47 of 60 candidates were taken from the literature (counted from the stores on 2026-11-09), and the work
that formed a statement left no tool record. In 0.82.0 no Ideate command runs in a problem record, a value
paragraph can cite a solver result only from the attempt on the current statement, and a statement that the
record formed itself is recorded as found in a source.

The principle that the owner accepted on 2026-11-09 words the aim by the activity: research on a phenomenon that
appears in several places, with the question of what one exact statement says about all of them. The work ships
in three stages. This release records the forming work. Stage B adds the accounts of a statement to the value
evidence and the reviewer texts that read them. Stage C adds the report of an exploration and further areas. A
statement that is formed with this release is posted under the bar and the reviewer texts of 0.82.0.

## What changed

- Two new commands of the Ideate tool run in a problem record. `exactory-research forming` records one forming
  call: the question with its places, the move as an open value, the inputs, the amounts that the performer
  reports, every form that the call produced, the probes that it used, the stopping reason and the performer. A
  form is a tentative statement with its purpose, its grounds, the earlier form that it changes, its nearest
  alternatives, the relations expected of it and its planned checks, each with the outcome that would contradict
  the form. `exactory-research form-outcome` records how a form, an expected relation or a planned check ended.
  For a form it keeps the standing as a claim and the disposition in the exploration apart. Each evidence item
  names its standing: a full reading, an admitted computation and an accepted result are admissible, and a probe
  is exploratory. A form can be recorded as refuted on a probe that exhibits the counterexample. These records
  grant no openness, significance, proof credit or test credit, and the post gate reads none of them.
- A value paragraph and the evidence of a form outcome can cite an accepted proof, reduction, obstruction or
  counterexample of any math-solver objective that was delivered into the problem store, as `{kind: "result",
  native_attempt_digest, acceptance_id, purpose, extension_reason}`. The reference names the latest delivered
  snapshot of its objective and the purpose of the objective, and it carries the reason when a delivered total
  exceeds a normal limit. The result reviewers receive the claim of the objective beside the accepted claim. The
  new codes are `problem_result_unbound`, `problem_result_superseded`, `problem_result_missing` and
  `problem_result_extension_unexplained`. In 0.82.0 a result outside the attempt on the current statement was
  refused, and a counterexample could not be cited.
- `problem-candidate` accepts a seventh source kind, `phenomenon`, for a statement that the record formed from a
  phenomenon that appears in several places. Such a candidate cites at least one forming call
  (`problem_candidate_forming_missing`). A candidate of any kind can cite forming calls and form outcomes as
  records.
- `problem-init` takes an optional `start` entry, `{kind, description}`, which records the kind of start as an
  open value: a field question, a phenomenon that appears in several places, or a thought experiment. Full
  `status` shows it under `problem.start`.
- A slate that selects a candidate formed in the record cites at least one current slate review
  (`problem_slate_review_required`). A candidate formed in the record is one of the kind `phenomenon`, or one
  whose source evidence cites a forming call or a form outcome. This is the owner's decision of 2026-11-09. It
  narrows the decision of 2026-10-18, under which no slate review is compulsory, for these candidates only.
- `status` and `status --summary` report `forming: {calls, forms, by_standing, by_disposition, without_outcome,
  outcomes}` once a record holds a forming call. The output folders show the calls in
  `intermediate/problem/forming/` and the outcomes in `intermediate/problem/form-outcomes/`.
- The math-conjecturer skill has a new subsection of "Exploration", "An exploration from a phenomenon that
  appears in several places". It gives the research steps: the places with their own grounds, the moves, each
  form stated before its checks, the checks with what each can show, the attacks on a form and on its stronger
  forms, the corrections, and the candidates derived from every outcome. The thought-experiment subsection
  records its interpretations and expectations as forms with outcomes, under its present rules. "The attempt"
  gains "Objectives beside the attempt", for a bounded objective on a form, on a stronger form or on a relation.
  "Candidates and the slate" states the required slate review and, for an exploration opened on a phenomenon,
  the priority by the expected gain.
- The skill states three necessary conditions for a statement posed in place of a refuted one. The agent applies
  them before it forms or selects such a statement. The absolute principle of problem selection and rubric item
  A7 are unchanged, and the purpose of the question still decides.
- Each refusal of the new code names the repair in its message. `docs/research-tools.md` gains the section
  "Forming calls and outcomes". `docs/research-cli.md` gains "Forming records", with both payloads, the refusal
  table and the way to compute the digest of a record reference. The skill description names the new start, and
  the Codex entry is generated again.

## What stays the same

- `intent` stays refused in a problem record, and the forming commands are refused outside one
  (`orchestration_conflict`).
- No reviewer text changes. No rubric item, level or threshold changes, and the post gate has no new condition.
- A slate that selects no candidate formed in the record behaves as in 0.82.0: a slate review stays optional
  unless a carry imported an objection.
- The existing result reference, `{kind: "result", attempt_id, acceptance_id}`, keeps its behaviour, and the
  attempt on the exact statement keeps its rules.

## What stored records see

- A record that holds no forming record keeps its `status` report, its output folders and its digests. A marker
  that 0.82.0 wrote validates, digests and renders the same bytes. A stored value record projects the same
  bytes, and a stored slate validates as before. Fixture tests pin each of these.
- A posted statement gets no new obligation, and no recorded review becomes stale.
- A record in progress gets no new obligation while it records no forming call and no candidate of the kind
  `phenomenon`. A thought-experiment record that cites a forming call or a form outcome in a candidate needs a
  slate review before its slate selects that candidate.
- Records written by earlier releases do not change, and no migration is necessary.

## Known limits

- The forming commands run in a problem record. Studies, verifications and math-solver roots have no forming
  command in this release.
- The reviewer packets are unchanged. A slate reviewer reads the source kind, the reference and the observation
  of a candidate, as before. The reviewer texts that read the history of the forms follow in stage B.
- The amounts of a forming call and the usage of a model lane are the performer's report. The harness records
  them and does not verify them.
- A forming record stays in the record that made it. No command carries it to another record, and the skill
  tells the agent to state the forms again there. A command for this is planned for stage C.
- The three conditions for a statement posed in place of a refuted one are a rule of the skill. The reviewer
  texts read A7 as 0.60.0 shipped it, and the paragraph for the reviewers follows in stage B.
- No record of this release makes a computation a test of a statement. A probe keeps its exploratory standing,
  and the declaration that makes a run a test follows in stage B.
