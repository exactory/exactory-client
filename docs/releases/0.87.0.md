# Exactory 0.87.0

This release keeps a compact full text when the layout mode of PDF extraction pads the text with whitespace, and it
lets a record choose which capture of an original a review receives. It follows 0.86.0. The failure, the
measurements and the decision are recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261111223126-layout-extraction-inflates-fulltext-delivery` of the exactory repository
(tasks T1 and T2).

## Why

`fulltext` extracts a PDF in layout mode by default. In a problem record of 2026-11-11, three of four papers had
figures with many short text labels. Layout mode padded each label to its position on the page, and the stored
texts had 2.5 to 19 million characters, of which 97 to 99 percent was whitespace. The same PDFs without layout mode
gave 67,458 to 259,648 characters. A review chain delivers a requested full text in parts of at most 120,000
characters, one part per turn, and a final assessment needs every requested part. The first bar of that record
delivered part 9 of 22 of one paper at turn 13. An estimate for the four texts was about 200 further turns.

A second capture of the same PDF without layout mode did not help. The main capture of an original was always its
first capture, and no command changed it.

## What changed

- A default PDF extraction measures the layout text. When its whitespace fraction is more than 0.90, or its
  expansion (extracted bytes divided by PDF bytes) is more than 2.0, the harness extracts the PDF again without
  layout mode and keeps the complete plain text. Neither mode truncates text.
- The thresholds come from measurements with `pdftotext` 26.02.0. The first five PDFs of each of six live problem
  records (30 ordinary articles) had a whitespace fraction of at most 0.644 and an expansion of at most 0.487. The
  three padded incident PDFs had at least 0.970 and 2.61. The fourth incident PDF, an ordinary control, keeps its
  layout text.
- `extraction.layout_selection` of a new capture records the protocol `exactory-pdf-layout-selection-v1`, the kept
  mode, the thresholds and the measurements of the layout text, and of the plain text when the harness extracted
  it. When the plain extraction fails, the capture stays pending with that status, and the padded text is not kept
  as an available capture.
- An explicit `extraction_options.layout`, an injected extractor and OCR keep their behavior.
- The new command `select-main-capture` takes `{version_id, source_id, reason}` and records which available main
  capture of an original the next deliveries use. A component, a declared supplement and a capture that is not
  available are refused with `invalid_main_capture`. The command changes no capture, no artifact and no reading. A
  new packet binds the choices under `exactory-main-capture-selection-v1`.
- Incident ledger entry `layout-extraction-inflates-fulltext-delivery`.

## What stays the same

- A capture that a release before 0.87.0 stored keeps its text, its digest and its projection.
- A packet without a binding of choices keeps the rule of the first capture, so its parts, excerpts and complete
  source manifests authenticate as before. Earlier deliveries, readings and verified excerpts keep their meaning.
- No file that a reviewer route pins changes, so no route becomes stale.

## What stored records see

- Nothing changes in a stored record. No migration is necessary. A record that holds a padded layout capture can
  acquire the PDF again, which gives a compact capture, and then choose it with `select-main-capture`.

## Known limits

- A reviewer still cannot settle its own pending full-text request, and the cost of pending deliveries is not
  reported before it is paid (tasks T3 and T4 of the record). These parts belong to the capacity and continuity
  designs of the record `20261113110022-every-open-record-and-review-capacity`.
- The CLI reference does not yet describe `select-main-capture`, `extraction.layout_selection` or the automatic
  plain extraction; `docs/research-cli-examples.json` has the example payload. The writer's pass on these texts
  comes in a later release.
- The thresholds rest on a sample of 30 ordinary PDFs from six records, not on a population.
