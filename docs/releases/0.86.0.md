# Exactory 0.86.0

This release gives two refusals of a review output the facts that an agent needs to correct the output, and it
rewrites the texts that 0.85.1 to 0.85.3 added. It follows 0.85.3. The failures and the decisions are recorded in
two records of `misc/harness-improvement` of the exactory repository:
`20261112072628-bar-final-refused-for-part-citation-with-whole-text-offsets` (tasks T1 and T3) and
`20261109153246-abc-type-conjecture-creation` (task T8).

## Why

A bar review of a problem record cited the second delivered part of a split full text. The span used the offsets of
that part in the whole text (118,926 to 157,555) on the part file, which holds about 38,600 characters. `value-review`
refused the final output twice with `invalid_locator` and the message "The span must lie within the saved text". The
message did not say that the offsets counted from the start of the whole text, so the reviewer repeated the same
span, and the slot ended with `review_slot_exhausted`.

In the same record, a context repair bound only the newer of two route retirements. The refusal
`review_context_repair_mismatch` said that every event of the predecessor lineage must be bound, but it did not name
the expected events or the missing one.

The texts that 0.85.1 to 0.85.3 added were written during the releases. For task T8 the project's writer rewrote
them, and an Astra 6 review at effort max read the result.

## What changed

- A span on a delivered part that uses whole-text offsets is still refused with `invalid_locator`, and the harness
  does not reinterpret it. When the span lies inside the whole-text range of a part that a packet of the review
  delivered, for the same source, version and part file, the message names that range and gives the part-relative
  start and end of the same span. The details give `part_relative_span` with `start` and `end`. A span outside every
  delivered part keeps the message of 0.85.3.
- `review_context_repair_mismatch` names the expected event ids in recorded order, the missing event ids, and the
  expected evidence in the order of the events. The details give `expected_event_ids`, `missing_event_ids` and
  `expected_evidence`. The same refusal covers a repair that binds the right events with other evidence.
- The message of `record_command_timeout` says that the reader did not finish within its budget of processor time.
  The code, the details and the recovery do not change.
- `problem-candidate-transfer` names the remedy when `destination_workspace` does not exist (open the record with
  `problem-init` first) and gives an example of a path that cannot be resolved (a loop of symbolic links). The code
  stays `invalid_input`.
- `docs/research-cli.md`, `docs/research-decisions.md` and `skills/math-conjecturer/SKILL.md` have the rewritten
  texts: the candidate transfer refusals, the startup contract of Codex CLI 0.167.0, the `report` step of
  `record-check`, the time budget and the local tool limits.
- The section on `--time-budget` now says that input which the option parser rejects, for example `abc`, ends with a
  usage error and exit status 2. Only a value that the parser accepts and that is not finite or is out of range is
  `invalid_input`. The maximum of 100,000,000 seconds is a limit of the harness inside the range that macOS accepts
  for an interval timer.
- Incident ledger entry `bar-part-locator-and-repair-binding-diagnostics`, with a fixture of synthetic content in the
  shape of the incident.

## What stays the same

- The acceptance of a span, a repair and an evidence entry does not change. A stored repair that a set comparison
  accepted is still accepted.
- No file that a reviewer route pins changes, so no route becomes stale.
- No record, review, gate or obligation changes.

## What stored records see

- Nothing changes in a stored record. No migration is necessary. Stored packet, prompt and request digests stay
  verifiable.

## Known limits

- A slot that a repeated refusal exhausted still ends with `review_slot_exhausted`. The bounded delivery of a refusal
  to the retry and the recovery of the slot are planned in the continuity design of the record
  `20261113110022-every-open-record-and-review-capacity`.
- The CLI reference does not yet describe the new details of `invalid_locator` and `review_context_repair_mismatch`.
  The writer's pass on these texts comes in a later release.
