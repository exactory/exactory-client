# Exactory 0.91.6

This release gives the `math-conjecturer` skill a new standard for the value of a problem. A problem has no
importance of its own when it is posed. Its value comes from its place in a line of work, and from what a
resolution changes for work that exists. A new value record names the works in which the statement arises. It also
holds a new public section, "What a resolution would change". The reviewers grade significance from both. The
release follows 0.91.5. Its record is
`misc/harness-improvement/20261119212253-impact-of-a-resolution-lineage-and-future` of the exactory repository.

## What changed

The skill:

- "Exploration" states a second principle beside the absolute one. The agent looks for the statement that the
  work of a field needs, and it poses that statement. It does not widen, strengthen or generalize a statement so
  that more follows from it.
- A place of a statement is a work, or a group of works on one question, in which the statement arises. The work
  states the statement as open, needs it, stops at it, or states an open item that an answer decides. Results
  that the statement only continues are a place too, and they show no need.
- A full reading notes what a work states as open, what it assumes, and where its argument stops. The observation
  of a candidate says where the candidate arises. Sometimes no work read states the candidate, needs it or stops
  at it. The agent then runs one bounded search for such a work before the selection.
- The motivation says where the question comes from. It opens with the line of results that leads to the step at
  which the statement arises. The results stand in the order of their dates, each with its year and its work.
- The manual compose step asks for that line of results and for what a resolution would change. It does not ask
  the agent to argue the importance of a problem.

The value record:

- A new value record holds the part `places` of the accounts, with at least one entry
  (`problem_places_required` otherwise). An entry is `{id, fact, scope, role, relation_ids, evidence,
  evidence_ids}` with the optional `date`. `role` is an open value, and the described kinds are `stated`,
  `needed`, `decides`, `stops` and `continues`. Each place rests on a full reading
  (`problem_place_evidence_missing` otherwise).
- A new value record holds `changes_if_resolved`, the paragraphs of the section "What a resolution would change"
  (`problem_changes_if_resolved_required` otherwise). The section says three things. The first is what a
  resolution decides or supplies under each answer, and for which work. The second is what a resolution supplies
  at the step where the recorded arguments stop. The third is which next question or step of the line a
  resolution opens.
- At least one paragraph of the motivation and at least one paragraph of `changes_if_resolved` cite a place
  (`problem_value_place_uncited` otherwise).
- `problem-value` counts the joined text of `changes_if_resolved` as the post command counts it. The text has 100
  to 5,000 characters (`problem_changes_if_resolved_length` otherwise). A text of another length is refused before
  the reviews.

The reviews:

- A result review of such a value record carries the paragraph `exactory-problem-changes-if-resolved-v1`. An
  adjudicator of `significance` or `calibration` receives the paragraph with the definition of the item.
- The paragraph gives a reading of grounds B1 and B2. The rubric keeps its items, levels and thresholds.
- By the reading, a place counts for a level under three conditions. A work read in full shows the place. The
  item is open in that work. The item is the statement, or an established relation ties the statement to it.
- Results that the statement only continues count for no level. A result that the works already have counts for
  no level. Level 2 on B2 also needs an account of what a resolution opens. No level rests on the judgment that a
  statement is natural on its face.
- The reviewers also judge the line of results in the motivation and each sentence of the new section.
- Every new slate review carries the paragraph `exactory-problem-slate-places-v1`. It asks, for every candidate,
  in which work the candidate arises. A new slate packet holds the member `place_review`.

The post:

- When the value record holds the section, `export --kind problem` and `export --kind problem-override` write
  `changes-if-resolved.md`.
- `exactory post-problem` takes `--changes-if-resolved` or `--changes-if-resolved-file`. It sends the text as
  `changesIfResolved`. The problem page shows the text under the heading "What a resolution would change",
  between "Motivation" and "Current state".
- The command compares the answer of the server with the text that it sent. If the answer does not hold that
  text, the command says so and exits with a status of its own. The problem is then posted without the text.

## Compatibility

No transport file differs from the release before this one, so no reviewer route becomes stale. Stored records
keep their bytes and their digests. A value record without `places` and without `changes_if_resolved` stays valid.
Its evidence digest, its delivered evidence, its export and its prompts are those of the release before this one.

Every recorded prompt keeps its bytes and stays verified. New prompts differ in two cases. The first case is a
result prompt or an adjudicator prompt for a value record that holds `changes_if_resolved`. The second case is
every new slate prompt. Such a prompt has a new template id and a new prompt hash.

CAUTION: Post a problem with `changesIfResolved` only to a server that returns `changesIfResolved` in a problem
detail. An older server stores the problem without the text, and the content of a problem is immutable.

Two payloads that the release before this one accepted are now refused:

- A `problem-value` payload without `changes_if_resolved`, or with accounts that hold no `places`. Add both.
- A `problem-value` payload whose motivation cites no place. Cite a place in one paragraph of the motivation and
  in one paragraph of `changes_if_resolved`.

Some records hold a value record that is older than this release, and their statement is not posted. For such a
record, the skill tells the agent to record a new value record with both parts before the decision. The result
reviews are then refreshed in their slots. A posted record needs no step.
