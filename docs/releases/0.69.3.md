# Exactory 0.69.3

This patch fixes findings 1, 2, 6 and 7 of the independent 0.69.2 review in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261031165300-review-planning-rebuilds-lineage-requests/codex-outputs/20261031T191510-astra-t3-final-review.md`
of the exactory repository, following `DESIGN-T3.md` in its parent directory.
It also removes repeated material-objection evidence from newly planned final packets.

## What changed and why

- A final problem evidence turn requires every artifact body referenced by its material objections'
  `version_evidence` in the fitted request, matched by the full reference. Transport elision now refuses planning
  with `review_input_too_large`, `stage: final` and `unit: null`, including when answering source requests.
  Part turns omit this evidence, so accepting its loss in the final turn could leave it undelivered (finding 1).
- The single-unit fallback leaves a body that does not fit pending and tries later bodies in owned-artifact order.
  Tiny bodies that shrink pending entries can still collectively make the initial request fit, using the actual
  request's character cost, including Unicode and JSON escaping. Previously,
  testing only prefixes could refuse a turn despite a later fitting body making progress (finding 2).
- Five regressions cover final elision refusal, full-reference attachment in the recorded fitted final request,
  source-reply final refusal, progress by a later fitting body, and tiny Unicode bodies (finding 6). The objection
  reader's docstring now distinguishes part packets without delivered evidence from final packets carrying it
  (finding 7).
- Final problem evidence packets, including final source replies, keep the first full entry at each canonical
  `evidence.digest` across `material_objections`. Objections are visited in order, then `version_evidence` and
  `version_source_mappings` in list order. Each later equal entry becomes exactly
  `{delivery: "repeated-in-material-objections", objection_id, field, index, digest, note}`. The objection id,
  field and zero-based index identify the first full entry in this packet. The note is the text
  `FINAL_REPEATED_OBJECTION_EVIDENCE_NOTE` of `prompts/t3-texts.md`, written by Fable and reviewed by Astra 6 at
  max. This happens in the part planner's
  final projection before evidence attachment and the packet digest.
- The supplied read-only measurement of unknotting at store revision 870 put Slot A's final request at
  1,311,706 characters before the last part response and 1,340,000 to 1,357,000 with it, above Claude's
  1,300,000-character limit. Replayed reviewer responses occupy 464,802 characters and remain in every request.
  The final packet's `material_objections` occupied 452,028 characters: six objections with 29 `version_evidence`
  entries, only 12 distinct (184,924 characters distinct and 254,693 repeated). `problem-value-1` occurred under
  all six; `problem-attempt-2` and reading `full-1810.05125v2` occurred under four. Removing the repeated entries
  reduces the frame without discarding evidence or reviewer responses. These are the supplied baseline
  measurements, not a new replay measurement under this patch. With this patch, on a copy of the record at
  revision 874, slot A's final request is 1,092,454 characters and slot B's final turn that answers its source
  requests is 1,006,516; their material objections take 207,561 characters, and no evidence entry is elided.
- A shared resolver verifies the target position and canonical digest and returns the full entry without
  mutating the packet. Required objection artifact bodies are collected from resolved `version_evidence` and
  checked once per full descriptor. Equal byte hashes with different descriptors remain distinct.

## Evidence reader audit

`grep -rn -E 'material_objections|version_evidence|version_source_mappings' research_harness` was checked,
along with the callers that consume these readers.

| Reader | Resolution needed and change |
| --- | --- |
| `review_problem_parts._project` | The final projection deduplicates both lists before the digest. The part projection still removes both lists. |
| `review_problem_parts.prepare_packet` | Yes. Resolves objection evidence before collecting required artifact bodies, then checks each full descriptor once. A full record first carried in source mappings still requires its artifacts when evidence references it. |
| `review_protocol._list_packet_records`, `collect_carried_records`, `find_carried_record` | Yes. The first reader resolves each evidence entry, so the downstream record lookup for review contracts, corrections and adjudication sees the full projection and its supplied artifact descriptors. |
| `review_supplied_evidence.collect_qualified_objections` | Yes. Resolves evidence before reading nested review objections; scientific-history recursion uses the enclosing packet of each turn. |
| `review_supplied_evidence.supplied_context` | Yes. Resolves objection source mappings in frozen and current lookup. Corrections, `describe_version_evidence`, adjudication and `adjudicator_undelivered_references` inherit this lookup. Artifact bodies still come from actual packet evidence. |
| `review_supplied_evidence.list_delivered_objections` | No. Enumerates objection objects without inspecting their evidence. Its evidence-reading callers use the resolver. |
| `research_decisions._visual_supplied_context` | Yes. Resolves mappings for native visual and series contract checks and for the post-gate reference audit. |
| `research_decisions.validate_review_contract`, `_validate_value` | The direct objection list is used only for required finding ids. Evidence checks use the resolved carried-record and supplied-context readers above; output fields and requirements are unchanged. |
| `research_decisions._list_delivered_objection_ids`, `_assign_objection_identities` | No additional change. These reads use ids, stated ids and claims, without inspecting evidence. |
| `review_context_repair.validate_reassessment` | No direct change. Its qualified-identity lookup inherits resolution of nested objections from the updated `collect_qualified_objections` reader. |
| `research_decisions.find_undelivered_review_references` | Resolution is supplied by the updated record and mapping readers. The regression verifies the post-gate audit accepts evidence cited through a repeated entry. |
| `research_decisions._assess_problem_coverage` and `assess_problem_objections` | No direct resolution needed. Coverage reads problem units and attached artifact bodies; the objection check compares ids and material versions. Both keep their existing rules. |
| `research_decisions.find_undelivered_adjudication_keys` | No. Compares an adjudicator's top-level version evidence and mappings, which this final-material-objection projection does not deduplicate. |
| `review_protocol.describe_version_evidence`, `describe_objection_delivery`, `VERSION_EVIDENCE_KEYS` consumers and packet builders | No direct resolution needed. They build full deliveries, extract generated evidence or forward top-level adjudication evidence. Prior supplied content is resolved through the readers above. |
| `review_protocol.collect_references`, `_evidence` | No. Generic traversal still encounters each distinct full entry once and attaches its referenced descriptors. A marker carries no artifact descriptor. |
| `review_transport.REPEATABLE_KEYS` and prompt definitions | No semantic evidence read. Existing replay compaction and all reviewer prompt strings are unchanged. The transport module was not edited. |

## Existing records

Recorded values, reviewer prompts and their authentication, response contracts and post-gate rules stay the same.
`assess_problem_objections` checks material-version identity in the frozen final packet; its comparison is unchanged.
New final assignments enforce attachment before recording without reinterpreting prior reviews.
The route-pinned transport and cost modules and `review_state_evaluation.py` are unchanged. Existing 0.69.2 part
series can continue under the same series, units, receipts and prompt rules.
Part packets still omit objection evidence; replayed reviewer responses, final response requirements, source
delivery and all post-gate standards stay the same. Recorded packets are never rewritten. An earlier final packet
with full repeated objections still reconstructs and authenticates under its original bytes.
