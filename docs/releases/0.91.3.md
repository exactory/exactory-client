# Exactory 0.91.3

This release removes the kind of start of a math-conjecturer exploration. From 0.83.0 to 0.91.2 a problem record
stated a kind of start, and the skill gave a procedure of its own for two kinds: a phenomenon that appears in
several places (introduced in 0.83.0, refined up to 0.86.0), and a simple thought experiment (introduced in
0.74.1). A new record now holds no kind of
start, and the two procedures are retired. A record that an earlier release opened with a start keeps working.
The release follows 0.91.2. Its record is
`misc/harness-improvement/20261119131613-retire-phenomenon-and-thought-experiment-entries` of the exactory
repository.

## What changed

New records:

- The `math-conjecturer` skill has no step that chooses or records a kind of start. Every exploration is opened
  on an area and a field question, with `{slug, area, field, authors}`. The words of a request give the direction
  of the exploration and select nothing else.
- `problem-init` refuses a payload that holds `start`, whatever its value, with the new code
  `problem_start_kind_removed`. A new marker has the shape that it had in 0.82.0.
- The skill's list of candidate sources names five kinds: `own_record`, `literature`, `computation`, `section`
  and `user`. `problem-candidate` refuses the source kinds `phenomenon` and `thought_experiment` in a record that
  does not hold them, and `forming` refuses the two as the kind of a question in such a record. Both refusals
  have the code `problem_start_kind_removed`, and each message says what to give in place of the kind.
- When a request names one of the two retired starts, the skill tells the agent to say in one sentence that the
  kinds of start were removed, and to run the exploration in the direction that the rest of the request gives,
  without a stop.

Records that hold a start or a retired kind:

- A record holds a retired kind when its `start` entry names the kind, when one of its candidates has it as the
  source kind, or when one of its forming calls has it as the kind of its question. Such a record accepts a new
  candidate and a new forming call of that kind, a new version of such a candidate, a slate and a report as
  before.
- Its marker, its candidates and its forming calls keep their bytes and their digests. `status` and the report of
  the exploration show its start as before.
- These rules keep their behavior: a `phenomenon` candidate cites at least one forming call
  (`problem_candidate_forming_missing`), a forming call whose `question.kind` is `phenomenon` names at least one
  place, a slate that selects a candidate formed in the record cites a slate review
  (`problem_slate_review_required`), the slate prompt carries the paragraph `exactory-problem-slate-forming-v1`,
  and the report is due for the two start kinds.
- The new document `docs/retired-starts.md` holds the procedure of each retired start, the rules of its kinds,
  and the way in which a new record takes up a candidate of such a record. The skill names the document, and the
  two subsections that held the procedures are no longer in the skill.

The forming commands stay a tool of every problem record. The skill states in "Exploration" two rules of the
forming records that the removed subsections held: the further field of a `transfer` and of a `comparison`, and
the record of a correction. The CLI reference has the new section "The removed kind of start".
`docs/research-workflow.md` and `docs/research-tools.md` state the removal, and the `ideate` skill no longer uses
the two starts as examples of a question.

## Compatibility

No file of a reviewer route's runtime identity changes, and no reviewer prompt changes, so no route becomes stale.
Stored records, packets, prompts and requests keep their meaning and their digests. A record without a `start`,
as every record opened before 0.83.0, behaves as before except for the two retired kinds, which it accepts only
when it already holds them through a candidate or a forming call.

Two payloads that 0.91.2 accepted are now refused. A `problem-init` payload with `start` is refused: leave the
member out. A `problem-candidate` or `forming` payload with the kind `phenomenon` or `thought_experiment` is
refused in a record that does not hold the kind: give the source kind of the material, or describe the kind of
the question in your own words.
