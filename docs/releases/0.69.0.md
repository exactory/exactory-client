# Exactory 0.69.0

This release delivers the problem evidence of a result review in parts when the request of the review does not fit
one input of a local text route. The harness plans a series of turns of the same reviewer: part turns that carry the
frame of the evidence and a share of its items, then a final turn that carries the frame and returns the review. While
a session is not a series, a packet that fits is built as before. The work is recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261031101231-problem-result-packet-exceeds-route-limit` of the exactory repository.

## Why

On 2026-10-31 the first result assignment of the unknotting record (`assignment-result-a`, route
`route-claude-opus-max-a`) failed in 0.017 s with `review_input_too_large`, and the CLI received nothing. Its packet
measured 4,731,060 characters as canonical JSON against the Claude route limit of 1,300,000. The evidence bodies,
which the reductions withhold, were 2,423,099 characters. The `problem_evidence`, which no reduction touches, was
2,196,840: `passages` 1,085,104 (333 inline passages of 23 readings), `assessment_evidence` 366,613, `readings`
363,234 and `openness_searches` 313,287. The KMP record is behind it: the 29 searches that its openness minimum binds
have 1,462,365 characters, so its `openness_searches` alone will exceed the limit once its openness finding is
recorded. No reduction of the input shortens `problem_evidence`, a shorter projection cannot fit (`passages` and
`readings` together are 1,448,338 characters), and the modules that fit and send a request are pinned by every
route's runtime. The [decision guide](../research-decisions.md#problem-result-evidence-in-parts) gives the
measurements and the reasoning.

## When a result session is delivered in parts

- A problem `result` assignment on `claude_cli_v1`, `codex_cli_v1` or `codex_cli_framed_v1` whose request does not
  fit after the existing reductions becomes part turn 1 of a series. An initial assignment, a refresh and a
  continuation are planned this way; a context repair, a `complete-visible-v1` continuation and the adjudicator are
  not.
- The planner runs in `review-assignment`, after the history is known. It builds the request that the transport
  sends and measures it with the route adapter's own `packet_text`. While a session is not a series, a packet that
  fits is returned unchanged; once a session is a series, its later turns stay in parts.
- The frame is the keys `claim`, `openness`, `value`, `assessment`, `attempt`, `results`, `citations` and `delivery`
  of `problem_evidence`; every turn carries it whole. The units are the items of `openness_searches`,
  `assessment_evidence`, `readings`, `passages`, `computations` and `formal_checks`, named by key and index, each
  with the artifact bodies that it owns. A passage is never split.
- A part turn takes the longest prefix of the remaining units whose fitted request fits the route limit and attaches
  every owned body complete, with at most 800,000 characters of units (`PART_UNIT_CHARACTERS`). When the frame and
  the first remaining unit cannot fit, `review-assignment` is refused with `review_input_too_large` and the details
  `{stage, turn, input_characters, input_limit_characters, frame_characters, unit}`, and nothing is recorded.
- Each turn's packet carries `problem_evidence_parts` with `protocol` (`exactory-problem-evidence-parts-v1`),
  `series_assignment_id`, `stage`, `turn`, `units`, `delivered_before`, `delivered_now`, `remaining` and `note`.
  `delivered_now` and `remaining` give index ranges `[start, end]` per key, `start` included and `end` excluded. The
  prompt adds the instructions of the stage after the excerpt rules under the unchanged template id
  `exactory-problem-review-v5/result` and records `parameters.evidence_parts`.
- The [CLI reference](../research-cli.md#problem-evidence-in-parts) states the exact rules.

## A part turn

- The response is exactly `{stage: "result", phase: "evidence_part", part, notes, excerpts}`: exactly one note
  `{unit: {key, index}, note}` for every delivered reading and record unit, in any order and none for another unit,
  each nonblank, at most 60,000 characters of notes together (`NOTES_CHARACTERS`), optional excerpts under the
  excerpt rules, and no `source_requests`.
- `review-run` checks the response when an authenticated run completes and writes the output refusal
  `invalid_value_review` under `review.shape` when it fails, as for a `native_math` review. A refused part delivers
  nothing, and the existing retry and `review_slot_exhausted` rules apply.
- Continue a part turn with a new assignment of the same role, reviewer, route and author and
  `context: {prior_assignment_id}` while the latest output has `phase: "evidence_part"`. `next` and the post gate
  list `problem_evidence_part_pending {assignment_id, turn, remaining}` for a part turn without a continuation.
  `value-review` refuses a part turn with the same code and writes no refusal record.

## The final turn and the coverage check

- The final turn is the first turn after every unit reached a valid earlier turn. It carries the frame,
  `delivered_before` with every earlier turn (its assignment id, its selected attempt id and its ranges) and no
  units. It returns the ordinary result review, or `source_requests`; the continuation that answers them is a final
  turn again.
- The post gate counts each item of a split key when an equal item reached the reviewer under the same index in a
  valid turn of the series with the series digest, each reading's ranges through the union of the inline passages of
  those turns, and each owned artifact by its complete bytes in one of them. The final turn's `delivered_before`
  must match those turns exactly. A review without `problem_evidence_parts` is checked as before.
- The guarantee: every item of the current evidence reached the reviewer in an authenticated turn of the same
  session. The model call that writes the review holds the items of the earlier turns only through the reviewer's
  notes, its verified excerpts and the earlier packets that the replayed history still holds.
- `value-review` and the `review_evidence_undelivered` audit read the authenticated packets of the series, so a span
  delivered as an inline passage in a part turn is a delivered bounded span; it gives no whole-artifact credit.
- A series freezes the evidence digest of its first turn. Once a session is a series, a continuation whose evidence
  digest changed and a refresh of the series start a new series with fresh delivery, also when the new evidence fits
  one input. Only a first turn of a session that never was a series is measured against the limit, and it is
  delivered in parts when it does not fit.

## The failed result assignments of the unknotting record

`assignment-result-a` and `assignment-result-b` each hold one failed attempt without a CLI start. A new run of either
builds the same oversized request, so they are replaced. The slot rule is narrowed to this case: an initial problem
`result` assignment whose every attempt failed with `review_input_too_large` before the CLI started can be replaced
by a new initial assignment of the same reviewer, route and author in the same slot, and the old assignment then
returns `review_assignment_superseded`. A replacement after a completed output, a pending invocation or a failure
with another code, or on another route or with another author, is refused with `review_resampling_forbidden`.

The flow for each slot:

1. Record `review-assignment` with a new id, role `result`, the slot's route and reviewer (slot A:
   `route-claude-opus-max-a` and `claude-opus-5-5-max-a`; slot B: `route-claude-opus-max-b` and
   `claude-opus-5-5-max-b`), the same author, `dossier_id: null` and an empty context. It becomes part turn 1.
2. Run it with `review-run`. While the output has `phase: "evidence_part"`, record a continuation with
   `context: {prior_assignment_id}` of the latest turn and run it. The turn after the last part is the final turn.
3. Record the turn that returned the review with `value-review`.
4. Do the same for the other slot, then record the decision and run the post gate.

On a copy of the unknotting store, the replacement assignment of slot A is part turn 1: 73 units with 799,414
characters of units and owned bodies, in a request of 1,287,036 characters as the Claude route measures it, with 29
notes due. With the 800,000-character cap, the units of that slot (about 2.1 million characters) need three part turns
and a final turn; that count is an estimate, and the record's notes give the turns as they ran.

## Codes

- New: `problem_evidence_part_pending`, the obligation of a part turn without its continuation and the `value-review`
  refusal of a part turn.
- `review_input_too_large` is also raised by `review-assignment` for a frame and unit that cannot fit, with the size
  details above; nothing is recorded then.
- `invalid_value_review` under `review.shape` for a malformed part response.
- `review_assignment_superseded` for a failed initial result assignment that a new one replaced.
- Unchanged in meaning: `problem_evidence_unassessed`, `review_evidence_undelivered`, `review_resampling_forbidden`,
  `review_assignment_replaced` and `review_source_continuation_fork`.

## Existing records

- No module that a route pins by its bytes changed: `review_transport.py`, `review_claude_transport.py`,
  `review_codex_transport.py`, `review_codex_framed_transport.py`, `review_codex_visual_transport.py` and the modules
  they pin are as in 0.68.0. Every recorded route stays valid and needs no new probe. The reduction orders do not
  change.
- While a session is not a series, a packet that fits is built as before, byte for byte, with the same prompt. No
  stored packet carries `problem_evidence_parts`, so every recorded result review, bar and slate keeps its gate
  outcome.
- Every recorded attempt authenticates as before: a part packet is an ordinary saved packet, its request is built by
  the unchanged transport, and the part and final prompts are new rendered prompts under `parameters.evidence_parts`
  that the prompt match recognizes beside the existing ones.
- The store is append-only and needs no migration.

## Upgrade notes

- A record tool that drives a result session continues it while the latest output has nonempty `source_requests` or
  `phase: "evidence_part"`. The record tools of the unknotting and KMP records are outside the plugin and need that
  condition added.
- A result assignment that was refused at run time with `review_input_too_large` under 0.68.0 is replaced as above,
  not run again.

## Known limits

- The problem adjudicator's packet carries the full problem evidence and is not delivered in parts. An adjudication
  on a record whose evidence does not fit still fails with `review_input_too_large`. When a result review of either
  record leaves a continuing objection or a rubric shortfall that needs an adjudication, the mechanism is extended
  to the adjudicator in a later release.
- A context repair of a slot whose session holds a part turn is refused with
  `review_context_repair_history_unrecorded`.
- Repeated records inside a series, for example the search records that both `openness_searches` and
  `assessment_evidence` carry, are delivered under each index. The unknotting record repeats 279,665 characters this
  way.
- The series check counts delivery to the reviewer's session, not what the final model call held. The guarantee is
  stated above and in the decision guide.
- The turn-count estimates (three part turns and a final turn per slot for the unknotting record under the
  800,000-character cap, three to five turns per slot for the KMP record) were not run before the release.
