# Exactory 0.85.0

This release lets a math-conjecturer record name the further areas that its statements concern, take up a
candidate of another record with its forming history, and report how its exploration ended. The release follows
0.84.0. It is stage C, the last stage, of the work recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261109153246-abc-type-conjecture-creation` of the exactory repository.

## Why

0.83.0 records how a statement is formed, and 0.84.0 adds the accounts of a statement with the reviewer text that
reads them. Three things were left.

A problem record has one subject class, and its two bars stated the standard of that class only. A statement that
gathers others often has a consequence, an earlier statement or a nearer alternative in another area. The reviewers
graded such a claim without a standard of its area that they had stated before they saw a candidate.

The forming records stayed in the record that made them. When another record took a candidate up, the skill of
0.83.0 told the agent to state the forms again by hand. The restated forms had new ids and new dates, and only
their descriptions named the source.

No record said how an exploration ended. No single record showed the forms that were refuted or set aside, the
candidates that were withdrawn, and, for each post, whether its statement was posted with an established gain, as
an ordinary problem, or without that assessment.

This release builds the areas and the report of the design, which serve statements 8 and 9 of the principle that
the owner accepted on 2026-11-09, and the command that carries the forming history.

## What changed

- New command `exactory-research problem-area`. It records one further area of a problem record, `{id, area,
  reason}`: a `math.*` subject class other than the record's own, and the reason for naming it. The record keeps
  its one subject class, and the opening marker keeps its bytes. The command runs while no slate review and no
  result review has been assigned. The new codes are `problem_area_duplicate` and
  `problem_area_new_record_required`.
- The bar, slate, result and adjudicator packets of a record that names a further area carry `problem_areas`. The
  bar and result prompts then carry the areas paragraph, `exactory-problem-areas-v1`, which replaces the
  placeholder of 0.84.0. For a bar, it asks for the standard of each area separately: the active lines, the
  nearest problems and conjectures, what counts as a substantial advance, and the meaning of levels 2 and 3. For a
  result review, it says that each relation, each earlier statement and each alternative is judged by the standard
  of the area that it belongs to, and that every grade is given by the standard of the record's subject class.
- A bar that was given before an area was recorded states no standard for it. `problem-slate`, a slate assignment
  and a result assignment refuse it with `problem_bar_calibration_required`, which now also carries `area_ids`.
  Assigning the bar again to the same reviewer continues it in the same session and slot, with every area, and
  the next slate cites the continued bars. The standard of every area thus comes from a reviewer who has seen no
  candidate.
- New command `exactory-research forming-import`, the third command of the Ideate tool in a problem record. It
  gives a record the forming history of another problem record whose candidates it takes up. It keeps a verified
  snapshot of the source in the destination, as the objection carry of 0.82.0 keeps the proof of its source, and
  it records one manifest. The manifest lists the forming calls and the outcomes of the source, its probes, its
  plans, runs and assessments, the searches that its test declarations name, and the readings and solver snapshots
  that its outcomes cite. Each keeps its origin, its original id, its original date and its standing. No record of
  the source becomes a record of the destination, and the source is not written.
- A candidate, an outcome and an entry of the accounts cite an imported record as `{kind: "record", origin,
  record_kind, id, digest}`. An imported probe stays exploratory, and an imported full reading, assessed run,
  cited solver snapshot and outcome with admissible evidence are admissible. An imported forming call or outcome
  counts as a forming record of the candidate for the slate review rule and for the history that a gain claim and
  a passed test need. The new codes are `problem_forming_reference_missing` and `problem_forming_evidence_invalid`.
- The harness shows what a record inherited apart from its own work. The derived parts of a value record with
  accounts gain `inherited`: the forms, the runs and the counts of each source. The earlier computations beside a
  test include the probes and the runs of each source. The result reviewers receive every imported record with its
  origin, its date and its standing, and a slate reviewer receives them under `problem_forming_imports`.
- The destination of an import stays linked to its source. `gate post`, `status`, `export --kind problem` and
  `problem-decision` with `post` compare the current forming history of the source with the imported one. The new
  codes are `problem_forming_refresh_required` and `problem_forming_source_unavailable`, and a new import under a
  new id refreshes the link. The other codes of the command are `problem_forming_self_import`,
  `problem_forming_source_revision_mismatch`, `problem_forming_provenance_invalid`,
  `problem_forming_origin_conflict` and `problem_forming_sources_exceeded`. `record-check` verifies every import
  from its retained snapshot.
- New command `exactory-research problem-candidate-transfer`. It records in the source that a candidate of its
  latest slate was taken up in another record: the candidate, the absolute path of the destination workspace and
  the reason. The new codes are `problem_candidate_transfer_not_on_slate` and `problem_candidate_transfer_self`.
- New command `exactory-research problem-report`. It records how an exploration ended. The author gives the
  finding that would make the exploration worth reopening. The harness computes the rest from the records: the
  start, every form with its standing, its disposition and the evidence of its outcome, the imports, the
  candidates with their endings (posted, withdrawn, or taken up in another record), the statements with their
  publication and the classification of each post kept apart, what changed after each post, the consequences that
  the record proved, the counts, what was spent, and the outcome.
- The classification of a post reads the value record that the post decision covered. It is "a gain was
  established" when the accounts of that record claim a gain, and "ordinary" when they claim none. It is "kind
  unassessed" for a post that was made outside the gate, and for a post whose value record holds no accounts. The
  outcome of a report is one of six: a statement of the kind was posted; only ordinary problems were posted;
  statements were posted and the kind of at least one is unassessed; statements were formed and none was posted;
  no statement was formed; the assessment is incomplete.
- The report is due for a record that started from a phenomenon or a thought experiment, and for every record that
  holds a forming call, once its current slate selects nothing or every candidate that it selects is posted,
  withdrawn or taken up in another record. `status` and `next` then carry the advisory `problem_report_due`. They
  carry `problem_report_stale` when the records that the latest report read changed after its cutoff. The command
  refuses a due report while a form is open (`problem_report_forms_open`), and
  `problem_report_evidence_missing` names a post whose covered value record no saved review packet delivers. The
  output folders show the current report in `final/problem/reports/` and the further areas in
  `final/problem/areas/`.
- A `withdraw` decision of `problem-decision` now records what it withdraws: the current statement with its
  candidate, or, while no statement is formulated, the candidates that the current slate selects. The report reads
  these fields.
- The math-conjecturer skill gains three subsections of "Exploration": "Further areas", "The forming history of a
  candidate taken up in another record" and "The end of an exploration". The rule that one record holds the
  exploration now covers a further area, and the instruction of 0.83.0 to state the forms again in the other
  record is replaced by the import.
- Each refusal of the new code names the repair in its message. `docs/research-cli.md` gains "Imported forming
  history", "Further areas" and "The report of an exploration", and `docs/research-tools.md` lists `forming-import`
  as a command of the Ideate tool.
- The CLI reference is corrected for 0.84.0 in four places. It names the refusal `problem_surplus_missing`, for a
  weaker alternative without its surplus. It says that the `prior_knowledge` of a test declaration needs at least
  one entry. It says that a surplus with the status `used` cites an established relation, and that every search
  that an alternative names is validated.

## What stays the same

- No rubric item, level or threshold changes, and the response shapes of the bar, slate, result and adjudicator
  roles are unchanged. The thresholds read the grades that a reviewer gives by the standard of the record's
  subject class.
- A record has one subject class, a statement keeps the class of its record, and a post has one area.
- The carry paragraph of 0.82.0 and the accounts, slate and replacement paragraphs of 0.84.0 keep their bytes. A
  prompt whose packet calls for no paragraph keeps the bytes of 0.82.0, and a recorded assignment rebuilds its
  prompt from its own envelope.
- The carry of objections is unchanged. The import brings the forming history and no objection.
- The report passes no gate and receives no review. Its advisories change no readiness, no obligation and no next
  action, so a post does not wait for a report.
- `intent` stays refused in a problem record, and the three forming commands are refused outside one
  (`orchestration_conflict`).
- The absolute principle of problem selection and rubric item A7 are unchanged.

## What stored records see

- A record that names no further area keeps its packets, its prompt bytes and its bars. A fixture test pins the
  frozen prompts of 0.82.0 for the bar, slate and result roles.
- A record without an import keeps its evidence projection, its evidence digest and its views. A fixture test
  pins them.
- One difference is expected in `status` and `next`: the advisory `problem_report_due`, for a record that holds a
  forming call, or whose marker records the start `phenomenon` or `thought_experiment`, once its exploration has
  ended. A record without either has no due report, and a fixture test pins its `status`.
- A `withdraw` decision that an earlier release recorded keeps its bytes. It names no statement and no candidate,
  so the report counts it for a candidate only when every statement that was recorded before it formulates that
  one candidate.
- A posted statement gets no new obligation, and no recorded review becomes stale.
- Records of other orchestrations do not change, and no migration is necessary.

## Known limits

- The areas paragraph has not been tried on a record. The first record that names a further area tests whether
  reviewers state and apply the standards of several areas.
- The harness checks that the packet of each bar delivered every area. It does not check that the bar states a
  standard for each area. The paragraph tells a result reviewer to say so when a standard is missing.
- The `reason` of a further area reaches the bar reviewers as the author wrote it. The harness does not check
  that it names no candidate, and the skill tells the agent to write it without one.
- A slate reviewer and an adjudicator receive `problem_areas` in the packet and no paragraph about it.
- An import lists the whole forming history of its source. The history, the runs and the lineage counts that it
  computes are those of the candidates that are taken up.
- The reviewer paragraphs of 0.84.0 do not describe imported records. The slate paragraph says that the packet
  does not deliver the content of the forming records of a candidate, and for an imported record the packet does
  deliver it. A result reviewer receives the inherited forms and counts without a sentence that explains them.
- The replacement part of the accounts reads the forms of its own record, and a replacement entry rests on a
  reading, a computation or a result of its own record. For a statement that was posed in place of a refuted form
  of the source, the skill tells the agent to enter that form as a refuted conjecture and to establish the
  refutation in the destination.
- The freshness of an import is checked at the gate, at the export and at the post decision. A review assignment
  does not check it, as it checks a carry.
- `problem-candidate-transfer` needs a slate that lists the candidate, so a source that restarts before its bars
  records no transfer. The command does not open the destination, and the harness does not check that the
  destination imported the forming history.
- The dependency digest of a report covers whole record kinds. Any later probe, review, reviewer call or charge to
  a resource account makes the report stale, also when it changes no computed part.
- A report that is recorded while the record holds no current slate does not get the outcome "the assessment is
  incomplete". It takes its outcome from the posts, the statements and the forms that the record holds.
- The report lists the consequences that the record proved. The record does not hold whether their proofs were
  posted, and the report says so.
- The harness starts no re-examination of a posted statement that receives no reply. The report shows the date of
  each post and whether a fact after the post is recorded.
- A staged review still cannot post before its quality measurement.
