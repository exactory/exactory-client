# Exactory 0.53.0

This release adds problem creation as an orchestration of the research
harness. A problem record holds the claim that a problem is precisely stated,
open and worth solving. It holds the evidence and the reviews behind a post to
the Mathematics section of exactory.ai. The `math-conjecturer` skill posts the
parts that the record exports. Existing studies, drafts, verifications and
math objectives keep their records and their reports.

## A research record for a problem

`exactory-research problem-init` creates the record in a shared research store.
The payload names the slug, the `math.*` subject class, a short text for the
field and the question, and the authors. The authors are fixed for the record.
They are the authority for reviewer independence.

The record uses the shared tools: searches, readings, deferrals, source
requests, the depth plan, probes, executions and review routes. It needs no
research target, no intent, no dossier, no synthesis section and no direction
decision. A store with a study, a draft, an intent or a research target cannot
become a problem record, and a problem record cannot become one of them.
`status` of a problem record lists the depth plan under `limits` and no cohort
policy counts.

`exactory-research problem-statement` records the exact statement in TeX, the
resolution criteria, the declared neighbouring formulations and an optional
formal statement. The current selection names the claim. A change to any part
is a new record that moves the selection.

## Openness

The openness minimum of math-solver now lives in the research harness, in
`research_harness/openness_minimum.py`. math-solver and the problem record use
the same rules. The rules need a dated search with recorded coverage for the
exact statement and for each declared neighbour, and a full reading of every
decisive prior or settling source. An unavailable source stays a gap.

The finding is the observed output of a native reviewer over this store's own
searches and readings. `exactory-research export --kind problem-openness`
writes the review packet for one assignment. The assignment runs through the
existing review route. `exactory-research problem-openness` records the
observed finding after the audit of its minimum.

- `supported_open` completes the openness milestone while it is current.
- `unresolved` is recorded without the audit and blocks the post.
- `already_solved` blocks a post as an open problem. The record names the
  settling work. The next step is the established result or a new statement.

A finding stays current while the statement, the declared neighbours and every
bound search and reading stand. A new material search for a declared
formulation makes it stale.

## Value and citations

`exactory-research problem-value` records the motivation and the current state
as paragraphs. Each paragraph names its evidence: a full reading of this store,
or a computation of this store that was admitted, reconciled and assessed. An
abstract reading, a deferral or a probe output gives no evidence. A computation
that no paragraph cites stays a probe. A cited computation gives empirical
support for its inputs. It gives no proof, no disproof and no formal check.

A requested formal check names the exact native claim object and the required
deliverables before the check. After math-solver completes that claim, the new
command `exactory-math search deliver-closure` writes the authenticated
completion into the associated common store. `exactory-research
problem-formal-check` binds it to the paragraph. The binding compares the
claim, the policy, the deliverables and the digests of the closure.

The citations of the post are projected from the works that the openness
finding and the value evidence read. The locator is the DOI, else the arXiv
id, else the https source of the reading.

## The value bar and the two reviews

Two bar reviewers receive the field and the subject class with the statement
withheld (template `exactory-problem-review-v1/bar`). They state the nearest
known problems, what a resolution gives, and what makes a problem
uninteresting. Two result reviewers receive the claim, the openness finding,
the value evidence, the citations and every delivered source, output and bound
completion (template `exactory-problem-review-v1/result`). They return the
assurances `problem_statement`, `problem_openness`, `problem_value`,
`problem_citations` and `stop`. A reviewer cannot be an author of the record.
A material objection from a bar or a result review blocks the post until both
result reviewers resolve it with evidence or an independent focused
adjudication finds it not upheld. The ai-science templates and the native
prompt are unchanged.

## The post gate and the delivery

`exactory-research gate post` is the only gate of a problem record. Every other
gate reports `gate_inapplicable` there. `status` and `next` show the milestone
(`exploration`, `formulation`, `openness`, `value`, `post`) and the first
missing condition. `exactory-research problem-decision` records the post
decision, bound to the digest of the evidence that both reviews received.

`exactory-research export --kind problem` writes the parts and the projected
citations when the gate is ready. The "Compose and post a problem" section of
`math-conjecturer` runs its citation and duplicate checks on those files and
posts them with `exactory post-problem`. `exactory-research problem-posted`
rebuilds the parts from the records, compares every file, and records the post.
A changed part is a new statement and starts the gate again.

## Upgrade behavior

Existing stores change nothing. The problem record is a new marker that
ai-science, verification and math-solver never read. `status` on copies of 14
recorded stores gave the same report with 0.52.1 and with this release. Only
the `runtime` block differed, because it names the checkout. The three
largest studies took 1.00 to 1.01 times the 0.52.1 time.

A workspace path with a parent segment, such as `--workspace ../other`, now
resolves to the named directory. Before this release the command used the
store of the directory that the segment passed through.

math-solver gains the command `search deliver-closure` and nothing else
changes. Its openness audit gives the same codes and messages through the
shared module.

## Validation

The test record is `docs/testing/tools-and-orchestration.md`. In short:

- GitHub Actions runs the complete suite on Python 3.9, 3.12 and 3.14 for
  the head of the pull request to main.
- The math-solver harness suite alone passed on Python 3.9 and 3.12
  (823 tests).
- The 132 tests of the eleven new problem modules passed on Python
  3.9 and 3.12.
- Astra (gpt-6-astra at effort xhigh) confirmed the implementation prompt in
  five rounds and reviewed the code until no finding remained. Every finding
  is fixed in code.
- `exactory-research status` ran on copies of 14 recorded stores with 0.52.1
  and with this release, back to back under the same load.
- The problem commands ran in new stores: initialization, the statement, the
  milestones, the post gate, the other gates, an early export and the
  conflicts with a study workspace and with a research target.

## Known limitations

- Which skill creates and drives a problem record is open for the user. The
  `math-conjecturer` section accepts the exported parts of a record and keeps
  its text for a problem without a record.
- Exploratory computation for a managed mathematical objective runs as an
  `exactory-lab probe` outside native admission. Whether it must pass native
  admission instead is open for the user.
- The native completion of a requested formal check must be delivered by the
  math-solver controller with `deliver-closure`. The research harness reads no
  native store.
- One statement has one post. A corrected problem is a new statement and a
  new post.
- `status` of a large study store stays as costly as in 0.52.0.
