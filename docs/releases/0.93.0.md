# Exactory 0.93.0

This release selects four new forms of a review: the session rules v4, the parts protocol v3, the final of
protocol v7 in pages, and the math protocol v2. Release 0.92.1 held the code of these forms, and no command
selected them. This release also repairs plans that named a step that the store refused. It adds a search after a
slate that selects nothing, and it repairs the Codex review routes. The release follows 0.92.1. Its record is
`misc/harness-improvement/20261122143013` of the exactory repository.

## The four new forms

A new review starts in a new form when the admission of this form accepts it. A stored chain keeps the form that
its record holds. `docs/research-cli.md` describes each form.

### The session rules v4

- A new `bar`, `slate` or `result` review starts as a session under `exactory-independent-review-sources-v4`. The
  admission of these rules must accept its packet on its route.
- A session is a chain of turns, and each turn is a fresh input. A turn can ask for texts, and a later turn
  delivers them. A final that one request does not hold is taken in pages, with a closing turn.
- The admission stops in three cases: the rules v4 do not serve the role or the route, the support contract is
  scoped, or required evidence is not text. The review then starts under the rules of 0.92.1.
- When the capacity plan does not fit the route, a context repair starts under the earlier rules. A review that
  needs parts starts its series in parts.
- A review that the earlier rules start as a session on a route below the floor of the rules v4 does not start.
  Release 0.92.1 started this session under the rules v3. The refusal is `review_route_capacity_below_floor` with
  `details.reason: "session_below_floor"`. The stop offers "Probe this route" with the tokens that the admission
  needs, or "Move to another route".
- With the rules v4 selected, the capacity stop of a stored v3 head can offer "Reassess the retained review" on
  another route. The stop then offers no "Probe this route".
- `review-assignment` of a review chain that a post closed is refused with `problem_already_posted`.
- A scoped study result runs under the rules v3.

### The parts protocol v3

- `review-assignment` plans a new series under `exactory-review-parts-v3` first, and under the parts protocol v2
  second. A recorded series keeps its protocol for each later turn.
- A new `slate` review whose first request does not fit its route, or withholds a body, is a series under the
  parts protocol v3.
- Each turn carries the table of the targets of the review. The frame of a turn holds a maximum of 200,000
  characters on each route. A review whose table does not fit this frame starts its series under the parts
  protocol v2, as in 0.92.1.
- The largest table that the admission accepted on the release tree had 1,270 targets, with 97,679 characters. A refusal of the
  admission states `target_table` and `frame_maximum_characters` in its details.
- A context repair of a head that is not a series of v3 goes to the parts protocol v2 when v3 refuses it. The
  repair goes through the coverage check of the assignment. A refusal of the planner of v2 gives the capacity stop
  with its answers.
- A context repair of a recorded series of v3 stays in v3. On a route below its floor, the repair gets the
  capacity stop with its answers.
- A page that cites a text that its series did not receive is refused at the page, and the harness assigns the
  page again. The same holds for a source link without a full text. This also holds for a page of a session of
  the rules v4.
- `plan_next` names the chain `parts_v2:<adapter>` under the two protocols. The member `review_parts.protocol` of
  the assignment holds the protocol.

### The final of protocol v7 in pages

- A final that one request does not hold is taken in pages: page turns, condensing turns where they are
  necessary, and one closing turn. Each is a fresh input and an assignment of the chain.
- A chain that an earlier release started also takes its final in pages when one request does not hold it.
- The harness composes the finding from the answers of the pages and of the closing turn. Then the harness
  examines the composed finding by the rules of the finding.
- A condensing turn gets no smaller task when its block cannot be divided into blocks of 1,024 characters or more.
  After its attempts, the turn ends at the capacity decision.
- The admission of a new v7 chain first makes sure that the output of the route holds one final page of one
  target. This page has 7,500 characters. A route with less output gets `review_route_capacity_below_floor` with
  `reason: output_floor`, and protocol v6 does not start the chain. Release 0.92.1 admitted this chain with its
  final in one request.

### The math protocol v2

- `review-assignment` chooses the protocol of a new math-solver reading chain by admission:
  `exactory-openness-reading-math-v2` first, then `exactory-openness-reading-math-v1`. A stored chain keeps its
  protocol.
- A packet that math v2 refuses takes the path of math v1.
- The final of a math v2 chain is one request. The code holds page, closing and combine prompts of math v2, and
  no command reaches them.
- `status` does not list `math_reading_v1` under `native_math`.
- When the math controller refuses the export or the recording of a successor, `review-assignment` refuses with
  `native_review_recording_blocked`. The plan gives the same stop. The details hold the code of the controller in
  `details.refusal` or `details.export_refusal`.

## Changes for the forms of release 0.92.1

### Plans name only steps that the store takes

- A plan names `review-run` for a head only when the start of this run is admitted. When the start is refused,
  `status`, `next` and `review-series` give the plan of this refusal. Release 0.92.1 named the refused run again.
- Example: when the call allowance of a store is used, the plan is the stop `review_budget_exhausted` with its
  `review-budget` step.
- Example: for a bar record whose subject is not a problem, the plan is the stop `review_prompt_mismatch`.
- Example: when the input limit is less than the request, `record-check` reports the decision stop
  `review_input_too_large` and counts the record as ready. Release 0.92.1 reported a failed run.
- A fresh staged start under a standing stop is a stop.
- After an output cap of a replacement that carries a refusal notice, the plan gives the handoff `export` with
  the code `review_slot_exhausted`. Release 0.92.1 named a `review-run` that the store refused.
- When the head of a problem openness chain has a successor chain after its export, the plan of the old head is
  `done` with `successor_assignment_id`.
- The admission stop of a v7 chain offers only the route answer when no target fits one request. Release 0.92.1
  also offered a statement with fewer neighbors.
- The plan of a head on a stale route record holds the refusal of the dispatch in `details.refusal`. The procedure
  text stays in `details.message`.

### The answers of a capacity stop

- A capacity stop that offers a probe prints only the probe. Release 0.92.1 printed the probe and then the same
  assignment. The answer "Probe this route" has a condition that names the command after the probe.
- A reassessment answer retires the route of the predecessor and probes a serving record that exists. The answer
  ends at this probe. `serving_routes` does not list reassessment rows.
- The harness prints a direct serving row only after a trial of the exact payload.
- The answer "Prepare a new entry" for a plain adjudicator appears only when the store admits its assignment.
  Release 0.92.1 named a `review-assignment` that the store refused with `review_adjudicator_fixed`.
- A stop whose request is more than a fixed character limit prints no probe answer.
- A stop at the floor of the planner offers "Probe this route" when a probe can verify the room that the floor
  needs.
- In a plan, the measure of a serving route is the value that the store calculates. On one fixture this value is
  333,240 characters, and the plan of 0.92.1 showed 333,244.
- The answer "Move to another route" can have a condition: the new route must have a different adapter, model or
  effort. The condition appears after an output cap of a recorded series of the parts protocol v3. In this case
  the store refuses a route with the same three.
- An invalid `scheduler` is refused before the assignment commits.
- A context member with the name `defer_forming` is refused with `invalid_review_context`.

### A slate

- A slate stops for a capture when it holds a forming body that its reviewer cannot read as text. This occurs
  before the first call. The slate records after the capture.
- This rule applies to a fresh slate, a large slate, a refresh and a repair of a recorded slate with a PDF body.
  It also applies when a turn adds a PDF after the first turn of a single chain.
- A chain in progress keeps the form of each body that its first turn delivered.
- A first slate whose candidates cite a work is a session of its own. Its packet holds `slate_delivery`, and its
  prompt holds the paragraph `exactory-problem-slate-delivery-v1`. A first slate without cited works keeps the
  bytes of 0.92.1.
- The refresh of an ordinary slate review is a new chain in its slot. A review of a refresh chain supersedes the
  review that it refreshes. A bar and a result review are reassessed in their session, as before.
- When the refresh does not fit its route, the plan is the capacity stop. The stop offers the probe of the route
  or the refresh on a serving route.
- The plan of a refresh does the trial of the assignment that the store does. If the store refuses the refresh,
  the plan is the stop of the store with the same answers.
- On one live record, release 0.92.1 refused the refresh of the slate review at 795,762 tokens. This release
  recorded it as a new chain of one message at 463,726 tokens, with a limit of 687,640.

### A repair

- The request of a context repair can be more than the token limit of its route and less than the character
  limit. This repair now starts a series of the parts protocol v2. Release 0.92.1 measured only the character
  limit and stopped this repair.

### The search after a slate that selects nothing

- With a current slate that selects nothing and no post, `status` and `next` give `problem_search_step_required`,
  `problem_search_step_open` or `problem_search_continued`. Release 0.92.1 gave `problem_no_post`. A record with a
  post keeps `problem_no_post`.
- The advisory `problem_report_due` is due at this slate only when the record holds a post or the search left the
  record.
- The commands `problem-search-step` and `problem-search-origin` are new.

### Codex review routes

- The three Codex transports changed. As a result, each stored Codex route record is stale, and you must record
  the route again. This includes the visual Codex route.
- Stored reviews and their visual manifests verify as before.
- With the byte measure, the first capacity probe of a Codex text route is the character limit of its transport
  (1,048,576 characters).
- A probe record holds `unverified_reasons`.
- A Codex route takes an optional `effort` from `low` to `max`. The default is `high`. The effort is a part of the
  identity of the route.
- The timeout limit of the three Codex adapters is 3,600 s. Before, it was 600 s.

### A chain of protocol v7

- A turn of a new v7 chain keeps room for the cost of its notes and for its list rows. As a result, the first
  reading turn can get 60,000 characters of material. Before, it got 120,000 on a route without a measured output
  profile. A new chain can then take more reading turns for the same material.
- The admission floor of a new v7 chain increases by these two reserves. On one test route the minimum increased
  from 127,825 to 130,273 characters.
- The prompts of a new v7 chain hold two new paragraphs, `exactory-openness-notes-cost-v1` and
  `exactory-openness-list-rows-v1`. They hold `exactory-openness-finding-units-v2` in the place of version 1.
- A chain that release 0.92.1 stored keeps its packets and its prompts.

### Refusals and notices

- An identifier URL that does not parse, for example `https://[`, is refused with `invalid_identifier`. Release
  0.92.1 raised a Python `ValueError`. Under the source rules v3, the answer of a reviewer with such a link is
  refused, and the head is replaced.
- An import of mapped records and a cohort read also refuse such a URL with `invalid_identifier`. Release 0.92.1
  gave `input_io` for it.
- A payload of an incorrect JSON type gets a refusal code of its command. Release 0.92.1 raised a Python
  `TypeError` in 13 cases.
- `review_objection_history_incomplete`, `review_work_items_incomplete` and `review_reassessment_incomplete` hold
  `details.missing_ids`. The notice of the replacement shows these ids.
- When the details of a refused entry are more than 4,000 characters, the notice keeps each detail that fits. It
  shows the other details as a preview or a digest. Release 0.92.1 showed one preview of all the details.
- A refusal of a field of a page or of a closing answer points into this answer.

### Citations of a problem post

- `export --kind problem` refuses a composed citation that the post refuses, with the code of the post. Release
  0.92.1 wrote the files. The refusal names the cited version in `details.version_id`.
- `problem-citation-metadata` and `problem-citation-locator` apply the field rule of the post when they record a
  field.
- The stop of a refused export names one of these two commands only for a work for which the command takes the
  correction. For a DOI or arXiv work, the stop gives `record-check` and `status`.

### Stores

- A write holds the write lock for less time than a replay of the history. No stored byte changes. A snapshot
  read decodes the current records in its own transaction.
- A store keeps its history when its only history marker is a carried selection of protocol v2 on an assignment
  without `reading_chain`.
- The carried selection of protocol v3 cuts and orders its entries by their creation revision. When the
  `recorded_revision` of a first assignment is not its creation revision, the selection ends with
  `claim_reassessment_required`. In the 21 live stores of the owner, no result changes.

## Measured costs

- The history proof, on a store of 4.1 GB. The write lock of a scoped write took 4.8 s to 4.9 s. In 0.92.0 it took
  80.6 s to 81.5 s. The longest read transaction of a snapshot read took 19.9 s to 20.3 s, and 94.0 s to 94.4 s
  before. The peak memory of five uses was 10.8 GB to 12.4 GB, and 15.7 GB to 16.7 GB before.
- `status`, on a fixture with 32 heads: 4,384 rows read and 0.147 s of processor time. Release 0.92.1 read 8,352
  rows in 0.110 s. The plan now does the admission of each run that it names.
- One plan of the final head of a v7 chain: 28 reads of the artifact store and 35 ms to 38 ms. Release 0.92.1
  used 15 reads and 10.9 ms.
- One plan of a continuing head of a series of the parts protocol v3: 0.635 s on a fixture of 22 targets. The
  plan prepares the next turn one more time.
- One plan of a head of a session of the rules v4: approximately 0.25 s for each completed turn of a large result
  chain.
- A terminal plan of a math v2 chain does a build of the controller: 0.24 s, 1.46 s and 3.40 s with 0, 64 and 128
  searches in the common store. Larger stores were not measured.
- One refused slate assignment on a record of 279 MB with three verified routes took 26.7 s. It took 10.7 s in
  0.92.0, which did no trial.
- The plan of a refused refresh takes approximately 1 s on a fixture with two routes. Before, it took 0.08 s.

## Checks of the selection

- Six independent reviews examined the merged tree. Each review gave one verdict for the merge and one for the
  selection of a form. The release holds the repairs that these reviews made necessary.
- New chains and stored chains of the session rules v3, the parts protocol v2 and protocol v7 gave the packet and
  prompt digests of 0.92.1.
- With the four forms selected, chains ran on copies of live records. A reviewer stub answered from the prompt of
  each request only. Each chain recorded its review, and each request was less than the limits of its route.
- These runs did not show three cases on a live copy. Fixtures showed them:
  - a final in pages for a bar
  - a stored series of the parts protocol v2 with an admitted next turn
  - a stored math v1 chain

## Known and left

- For a session in progress, `next` and `status` can name a next turn that the store refuses for its size.
  `review-series` ends at a recorded stop with its answers. Release 0.92.1 has the same defect.
- In a read turn of the parts protocol v2, more units than the card allowance holds give no valid answer. The
  turn uses three calls before the handoff. Release 0.92.1 has the same defect.
- A turn of a series of the parts protocol v3 does not hold the paragraph `exactory-problem-areas-v1`. A result
  review of a record with more areas does its v3 final without the rule of this paragraph.
- The packet member `slate_delivery` has no fixed bound. The harness measures the request that holds it.
- A refresh that the store refuses for a cause other than capacity still gets a plan that names the refresh.
- The store takes a second registration of a route with the same identity. This is how you renew a stale route
  record.
- The reason text of `controls` in a capacity plan names model settings that the controls do not hold.
- `plan_next` names a series of the parts protocol v3 `parts_v2:<adapter>`.
- The measurement tool `tools/review_measurement/run_pair` uses the session rules v3 and the parts protocol v2 for
  its pairs. Its measurement compares one request with a series of the parts protocol v2.
