# Exactory 0.71.2

This patch lets a full-text job finish for a work that is not an arXiv version, for example a DOI work whose PDF
comes from its publisher. It also corrects seven texts of 0.71.0 that agents read.

## Why

A full-text job of 0.71.0 (`fulltext-start`, `fulltext-resume`) required the final URL of its transfer to normalize
to the job's identifier, for every work. A publisher URL, and a URL with a query or a fragment, does not normalize, so
the job of a DOI work stayed pending with `invalid_identifier` after its transfer, and a retained original that the
`fulltext` command had captured for such a work could not start a job. In practice the jobs of 0.71.0 worked for
arXiv versions only. The `fulltext` command checks the URL identity only for arXiv captures.

A GPT 6 Astra max review of the texts of 0.71.0 found this defect and six text errors. The supervisor checked each
finding against the code and accepted all seven.

## What changed

- A full-text job follows the identity rule of `fulltext`. For an arXiv identifier, the final URL must normalize to
  the exact requested version (`version_mismatch` otherwise), and the source records `observed_identifier`. For any
  other identifier, the final URL is not an identity, `observed_identifier` stays unset, and the job's source and
  assembly bind the requested identifier and its version. A retained source is accepted by its requested identifier,
  and for arXiv also by its observed exact version.
- The texts that agents read are corrected:
  - After `review_source_no_progress`, the texts no longer tell the agent to record the reviewer's output, which
    `value-review` refuses while source requests stay pending (`review_sources_pending`). The agent changes the source
    state and continues, or reports the source gap.
  - Recovery by repeating a `review-run`, `review-probe` or `review-output-correction` needs a committed
    `review_call_observation`. A process that stopped after the answer but before the capture leaves an uncaptured
    pending invocation, which follows the existing reconciliation.
  - A job extracts PDF originals only; an HTML or XML original stays with `fulltext` and its limits.
  - `unit_seconds` limits extraction work; a transfer uses what remains of `invocation_seconds`.
  - A completed range transfer records a `source_assembly` while the extraction can still be pending; only a
    completed extraction adds the readable capture to the work.
  - The verify skill bounds a native search by the effective `hits_per_query`, 10 by default, not by a fixed ten.

Assemblies that 0.71.0 recorded for arXiv versions validate unchanged.

## Upgrade

No action is necessary. A job of a non-arXiv work that 0.71.0 left pending after its transfer can be resumed with
`fulltext-resume`.

## Checks

New tests in `tests/test_research_fulltext_jobs.py` cover a DOI job with a publisher URL and with a URL that carries a
query, a DOI range transfer and its assembly, a job on a retained DOI original, and the unchanged arXiv checks (another
version and a tampered assembly are still refused); the three DOI tests failed before the change. The nine
acquisition, full-text, source and continuation modules (139 tests) pass on Python 3.12.8 and 3.9.6, with the
documentation, manifest and version tests at 0.71.2, `codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check`.
