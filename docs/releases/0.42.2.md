# Exactory 0.42.2

This release settles what the review of 0.42.1 left open. It changes no stored
record: the research constitution stays at version 5, and no study revalidates.

## `next` names the Grand Challenge record before the objective

Since 0.38 `next` named `objective_missing` beside the roots. 0.42.0 asked for
the objective to be written against the Grand Challenge record, whose evidence
is a source read in full, so the two orders disagreed: an agent that followed
`next` fixed the objective first, and a research objective cannot be replaced
once set (`objective_locked`). `next` now names the record after the searches
and the objective directly after the record, before the synthesis sections:
roots, critical full text, bundles and units, searches, Tier 3 abstracts, the
Grand Challenge record, the objective, synthesis. The preparation gate owes the
same obligations as before; only their order in `next` changed.

## Only an approved decision binds a later goal

A goal could not repeat a candidate that any earlier round decision rejected,
including a decision the independent review did not approve. The reviewer may
have objected to the rejection itself, and the author then had no honest way to
pursue that candidate. `round_goal_repeated` now reads the rejected candidates
of the decisions an independent review approved; a decision that was not
approved binds nothing. A rejected candidate of an approved decision still never
becomes a goal, with or without a reopening, and a step that is not pursued is
still `deferred` unless the evidence rejects it.

## Skills

The study workspace, cohort and math-solver skills name the Grand Challenge
record among what the preparation records. The cohort doctrine's open problems
and their advance criteria are the first input to the record. Native
mathematics keeps its requirement: `export --kind native` needs complete
research preparation, which includes the record since 0.42.0.

## Upgrade

Nothing to do. A study prepared under 0.42.1 owes the same obligations; when
both the record and the objective are missing, `next` names the record first.
