# Exactory 0.66.3

This release bounds the final turn of an openness reading chain. The records
that the openness packet carries as evidence arrive as reading units after the
full texts, the final turn withholds what earlier turns delivered until its
request fits the route, and a refusal names the size of each part. A packet
whose units exceed the reading budget of one turn becomes a chain even when
the whole packet fits the route. The change was planned as 0.66.1; 0.66.2
(lower peak memory of history validation, CI push runs only on `main`) merged
first, so it ships as 0.66.3. The work is recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261027085554-bar-source-requests-exceed-route-capacity`
of the exactory repository (task T11, design
`codex-outputs/20261028T223853-astra-design-t11.md`). The single-turn
condition repairs defect D3, found by the session that verified 0.66.0.

## Why

Under 0.65.2 the final turn of an openness chain had no bound. On 2026-10-28,
in the KMP problem record (route `codex_cli_v1`, gpt-6-astra, input limit
1,048,576 characters), the chain `assign-openness-astra-6` read the eight full
texts of its readings (291,855; 195,429; 63,236; 304,336; 101,335; 243,326;
185,115 and 213,686 characters) in 17 reading turns, every one completed and
input-verified. The assignment of the final turn was then refused with
`review_input_too_large` at 1,284,492 input characters. The earlier chain
`assign-openness-astra-5` on the same record had been refused at 1,296,387.

A read-only rebuild of the refused request showed where the characters were.
Every full-text body was already withheld by `text_priority`. The native
packet without full texts was about 480,000 characters: 37,512 for the native
part, and about 431,000 for the 17 record artifacts that the export includes
as evidence (the eight bound search records with their dispositions, the
eight reading records of the store and the statement record, 8,418 to 84,505
characters each). The reading record of the chain was 724,489 characters: the
notes of 17 turns, 230,004; 341 excerpts (339 verified), 385,421, of which the
excerpt texts were 198,944 (allowance used 198,332 of 200,000) and the rest
the artifact descriptor and locator that every excerpt repeated; and 13
passages, 108,862, of which the texts were 98,350 (allowance used 98,350 of
100,000). No reading turn delivered the records, so the final turn was the
first turn to carry them, and the reading record grew with every turn. No
export of that record could fit a Codex route under 0.65.2. The record
estimated that delivering the records in reading turns alone would leave a
final turn of about 0.86 to 0.95 million characters, still growing with every
turn, so the reading record is bounded as well.

The verification of 0.66.0 found defect D3 in the single-turn path:
`prepare_packet` kept a packet as a single turn whenever its request fit the
route. A packet with one source of 120,001 characters stayed a single turn,
and a three-source packet sent 397,000 characters of new full text in one
turn of 420,076 characters, against the 0.65.2 rule that a turn delivers at
most 120,000 characters of new full text. The reason for that rule is the
600-second limit of one turn.

## Measured

On a copy of the KMP record's store with the repaired 0.65.2 code, the chain
`assign-openness-astra-6` continued after its 17 recorded turns with four
reading turns of the 17 records (requests of 800,482; 829,527; 838,571 and
782,593 characters) and reached a final turn of 981,364 characters, within
the limit of 1,048,576, after withholding 17 bodies (base packet 298,186,
notes 281,482, excerpts 295,645, passages 106,051). The 17 recorded turns were
checked as recorded. The answers of the four new turns were synthetic, 13,500
characters of notes each and no excerpts, and that simulation accepted their
provenance, so the sizes of the live chain are not yet measured. The
KMP-shaped test reaches a final turn of 1,017,797 characters (base packet
339,310, notes 297,374, excerpts 279,016, passages 102,097).

## Review before the merge

An independent review of the code commit found that every carried main
capture of a bound version became a reading unit, including the extraction
that the packet's `openness_delivery` record omits as an alternate. The route
projection blanks that body in every request that carries the native packet,
so a small packet with an alternate never stayed a single turn, every chain
read the alternate in full, and an excerpt that named the version without
`text_sha256` came back `unmatched` as if no text were stored. The pull
request fixed this before the merge: an omitted alternate is not a unit, an
identity that names more than one bound body is reported as
`ambiguous_identity`, and a part of a captured response is saved as plain
text. The three new tests fail on the previous commit and pass now.

## Reading units of three kinds

- A reading unit has a kind and an identity. A `fulltext` unit is named by
  `version_id`, a `search_response` unit by `source_id`, and a `record` unit
  by `record_kind` (`literature_search`, `reading` or `problem_statement`) and
  `record_id`. A full text or a captured response can add `text_sha256`, the
  SHA-256 of its whole stored body. A plan entry without `kind`, from a chain
  of 0.64.2 to 0.66.2, is a full text. A unit above 120,000 characters is
  divided into parts by the unchanged rule of 0.65.2; a part of a record or of
  a captured response is saved as plain text (`text/plain; charset=utf-8`),
  because a fragment of a JSON body is not JSON, and a whole record keeps its
  reference and media type.
- A problem-openness chain delivers the full texts of the readings first, in
  `text_delivery_priority` order and then by version id and text digest, and
  after them the packet's own records: the bound search records in
  `minimum.searches` order, each once, the reading records by record id, and
  the statement record. The full texts are the extracted texts of the main
  full-text captures of each bound reading's version that the exported packet
  carries as evidence, one unit per version and text digest; a capture or
  reading recorded after the export does not enlarge the chain. When the
  packet binds an `openness_delivery` record (an export with
  `native_reviews`), the alternate main bodies that the record omits
  (`scientific_context.openness_delivery.alternate_body_digests`) are not
  units; the selected representation of each version is read. The design
  also required the capture that the reading's source bundle names; that
  condition was dropped, because a reading need not name a bundle and the
  evidence of the export already fixes the captures. A record arrives as the
  exact JSON text of the artifact that the packet carries;
  `problem_openness.serialize_record` gives those bytes, unchanged from the
  export. The `search_response` kind is accepted by the splitter, the checks
  and the chain verification, for the math-solver chains that another lane
  builds; a problem-openness chain delivers none.
- An excerpt or a passage request names its unit by one identity form, with
  `text_sha256` optional on a full text or a captured response; mixed identity
  fields are refused. The harness checks it against the exact bytes of the
  bound unit, with the matching and the allowances of 0.64.2 unchanged:
  per-character NFKC, collapsed white space, first match, 20 to 6,000
  characters per excerpt, 20,000 per passage, 200,000 and 100,000 per chain. A
  record is matched on its JSON text, escapes included. A `text_sha256` that
  differs from the bound body, or an identity that names no bound unit, gives
  `unmatched` or `not_found` with the reason `no_stored_text`; an identity
  without `text_sha256` that names more than one bound body gives them with
  the reason `ambiguous_identity`.

## The bounded final turn

- The final turn starts with every body and every note, measures the exact
  request that the route sends, and withholds in this order, one item at a
  time, until the request fits: the full texts that reading turns delivered
  complete, from the end of `text_delivery_priority`; the other full texts
  delivered complete, largest first; captured responses delivered complete,
  largest first; reading records delivered complete, largest first; search
  records delivered complete, largest first; and then the notes of the earlier
  turns, oldest first. Only the actual authenticated requests of the reading
  turns decide what counts as delivered. Protected bodies, the statement and
  its record, the formulations, the excerpts, the passages, the source
  descriptors and the allowances are never withheld.
- A withheld body keeps its artifact reference and encoding, with
  `content: ""`, `delivery: "body-delivered-in-reading-turns"` and a note that
  names the delivering turns. Withheld notes become `notes_withheld`, a
  sentence naming the turn; the observed outputs keep the notes, and every
  later assignment starts from them again.
- The reading record is compact: `{protocol, turns, sources, excerpts,
  passages, allowances}`, with one descriptor per cited artifact in `sources`
  and, per excerpt or passage, its identity, offsets, span digest, status,
  text and purpose. Reading turns use the same record and withhold the
  earliest notes in the same way when their request does not fit; they never
  withhold a newly delivered unit.
- When even the reduced request does not fit, `review-assignment` refuses
  with `review_input_too_large` before it records the assignment, with
  `input_characters`, `input_limit_characters` and `parts: {base_packet,
  notes, excerpts, passages}`. The four parts add up to the input characters,
  and the message names them. A new export does not reduce them.

## Single-turn condition

An openness packet stays a single turn only when its fitted request holds
every unit of the packet, the full texts and the records, complete within the
route's input limit, and those units together are at most 120,000 characters.
Otherwise it becomes a reading chain. This closes defect D3.

## Recording

`problem-openness` accepts the final turn of a chain when every unit of the
packet was delivered complete in the actual requests of the reading turns,
whole or in all of its parts, or whole in the final turn's own request. A part
counts as delivered when a request carried, complete, the exact bytes of that
part of the unit's whole body as the part rule cuts it. An excerpt, a passage,
a descriptor, a withheld body or a planned part is not a delivery. The
record's `reading_turns` lists every turn of the chain, the final turn
included, as `{assignment_id, attempt_id, delivered_units, delivered_parts?}`;
an earlier final turn that returned passage requests lists the units whose
bodies its own request carried complete, but only the reading turns and the
terminal final turn's own request count as deliveries. A chain of 0.64.2 to
0.66.2 keeps the older entries `{assignment_id, attempt_id, delivered,
delivered_parts?}`. `openness_reading_incomplete` names the missing
units by kind and identity in `details.units`, with `missing_parts` for a unit
delivered in parts, and still names missing full texts in
`details.version_ids`.

## Prompts

The reading and final prompts `exactory-openness-reading-v3` state the three
unit kinds and their identity fields, that records arrive as exact JSON text,
that a later turn can withhold the notes of the earliest turns and the final
turn the bodies delivered in reading turns, that an excerpt or a passage is
never withheld, and therefore that the reviewer keeps what the judgment needs
as excerpts and in its latest notes. The v1 and v2 prompts keep their exact
bytes and canonical hashes, so recorded assignments stay authenticated.

## Interface changes

No public function of `openness_reading` or `review_excerpts` was renamed.

- `review_excerpts`: `check_excerpts(records, artifacts, excerpts, allowances,
  *, unit_sources=None)` and `answer_passages(records, artifacts, requests,
  allowances, *, unit_sources=None)` take the chain's catalog of bound unit
  sources; `None` keeps the version-only lookup of bar, slate and result
  sessions. `validate_excerpts(values, code, kinds=None)` and
  `validate_passage_request(request, code, kinds=None)` take the identity
  kinds of a protocol; v1 and v2 responses keep their version-only validation.
  `save_part` takes an optional `media_type`. New `UNIT_KINDS`,
  `IDENTITY_FIELDS`, `RECORD_KINDS`, `find_kind`, `find_identity`,
  `find_identity_fields` and `find_source_reference`. Matching, the
  allowances, `find_source_text` and `find_complete_bodies` are unchanged.
- `openness_reading`: `PROTOCOL` is now `exactory-openness-reading-v3`, and
  `READING_PROMPT` and `FINAL_PROMPT` hold the v3 texts; `PROMPTS` adds them,
  and the v1 and v2 constants stay (`PROTOCOL_V2`, `READING_PROMPT_V2` and
  `FINAL_PROMPT_V2` are new names for the 0.65.2 texts). `list_reading_texts`
  emits typed rows; new `list_record_rows(artifacts, native)` and
  `list_reading_rows(records, artifacts, native)`;
  `list_reading_units(artifacts, rows)` takes rows of any kind.
  `prepare_packet` and `verify_chain` keep their signatures. `verify_chain`
  dispatches by the final packet's recorded protocol, and the v1 and v2
  verifier keeps its exact `reading_turns` shape. `validate_turn_output`
  validates by the recorded protocol. Plan entries add `kind` and the identity
  fields; entries outside `delivered_now` carry `artifact_descriptor`. The
  refusal of an oversized final turn no longer tells the operator to export
  the packet again with `minimum.text_priority`: its message says that the
  final turn, or the reading turn, has that many input characters after it
  withholds every body and note that it may withhold, above the route input
  limit, and names the base packet, the notes, the excerpts and the passages.
- `problem_openness`: new `serialize_record(record)`, the bytes of an exported
  record.

## Compatibility

- The five reviewer transports (`review_transport.py`,
  `review_codex_transport.py`, `review_codex_framed_transport.py`,
  `review_codex_visual_transport.py` and `review_claude_transport.py`) are
  unchanged against 0.66.2, so registered routes stay valid.
- The v1 and v2 reading and final prompts are unchanged, so assignments
  recorded with them keep authenticating. Records, assignments and attempts of
  0.60.0 to 0.66.2 stay readable, authenticated and current. A finding recorded
  from a v1 or v2 chain stays current: its chain is verified under its own
  protocol and gives the same `reading_turns` entries.
- A chain that 0.64.2 to 0.66.2 started continues under the new rules from
  its next assignment, with its native packet, first assignment id, route,
  reviewer, author, recorded turns and observed attempts unchanged. The
  previous answer is validated under its own protocol, and its excerpts and
  passages are checked by version id as before; the carried record becomes
  the compact form without charging them again. The harness does not keep the
  earlier plan: at every continuation it computes what remains from the actual
  authenticated requests of the chain's reading turns and delivers next, in
  the order of the units, every unit that no reading turn delivered complete,
  whole or in the parts that no reading turn delivered. The 17 turns of
  `assign-openness-astra-6` therefore stay valid: its next assignments read
  the records, and the final turn follows. A final turn that returned the
  native result still ends its chain.
- The KMP record continues its chain on a local copy of 0.65.2 (`448b6da`,
  branch `record/kmp-0.65.2-final-turn`) with the repair commits, because its
  routes are pinned to the transports of 0.65.2 and 0.66.0 changed the bytes
  of the three Codex transports. The extremal and Sidorenko records ran
  repaired copies of 0.56.0 in the same way. The copy writes the same v3
  prompts and protocol as this release, so later releases authenticate what
  it records; its runtime names `plugin_version` 0.65.2 with the copy's
  source commit. Every commit before the release commit applies unchanged on
  `448b6da`.

## Choices for the owner

- The withholding order of the final turn: delivered full texts from the end
  of `text_delivery_priority`, then other delivered full texts, captured
  responses, reading records and search records, largest first within a kind,
  and then the notes, oldest first. Equal sizes go by identity and then by
  artifact SHA-256.
- Excerpts, passages and the allowances are unchanged (200,000 and 100,000
  characters per chain), and they are never withheld. A chain whose
  irreducible parts (protected bodies, the statement, unmatched excerpt
  copies, purposes, excerpts and passages) exceed the limit is refused with
  the four sizes, not truncated.
- Every note can be withheld, the newest last. The reviewer's continuity is
  its checked excerpts, its latest notes and bounded passages; the original
  notes stay in the observed outputs.
- The packet's records arrive after the full texts, in the order searches,
  readings, statement, and are matched on their JSON text with escapes, not on
  decoded field values.
- The full-text units of a chain are the main full-text captures that the
  export carries as evidence for each bound reading's version, without the
  design's condition on the reading's source bundle.
- A continued chain of 0.64.2 to 0.66.2 keeps no earlier plan: what remains is
  computed at every continuation from the actual requests of its reading
  turns, in the order of the units, full texts first.
- The single-turn condition counts the full texts and the records of the
  packet together against the reading budget of 120,000 characters, next to
  the route fit of every unit.
- A binary captured response needs an explicit readable representation before
  it can be a text unit.
