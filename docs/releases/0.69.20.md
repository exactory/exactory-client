# Exactory 0.69.20

This patch lets a problem `result` slot continue after several refreshes. When the part planner refuses the next turn
with `review_input_too_large`, the harness replaces the reviewer's replayed outputs of completed earlier series with
short placeholders and plans the turn again.

## Why

A result slot replays its whole session on every turn: the user packets are reduced to stubs, but every assistant
output stays verbatim. Each refresh of a problem with evidence in parts adds the part notes and the final review of
one more series. On a math-conjecturer record (route `claude_cli_v1`, input limit 1,300,000 characters), the fourth
series of one slot delivered four part turns, and the planning of its final turn failed:

```text
review_input_too_large {"stage": "final", "turn": 5, "input_characters": 1472433, "input_limit_characters": 1300000,
 "frame_characters": 1472433, "unit": null}
```

The replayed history held 199,472 characters of part notes from three earlier, recorded series, and 72,006 characters
of two earlier final reviews that a later review supersedes.

## What changed

- A new module, `review_problem_history.py`, runs when `review-assignment` records a problem `result` assignment on
  `claude_cli_v1`, `codex_cli_v1` or `codex_cli_framed_v1` with a history parent. It runs the unchanged part planner
  first. Only if the planner refuses with `review_input_too_large`, it maps each history message to its assignment by
  lineage. Then it replaces the part outputs of each earlier series of the same slot that closed with an
  authenticated recorded review, and that series' final output when a later recorded result exists. A series
  qualifies only when its review is delivered in the packet's `historical_reviews`.
- The bar session, the latest recorded result, the current series, every series without a recorded review and all
  user messages stay as they are. A placeholder replaces an output only when it is shorter.
- The planner then runs again with the reduced history. If it still refuses, nothing is recorded.
- The placeholder is a JSON object with `protocol: "exactory-problem-result-history-v1"`, the assignment, series,
  turn, stage, output digest and review ids, and a short note to the reviewer. Astra 6 at max reviewed the note text.
- `docs/research-cli.md` describes the rule in "Problem evidence in parts".

## Upgrade

- A request that fits is built as before, byte for byte. Recorded assignments, invocations, attempts and reviews do
  not change, and their requests rebuild from their stored history.
- No route-pinned module changes: `review_transport.py`, `review_claude_transport.py`, the Codex transports and the
  cost modules are byte-identical to 0.69.18, so registered routes stay current.
- Excerpt ledgers, reading and passage allowances, full-text delivery accounting and the coverage check of the post
  gate do not change.
- A slot that stopped with this refusal continues with a normal `review-assignment` from its latest assignment on the
  same route.

## Limits

The rule removes the replayed outputs of closed series, but it does not bound the whole request. The packet's
`historical_reviews`, the material objections and the current series' notes still grow with each refresh.

## Checks

The new module `test_problem_result_history` (22 tests) reproduced the refusal at 1,472,433 characters before the
change. After the change, it plans the final turn of that shape at 1,207,255 characters. On a copy of the affected
record, the final turn of the slot plans at 1,200,926 characters within the limit of 1,300,000. With
`PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`, 434 tests in 28 modules passed on Python 3.12.8 and 3.9.6. They include the parts,
continued-parts, earlier-series delivery (0.69.19), excerpt, review-protocol, objection-identity, output-view,
check-mode audit, manifest and guidance modules. `codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` pass. The
route-pinned modules are byte-identical to 0.69.18. The full suite runs in CI on the pull request.
