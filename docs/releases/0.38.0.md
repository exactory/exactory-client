# Exactory 0.38.0

This release makes literature preparation proportionate to its scientific value
without lowering the standard of evidence. It keeps the shared research
foundation of 0.36.0 and 0.37.0, the three-review manuscript measurement, the
two-review publication gate, the five search purposes, and the five to ten
external innovation cases. The design is recorded in
[the literature efficiency design](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-11-literature-efficiency-design.md).

## Research workflow

- `status --summary` and `next --summary` return bounded advisory views (16 KiB
  and 4 KiB) of the same current evaluation; `obligations --code CODE` pages one
  obligation code bound to the revision. `next` follows the preparation order.
  The default `status` and `next` output is unchanged.
- One evaluation per command: a store replays its history once per use, and one
  `Evaluation` verifies each object once and computes the citation graph, the
  cohort report, the foundation and the synthesis once per snapshot. On the
  audited 2,505-family study, `status` fell from 78.0 s to 20.0 s wall and from
  68.2 s to 14.8 s CPU with identical obligations and digests.
- A `span` locator names saved text by offsets and the hash of the exact
  substring; whole-document quotes are no longer written. Legacy `text` locators
  stay valid and share one identity with spans.
- A full-text reading depends on its bundle's units, not on parsed bibliography
  entries; adding references or resolutions no longer forces a re-reading.
- Every full-text capture records its extractor, version, layout option and the
  measures of the extracted text; `fulltext` accepts `extraction_options`.
- `read-batch` records up to 100 abstract readings in one event, deriving the
  whole-abstract inspection itself; `batches` exports the unread abstracts as
  reader files. A batch of 60 replaces 60 single mutations.
- Cohort collection completes by entries, retains window and category
  exclusions with a reason, keeps its cursor on a repeated page, and honors the
  persisted Retry-After deadline in every acquisition.
- Searches judge every found work with a disposition, carry contradictory and
  unresolved findings forward, and depend on their scope, the works they rest
  on and the citation frontier; an unrelated reference's new version no longer
  invalidates them.
- Synthesis sections bind the dependencies they use (population for standards;
  frontier and judgments for rationale, innovation and context); plans and
  candidates bind a preparation digest, and literature comparisons a literature
  digest. An unrelated reading no longer stales every section and plan.
- Reviewer packets are neutral: no plan, author list (at any depth of the
  readiness packet), revision label, request identity, launcher token or
  assessment history reaches a reviewer; a second review by the same assessor
  of the same exact bundle is refused, and for reviews recorded before that
  rule the assessor's latest stands.
- `budget` records resource limits with a reason; an acquisition is admitted
  only with room for its request allowance (at least one request) beside the
  allowances of the acquisitions still admitted, finish charges the requests
  and bytes used, a new request id for the same operation and target
  supersedes an interrupted predecessor and releases its allowance, batches
  charge their counts and reported model usage, charged plus reserved at a
  limit is an obligation, and `status --summary` shows every account.
- The preparation policy is recorded at initialization: `exhaustive-v1` by
  default, or `screened-v1` with `exactory-lab init --preparation-policy`.
  Under `screened-v1`, `screen-batch` records dispositions, the cohort gate
  requires readings for promoted, doctrine and pending members, an audited
  sample of excluded members (a strong audit reopens every excluded member in
  a new round; rounds only rise), and doctrine representatives per month;
  `screening-checkpoint` records saturation after two batches whose every
  item was judged not consequential; `policy-report` describes the
  preparation set and its recall against an adjudicated reference.
- `RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION.md` is Version 2 with a population and preparation
  policy section.
- Every receipt and admission records the running build (version, commit,
  dirty flag, package digest, constitution digest); `status` reports it as
  `runtime`, and provider requests carry the plugin version.

## Reliability corrections

- Record tampering is caught at first use; unsupported schemas, dropped guards,
  bad headers and hot journals are still refused at construction.
- Two versions of one family in a listing no longer loop a partition; the dead
  `invalid_alias` restart is gone.
- Cohort abstract credit through a full-text reading compares the located
  substring, so span and text abstract units both discharge a member.

## Distribution and validation

The shared `bin/`, `skills/` and `hooks/` serve Claude Code and Codex; the Codex
entrypoints are regenerated. New tests cover provenance, the evaluation context,
report views, span locators, reading freshness, extraction diagnostics, batches,
collection completeness, search dispositions, section dependencies, review
packets, resources, the preparation policy, and screening. Measurements and the
full-suite result are in [the testing record](../testing/literature-efficiency.md).

## Upgrade and resume

An existing study keeps every reading, search, synthesis and review record. On
first use under 0.38.0 it reports:

- `constitution_revalidation_required`: run `constitution` with the previous
  SHA-256 to adopt Version 2.
- `search_dispositions_missing`, `search_evidence_stale` and
  `search_frontier_stale` for each selected search: re-record each purpose with
  dispositions, reusing the captured responses.
- `synthesis_dependencies_stale` for each section: re-record the four sections
  with the same payloads under new ids.
- A plan recorded under 0.37.0 fails `admit` and `launch` with
  `literature_comparison_stale`; plan again with `literature_digest` in its
  literature comparison. An assessment whose novelty comparison carries the
  old `foundation_digest` reports `novelty_comparison_stale`.
- A native mathematical foundation exported under 0.37.0 needs a fresh export
  and a reviewed `search amend-foundation` before fresh work.

Read `status --summary` and `next --summary`, then the `obligations` page for
the code in hand. An upgrade does not resume paused work, reset a budget, or
turn a historical receipt into current readiness.
