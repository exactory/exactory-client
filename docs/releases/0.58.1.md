# Exactory 0.58.1

This release repairs one defect of 0.58.0 in the coverage check of problem
result reviews. The defect appeared in the Sidorenko girth-six problem record
on 2026-10-22, when that record changed from its local runtime to 0.58.0
(`misc/harness-improvement/20261022095233-coverage-rejects-repaired-bar-history`
of the exactory repository). The record kinds, the prompts and the packet
shapes of 0.58.0 are unchanged.

## A result in a bar slot that a context repair continued

- 0.58.0 counts the coverage of a problem result against its actual request.
  Each packet of that request must match an assignment in the bound history
  of the review: the same digest, role, reviewer, dossier and subject.
- A context repair copies the packet of the bar that it reassesses, with the
  subject of that packet. A source continuation of a bar does the same. When
  the author recorded the statement after the bar, the new assignment names
  the statement and its packet does not.
- The exact comparison of the two subjects refused the whole request of the
  result that continues the repaired slot. Every part of the problem
  evidence then counted as unassessed (`problem_evidence_unassessed`), and
  the post gate stopped.
- The same check also selects the packets for the audit of cited evidence in
  a result on the visual route (`codex_cli_visual_v1`). For a result in a
  repaired bar slot, that audit also received no packet.
- The check now compares the subject with the rule of the context repair.
  Two bar subjects match when both are problem subjects. Every other role
  keeps the exact comparison, so a result packet must still name the exact
  statement. The repair applies to both uses of the check.
- Recorded packets and assignments are unchanged. On the Sidorenko record,
  all 37 completed review attempts still authenticate.

## Known limitation

When a problem packet is larger than the text route limit, the route
withholds the bodies of captured search responses. The coverage check counts
each withheld body as unassessed. On the Sidorenko record, this finding stays for both result
reviews (11 bodies and 20 bodies). This release does not change that rule.

## Tests

- `tests/test_problem_result_contract.py` has two new tests. In the first
  test, the result of a bar slot that a context repair continued after the
  statement keeps its actual input. In the second test, the ancestry check
  holds a bar packet to the field and a result packet to its exact
  statement. Both tests fail on 0.58.0.
- The runs of the release are in `docs/testing/math-conjecturer-harness.md`,
  section "Release 0.58.1".
