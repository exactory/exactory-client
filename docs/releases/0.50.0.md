# Exactory 0.50.0

This release adds scientific contribution decisions to managed AI Science
studies and drafts. A supported claim no longer suffices by itself to justify
extended work toward a research objective. Authors compare the consequence of
alternative objectives and approaches before commitment, then reassess the
actual consequence after results. Scientific efficacy is not established.

## Scientific decisions

An immutable intent preserves the complete objective, actual user instruction,
resource authority and any publication endpoint or quality condition. A
comparative dossier records alternatives, prior failures, transfer assumptions,
deciding tests and a bounded research question. Two independent assessors first
state a worthwhile consequence without the author slate, then assess that slate
in their own recorded contexts. Result assessments combine native scientific
support and realized consequence without merging those judgments.

One canonical decision governs each phase. Target commitment consumes the exact
approved objective and source dependencies. Cycle plans and admissions bind the
approved question, method and resource tranche. Writing consumes a result
decision; publication consumes the exact post-measurement decision. Existing
round commands adapt to the same records and retain the seven continue
assurances and two stop assurances.

Stable work items distinguish adoption from an actual attempt, result,
obstruction or resolution. New identifiers do not renew an exhausted research
question. Material source changes and later reviewer-context exposure invalidate
affected current approval. Repeated acquisition metadata alone does not require
repeating a scientifically unchanged assessment.

The workflow introduces no numerical contribution threshold, majority rule for
scientific objections or blanket prohibition on restricted models. An
unconditional requested delivery remains authorized when scientifically
supported; its unmet complete objective remains open. An explicit quality
condition remains binding. Source-limited manuscripts retain their exact scope,
scientific delivery projections and remaining obligations.

## Independent review

Canonical role packets, prompts, assignment identities, observed requests,
responses, failures and usage are retained. Imported reviewer prose can remain
scientific evidence, but does not establish an independently observed context.
New manuscript reviews, paired predictions and managed external verdicts bind
their unchanged actual outputs to verified assignments. Later contamination
blocks current approval without deleting the historical finding.

Strategic review packets expose a complete immutable inventory through bounded
pages and exact source requests. Initial context can include previously inspected
passages with their original provenance. A reviewer can request inventory pages,
literal title or identity queries, and exact held abstracts or full texts. The
same observed session receives the requested bytes, and unavailable or partial
sources remain pending. Required figures, tables, equations and supplementary
material retain their native completeness checks. Receiving a source does not
create reading credit or scientific approval.

A completed review slot remains fixed after later context contamination.
Explicit reassessment requires the recorded exposure and a later observed
isolation probe. It preserves the original scientific packet, adverse findings
and objections, and requires the new observed assessment to address inherited
findings. Its history is labeled as reassessment rather than a new blind review.
Ordinary source continuations cannot resample a completed scientific finding.

The direct Responses API adapter sends an explicit stateless evidence packet.
The optional Codex CLI adapter uses subscription authentication, disables
automatic project context and tools, and audits its actual rollout against the
tested native declaration signatures. A route must pass an allowed-evidence
positive control and current input inspection. A fresh-session label or absence
of a canary in the answer is insufficient. Changed or unknown native context
remains unverified.

The Codex adapter currently accepts text evidence and refuses binary files. Its
wall-time limit terminates the invocation's owned process group, including
native descendants, with a one-second graceful termination interval before
forced termination. Its output-token bound
is checked after generation, not advertised as a provider-enforced hard cap.
Provider training knowledge, internal service configuration and model-family
independence are not established by these checks.

## Upgrade behavior

The common store remains revisioned, immutable and replayable. Historical records
remain readable. Active legacy managed work needs the current intent and
decisions at new commitment boundaries. Previously admitted native execution
can retain its observed outcome without fabricated strategic history. Bare
shared preparation used by native mathematics, standalone paper evaluation and
external verification do not acquire strategic research-decision prerequisites.
Their independent reviews use the shared context contract.

Read the [decision workflow](../research-decisions.md),
[CLI contracts](../research-cli.md) and executable JSON examples before adopting
an active study. Both hosts use generated entrypoints from the same canonical
skills. The earlier 0.49.0 launcher and evidence-validation repairs remain.

## Validation and limits

The validation program separates software correctness, historical reconstruction
and operational feasibility. Required software checks include the complete Python
matrix, native mathematical harness and Lean fixture, generated entrypoints and
independent review. The published release record reports the exact validated
commit, operational pilot outcomes and remaining limitations.

The historical reconstruction retains ten paired decisions, 40 scientific calls
and four controls. Actual replay usage was 977,517 tokens. One case exceeded its
allowance; another required a prospective output-limit amendment before its two
first decision calls. Original failures, source gaps and all outputs remain
recorded. Curation used additional resources and is reported separately.

Bounded operational pilots retain every initiated task and failed call. They
measure executable transitions, evidence integrity and process cost, and cannot
establish general scientific efficacy. The separate prospective efficacy trial
has not been performed. This scientific decision workflow remains experimental.
