# Exactory 0.69.19

This patch lets a problem result review in a restarted part series cite records that earlier part series of the same
review slot actually delivered. Before, such a final review could not be recorded, and its slot could not continue.

## Why

A problem result review that does not fit one input arrives in part turns. When the problem evidence changes during
a series, the next turn starts a new part series on the new evidence, in the same reviewer session. The check that a
final review cites only content its reviewer received counted the packets of the current series only. In the actual
request, the packets of earlier turns are elided. So a final that cited a reading which an earlier series of the same
session delivered was refused with `review_evidence_undelivered`.

A math-conjecturer record (on 0.69.6) met this defect. Both of its result reviewers cited readings that the first part
series had delivered. A final cannot be continued until it is recorded, a second continuation of the last part turn is
refused, and two refused outputs exhaust an assignment. So both slots were blocked.

## What changed

- New function `review_problem_parts.collect_earlier_series_packets`. It walks from the first turn of the current
  series to its predecessor, one earlier series at a time, and collects the delivered packets of each earlier series
  with `collect_series_packets`. These are the packets of the authenticated actual requests of ready turns. Saved
  assignment packets are never used, because the saved packet of a part turn holds more than that turn delivered.
- The walk stops at a turn that is not a result turn of the same reviewer, route, slot, session and author (for
  example the bar session), at a missing turn and at a cycle.
- `research_decisions.validate_review_contract` (when a final is recorded) and
  `research_decisions.find_undelivered_review_references` (the delivery audit of the post gate) use these packets in
  addition to the current ones, for the delivery check only.

These stay as they were: the current packet, the delivered objections, the expected finding ids, the bar ids, the
digest rule (a citation without a digest resolves only when the delivered rows know one digest), the exact-byte and
locator checks, and the coverage check `problem_evidence_unassessed`. The current series must still deliver the current
evidence in full.

## Upgrade

No action is necessary. A final that was refused only because it cited content of an earlier part series of its slot
can be recorded again with `value-review`. Records and stored requests do not change. No module that a route pins by
its bytes changes.

## Checks

The new module `tests/test_problem_result_earlier_series_delivery.py` has 14 tests: 12 of them fail on 0.69.18 and all
pass with this change. With it, these modules pass on Python 3.12.8 and 3.9.6: the new module,
`test_problem_result_parts`, `test_problem_result_parts_continued`, `test_problem_result_contract`,
`test_review_frozen_record_evidence`, `test_research_problem_reviews`, `test_research_output_views` and
`test_research_check_mode_audit` (203 tests), and the manifest and version tests at 0.69.19.
`codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` pass. The full suite runs in CI on the pull request.
