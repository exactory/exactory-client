# Exactory 0.89.1

This release carries more units of Stage 1 of the capacity, continuity and openness designs, and it corrects
the openness binding that 0.88.0 shipped for a packet that binds no main capture. It follows 0.89.0. The records,
the designs, the rulings and the reviews are in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261113110022-every-open-record-and-review-capacity` of the exactory repository.

## What changed

The main captures of a packet without a binding (closure requirement C8, rulings 14, 29 and 30):

- 0.88.0 read the first capture of an original as the main capture of an openness packet that 0.87.0 exported
  without a binding. When the reading chain of such a packet had delivered a capture that `select-main-capture`
  chose, 0.88.0 reported the finding of that chain as stale and refused its continuation.
- The capture of each original of an unbound packet now comes from its delivery contract, then from the
  authenticated reading plans of the recorded turns, then from the delivered text of a review without a plan. A
  chain in progress whose chosen capture was not yet delivered keeps its authenticated prefix, keeps the credit of
  its delivered units, and delivers the chosen capture in its next turn. Stored packets, plans, prompts and
  requests keep their bytes.
- A new chain over an unbound packet that carries another extraction of a required original ends before any call
  with `openness_reading_incomplete` and the handoff `export`.

The session rules v3 (records `20261111235418-bar-final-refused-for-text-delivered-in-reduced-history` and
`20261114054500-bar-cannot-be-recorded-after-instalment-deliveries`):

- A new bar session runs under `exactory-independent-review-sources-v3`. It asks for bounded artifact ranges,
  settles each request that it made, and states the ranges that it did not receive as limitations of its final. A
  session delivery is no reading.
- A citation is credited by the union of the spans that the turns of the session delivered for the same artifact
  and version: a text delivered as characters 0 to 60,000 in one turn and 60,000 to 97,404 in another is cited as
  one span. A span that ends after the delivered characters is refused with `review_evidence_undelivered`.
- Sessions under the rules v2 keep their delivery, their audit and their digests.

The forming records of a slate (record `20261112012956-slate-review-cannot-carry-a-forming-import`):

- A new slate packet carries the forming calls and outcomes of the record and of an imported record as artifact
  descriptors without their bodies, and the reviewer asks for an artifact on its next turn. Stored slates keep
  their delivery and digests.

The capacity evidence of a route (closure requirements C1, C3 (a) and C10 (b), rulings 27 to 31; records
`20261112043216-bar-turn-over-output-cap-at-effort-max` and
`20261112235500-openness-chain-final-turn-over-route-limit-after-57-turns`):

- The observed input limit of a route rests on evidence with its key: the endpoint and the runtime, and for a Codex
  CLI route the CLI version, the transport file and its controls. Every member of the runtime identity and of the
  route configuration of each adapter has a classification, and every prior row of capacity evidence has its key.
  An attempt gives its input tokens less the output reservation.
- A route whose runtime differs from the runtime of its prior rows, and a Codex route that no prior row and no
  stored attempt matches, has the input limit zero until its own evidence exists, for example a verified capacity
  probe. After a Codex CLI update or a change of a Codex transport or its controls, a review on a Codex route needs
  such evidence.
- The input limit of a `claude_cli_v1` route of a listed model is the smaller of the documented limit (the
  compaction threshold less the output limit) and the observed limit; `describe_route_capacity` shows both with
  their sources.
- A session turn has the kind `request` under the session rules v2, v3 and v4. In the state `over_limit_expected`
  a chain starts at the largest level whose task falls below every range whose own row holds at least four
  attempts and a share above 5%.
- The serving routes that a decision stop lists are measured with their stored evidence.

## Compatibility

No file of a reviewer route's runtime identity changes, so no route becomes stale. A Codex route without capacity
evidence under its key stops before its next review and names the capacity probe. Stored records, packets, prompts
and requests keep their meaning and their digests.
