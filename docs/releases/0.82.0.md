# Exactory 0.82.0

This release carries the bar and slate objections of a math-conjecturer record into another record that takes up
one of its candidates. 0.77.0 kept every reviewed candidate in its record as an interim rule; this release ends
that wait.

## Why

A bar or slate objection bound the candidates of its own record only. When a candidate moved to a record of another
subject class, or a sibling record took it up, the reviewers of that record never received the objection, and
nothing there blocked the post on it.

## What changed

- New command `exactory-research problem-objections-carry`, run in the destination record with
  `{id, source_workspace, source_revision}`. It opens the named source record and authenticates its reviews. It
  imports every objection of the source's bar and slate reviews with their later versions, the objections that
  the source itself carried, and their evidence. The source's resolutions and adjudications travel as context. The
  payload names no candidate and no objection. The proof (the source's snapshot and its managed artifacts) and one
  `problem_carried_evidence` record per version stay in the destination, and `record-check` verifies them offline.
- The destination's slate and result packets deliver each imported objection under `<id>@origin:<source identity>`
  with its complete version set. Bars receive none. Each slate and result review gives a finding on each, and an
  unresolved one blocks the post as `research_objection_unresolved` under the existing clearance rule. The
  `material_version` of an imported objection hashes every retained version, so a later carry that adds a version
  reopens it. A source resolution clears nothing.
- A nonempty import requires a focused slate review (`problem_slate_review_unverified` with `import_set_digest`)
  and a slate that binds the imported set (`problem_slate_stale` with `slate_id` and the `reason`
  `problem_objection_carry_changed`).
- The destination stays linked to its sources. Slate and result assignments, staged frontier plans,
  `problem-decision` with `post`, `export --kind problem` and `gate post` compare each linked source's current cut
  (its bar and slate reviews, objection versions, links and unfinished relevant review work) with the imported one.
  A change is `problem_objections_refresh_required`, and a source that cannot be opened or holds another record is
  `problem_objection_source_unavailable`. The check compares identities, pending work and links and rebuilds no
  evidence.
  The other new codes are `problem_objection_self_import`, `problem_objection_source_revision_mismatch`,
  `problem_objection_provenance_invalid`, `problem_objection_origin_conflict` and
  `problem_objection_sources_exceeded`.
- The carry authenticates every review of the source recorded up to the latest exported one. It derives each
  review's objection identities again from its observed output. A stored mapping that differs is refused, also on
  first insertion. The immutable-origin guard runs for every scoped review mutation, the child tasks of a staged
  review included.
- `problem-posted` opens no source. Once the external post has succeeded, it records the post with the returned id
  and the unchanged approved export. A linked source that changed or disappeared after the gate does not stop the
  recording. `status` and
  `next` then show the posted terminal with the freshness failure under `after_post`, and the harness schedules no
  review work on the posted statement.
- `problem-objections-carry --check` with a reachable source validates the carry and its proof and publishes
  nothing in the destination. Full `status` lists the carries under `problem_objection_carries`.
- The math-conjecturer skill replaces the wait of 0.77.0 with the carry. A sibling record needs carries in both
  directions with every continuing record of the exploration. See the CLI reference, "Carried objections", for the
  payload, the refusal codes and the recovery of each.

## What stays the same

- A record without a carry runs the old path. Its prompts, packets, currencies, parts series keys, report shapes
  and `status` bytes are unchanged, and `--summary` has no new field.
- A recorded assignment keeps its prompt bytes, hash and packet. A later carry does not rewrite it, but it can make
  its assessment insufficient for posting.
- The restart of an exploration before its first review request stays as in 0.77.0 for a record that holds no
  carry. A candidate does not move while the source has a current bar, slate or relevant result review that is not
  recorded.
- Records written by earlier releases do not change, and no migration is necessary.

## Known limits

- The freshness check is local to the mutation and cannot make an external post atomic with a change in a source.
  The skill runs `gate post` immediately before `exactory post-problem`. A source change after the post is an owed
  refresh under `after_post`. The harness does not change the post, and the agent reports the change.
- A pending source review of an earlier statement can be recorded only while that statement's candidate is still
  current. After the candidate is superseded, `problem-statement` refuses with `problem_candidate_missing`, and no
  command records the review. Preserve the records and report it.
- An objection that a source result review states first, outside the bar and slate identities, is not carried.
- A text route marks carried image evidence (rendered PDF pages) omitted. Such a review needs a route that receives
  images.
- The proof retains the complete source snapshot and the managed artifacts found through its history and nested
  JSON, not a minimal subset.
- The proof is a check of local hashes. It establishes the consistency of the source's history, records, outputs,
  identities and evidence at the imported cut. It does not establish who produced an output, or when an event
  happened. It does not establish that the source's initialization was honest, that its history is complete, or
  that an objection is true. The identity check rejects a stored mapping that disagrees with the retained outputs
  and the recorded earlier reviews. It does not authenticate the order or the completeness of that history on its
  own. A party that can write the source store and its artifacts can build a consistent fabricated history.
