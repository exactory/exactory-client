# Exactory 0.82.0

This release carries the bar and slate objections of a math-conjecturer record into another record that takes up
one of its candidates. It follows 0.81.0. It is the full fix of
`misc/harness-improvement/20261105171520-objections-lost-on-area-handoff` of the exactory repository, which 0.77.0
guarded with a text rule. GPT 6 Astra at effort `ultra` designed it, Sol 6.1 at `xhigh` built it, and Fable 5.1
wrote the texts.

## Why

A bar or slate objection bound the candidates of its own record only. When a candidate moved to a record of another
subject class, or a sibling record took it up, the reviewers of that record never received the objection, and
nothing there blocked the post on it. 0.77.0 kept every reviewed candidate in its record, and a selected candidate
of another class waited for this release.

## What changed

- New command `exactory-research problem-objections-carry`, run in the destination record with
  `{id, source_workspace, source_revision}`. It opens the named source record, authenticates its bar and slate
  reviews against their observed assignments and outputs, and imports every material objection they stated: the
  later versions that the source's result reviews gave on them, the objections that the source itself carried, their
  evidence, and the source's resolutions and adjudications as context. The payload names no candidate and no
  objection. The proof (the source's SQLite snapshot and artifacts) and one `problem_carried_evidence` record per
  member stay in the destination, and `record-check` verifies them offline (`problem_objection_carries`).
- The destination's slate and result packets deliver each imported objection under `<id>@origin:<source identity>`
  with its complete version set, in a prompt with the suffix `/objection-carry-v1` and one appended paragraph.
  Bars receive none. Each slate and result review gives a finding on each. The post gate lists an unresolved one as
  `research_objection_unresolved` under the existing clearance rule: two evidenced resolutions of the delivered
  version, or a focused adjudication that finds it `not_upheld` or `not_binding`. The `material_version` of an
  imported objection hashes every retained version, so a later carry that adds a version reopens it, and a source
  resolution clears nothing.
- A nonempty import requires a focused slate review (`problem_slate_review_unverified`) and a slate that binds the
  imported set (`problem_slate_stale` with `slate_id` alone otherwise).
- The destination stays linked to its sources. Slate and result assignments, staged frontier plans,
  `problem-decision` with `post`, `gate post` and `problem-posted` compare each linked source's current cut with
  the imported one. The cut is its bar and slate reviews, its objection versions, its own links, and its unfinished
  relevant review work. A bar, slate or result review of the source that concerns a carried objection counts as
  unfinished until its `value-review` is recorded; a completed but unrecorded review counts too. The refusal is
  `problem_objections_refresh_required`; a source that cannot be opened is `problem_objection_source_unavailable`.
  The check validates the source's history and compares identities, pending work and links only; it rebuilds no
  evidence (713 s to 2.2 s on a clone of the 291 MB cokernel record). The other new codes are
  `problem_objection_self_import`, `problem_objection_source_revision_mismatch`,
  `problem_objection_provenance_invalid`, `problem_objection_origin_conflict` and
  `problem_objection_sources_exceeded` (more than 32 linked workspaces).
- The carry authenticates every review of the source recorded up to the latest exported one. It derives each
  review's objection identities again from its observed output and the native identity rules, and refuses a
  stored mapping that differs, also on first insertion (`problem_objection_provenance_invalid`). A review or carry
  whose `recorded_revision` differs from its event's revision is refused the same way. The immutable-origin guard
  (`problem_objection_origin_conflict`) runs for every scoped review mutation that commits review records, the
  deterministic child tasks of a staged review included.
- `problem-posted` opens no source. It records a post whose export the gate approved when it was sent, also when a
  linked source changed or disappeared between the gate and the recording. The exact approved export and the current
  local readiness stay required. The post is an ordinary `problem_post` with `gate` `passed`. `status` and `next`
  then show the posted terminal, `problem_already_posted` alone, with the freshness failure under `after_post` with
  its source details and no `next` action. Review work on the posted statement is refused as posted before any
  source is opened. So are an explicit continuation of a closed slate lineage and the children of a posted staged
  controller.
- `problem_slate_review_unverified` after a nonempty carry with an empty `review_ids` carries `import_set_digest`;
  `problem_slate_stale` in the carry case carries the `reason` `problem_objection_carry_changed`.
- `problem-objections-carry --check` with a reachable source validates the whole carry and proof in memory, sealed
  retained artifacts included, and writes nothing. Full `status` and unsummarized `next` of a record with a carry
  list `problem_objection_carries` (`id`, `source_workspace`, `source_origin`, `source_revision`). A record
  without a carry keeps its status bytes, and `--summary` is unchanged.
- The math-conjecturer skill replaces the wait of 0.77.0 with the carry ("Carry a reviewed candidate to another
  record" in "Exploration"). A sibling record on the same field question or seed is permitted with a carry from each
  other record of the exploration. The thought-experiment entry, Formulation step 5, the slate passages and the post
  section follow it. `docs/research-cli.md` has the command row, the section "Carried objections" and the new codes
  in the obligation table.
- The ledger entry of the record now names three real-shape tests of `test_problem_objection_carry`. Its `fixed_in`
  is `open` in the carry change and is set by the release.

## What stays the same

- A record without a carry runs the old path: its prompts, packets, currencies, parts series keys and report shapes
  are unchanged.
- A recorded assignment keeps its prompt bytes, hash and packet. A later carry does not rewrite it; a later carry can
  make its assessment insufficient for posting.
- The restart of an exploration before its first review request stays as in 0.77.0. A candidate does not move while
  the source has a bar, slate or relevant result review that is not recorded: the carry refuses with
  `problem_objections_refresh_required`.
- Records written by earlier releases do not change, and no migration is necessary.

## Known limits

- The freshness check is local to the mutation. It cannot make an external post atomic with a change in a source, so
  the skill runs `gate post` immediately before `exactory post-problem`. A source change in between is recorded as
  an owed refresh under `after_post`. The harness does not change the post or schedule review work on it, and the
  agent reports the change to the user.
- An objection that a source result review states first, outside the bar and slate identities, is not carried.
- A text route marks carried image evidence (rendered PDF pages) omitted. Such a review needs a route that receives
  images.
- The proof copies the whole source snapshot and every artifact that the source's events name, not a minimal
  subset.
- The proof is a check of local hashes. It establishes the consistency of the source's history, records, outputs,
  identities and evidence at the imported cut. It does not establish who produced an output, or when an event
  happened. It does not establish that the source's initialization was honest, that its history is complete, or
  that an objection is true. A party that can write the source store and its artifacts can build a consistent
  fabricated history.
- The texts report of this release (`drafts/fable-0820-texts-report.md` in the record) lists the places where the
  code, the implementer's report and the design differ.
