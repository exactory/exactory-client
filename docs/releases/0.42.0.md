# Exactory 0.42.0

This release directs every research study at the challenges ahead of it and makes
contribution something the study develops on purpose. Blind reviews now say what
each score below 4 needs to reach 4. After each measurement the author records a
contribution analysis, and the next manuscript and the round decision read it.
The research constitution moves to version 5.

## The Grand Challenge record

A research study records `grand-challenge` before ideation: the ultimate goal its
field is trying to reach and, where useful, nearer large goals on the way. Each
challenge has a horizon (`ultimate` or `near_term`), its current state, criteria
that would show it reached, and evidence read in full. At least one challenge is
`ultimate`, and criterion ids are unique across the record.

The latest record is current for the whole study, across manuscript iterations
and rounds. A new record with a reason replaces it only when the direction
changes. The record sits outside the preparation digest, so recording or
replacing it never makes a pinned bundle stale. The preparation gate owes
`grand_challenge_missing` until the first record exists. `status` shows the
current record, and `status --summary` shows its challenges and criterion ids.

Open Grand Challenges on exactory are one input to the record. The record itself
stays in the study; proposing it on exactory remains a separate action.

## Changes for the maximum

The review core has a ninth field, `changes_for_maximum`. For each of soundness,
presentation and contribution, it lists the concrete changes that would bring a
score below 4 to 4, and it is empty for a score of 4. `manuscript-review` refuses
a new review without it or with a list that does not match its score. Reviews
recorded before 0.42.0 keep their credit at the publication gate.

## The contribution analysis

After three blind reviews and three predictions measure a bundle, the author runs
a small investigation and records `contribution-analysis` for that bundle:

- where the paper stands against the criteria of the current Grand Challenge
  record;
- one disposition, `adopted` into a step or `rejected` with a reason, for every
  contribution change the three measurement reviewers named;
- at least one step toward the challenges. A step names the criteria it
  advances, its direction, whether it fits this round (`this_round`) or needs a
  new round (`next_round`), the current claims of the bundle it builds on, and
  the community that gains, with evidence.

The investigation uses the new search purpose `grand_challenge`. The foundation
state leaves these searches out, and their cited works create no full-text
requirement, so the measured bundle stays current. Before this release, any
search recorded after a pin broke readiness.

`manuscript` refuses to pin the next bundle while the selected bundle has a
complete measurement and no analysis (`contribution_analysis_missing`).

## Rounds

- `gate round` owes `manuscript_measurement_missing` and
  `contribution_analysis_missing` before `round_decision_missing`, and `round`
  refuses a decision with the same codes.
- The candidates of a decision list every step of the bundle's analysis
  (`contribution_step_missing` otherwise).
- A `continue` goal names `criterion_ids` of the current Grand Challenge record.
- The round review has a seventh `continue` check, `grand_challenge`.
- The round packet carries the current Grand Challenge record and the analysis.
  The blind manuscript packet carries neither.

## What an existing study owes

A study that opens under 0.42.0 reports `constitution_revalidation_required`, as
in earlier releases. Adopt the new constitution with `constitution` and the
previous SHA-256. After that, the preparation gate also owes
`grand_challenge_missing` until the study records its Grand Challenge record, and
the synthesis sections that the new configuration makes stale are recorded
again. A study with a complete measurement on its current bundle then owes its
contribution analysis before the next pin or round decision.

Two existing studies were measured on copies before the release. In both, the
only obligation 0.42.0 adds is `grand_challenge_missing`, and revalidation
removes `constitution_revalidation_required`. Every other obligation was owed
under 0.41.0 already. The test record
`docs/testing/grand-challenge-contribution.md` lists the codes.

## For reviewers and verifiers

Blind measurement and publication reviewers return `changes_for_maximum` in the
review core, and nothing else about the study reaches them. Verification is
unchanged.
