# Exactory 0.88.0

This release carries the first wave of fixes for the open harness-improvement records and the first units of the
capacity, continuity and openness designs. It follows 0.87.0. The records, the designs, the rulings and the reviews
are in `misc/harness-improvement/20261113110022-every-open-record-and-review-capacity` of the exactory repository,
and each change below names the record that it answers.

## Why

Sixteen records were open on 2026-11-13. Eight of them came from one week of problem records whose reviews could not
finish: citations that the check refused for TeX braces, obligations that stayed after a post, refusals that did not
say what was wrong, a safeguards notice of the provider that ended a turn, search receipts that hid their gaps,
packets that a review could not reuse, and result frames that delivered the same search record many times. This
release fixes the parts of those records that need no new protocol. The parts that need the capacity plan, the
carried turns or protocol v6 are designed and come in later releases.

## What changed

Citations (record `20261112210200-citation-check-title-with-tex-braces`, tasks T1, T2, T4 and T5):

- `exactory-check` compares titles after it removes TeX commands and braces, so a title with TeX braces matches its
  registry title. A `title_mismatch` quotes the registry title and the entry's title. A cached result without the
  registry title is fetched again when it mismatches.
- The arXiv requests of `exactory-check` go through the shared provider pacing: 3 seconds between requests, the
  interval of the arXiv API terms of use.
- Both problem exports add `citation-refs.json`, built from the stored identifier, title and authors of each cited
  work, under the manifest schema `exactory-problem-export-v2`.

Posted problems and payload paths (record `20261113074404-next-after-post-keeps-a-stopped-turn-obligation`):

- After a post, the obligations of a stopped or replaced review chain of the posted statement move to `after_post`.
  `next` then names `problem_already_posted` alone.
- Every payload file field accepts a canonical workspace-relative path or an absolute path inside the workspace,
  also when the workspace is reached through a symbolic link in its ancestors, and is recorded in the relative form.
  A path through a symbolic link inside the workspace is refused with `unsafe_path`.

Native math reviews (record `20261113074306-math-solver-non-approval-review-and-implication-objective-gaps`, tasks
T1 and T2):

- `reassess`, `accept`, `review` and `complete` refuse a well-formed native review that does not approve as
  `native_review_not_approved`, with its decision, reason and objections. `problem_author_unknown` names the problem
  authors in `details.authors`.

Provider stops, usage and full-text timeouts (record
`20261112135015-openness-reading-turn-stopped-by-provider-safeguards`, tasks T1, T2, T11 and T13):

- A Claude response that holds the CLI's safeguards notice is `provider_refused` and stays unverified. It is retried
  up to three runs in all. In 1,114 retained CLI runs of the live records, 18 ended this way.
- `status` and every `review-series` step show the latest usage windows of the Claude CLI and an estimated warning.
  The usage figures decide no outcome.
- A full-text transfer that times out records `timeout_seconds`, `elapsed_seconds` and `bytes_received`.

Search receipts and captures (records `20261112143000-openness-chain-ends-unresolved-on-search-integrity-gaps` T2,
`20261112135015-...` T6 to T8, and `20261112150500-result-review-frame-holds-cited-search-records` T3):

- A search receipt gives the counts and the items of invalid works, incomplete responses and excluded items, each
  with its query, and the warning `search_page_capture_interval` when the pages of one query were captured more than
  15 minutes apart.
- A new native search leaves out an OpenAlex `paratext` work with its id, DOI and reason, and records
  `native_parse_version` 2. A stored search without the field is read under version 1, so its results stay as they
  were.
- A web import may declare `query_path`, and a search may then bind the named path segment as its query.
- A capture of OpenAlex with the `select` list of design D3 imports as before. On one live page of 25 works, a work
  took 2,446 characters with that list and 14,283 without it.

Packets, views and exports (records `20261112143000-...` T5, `20261113074000-openness-packet-cannot-deliver-what-its-searches-cite`
T4 and `20261113074500-post-on-user-order-instruction-condition-export-and-chain-stop` T5):

- `review-series` renders the output views after each operation and at its terminal stop.
- An export whose packet equals a delivered packet folder reuses that folder after its artifacts are verified.
- The current problem exports stay under `final/problem/exports/`, and a superseded export moves to
  `final/history/problem/exports/` with its bytes. The history reads both export formats.
- `post-external` accepts an optional `export_digest`.

Result series and openness chains:

- A problem result series delivers each cited search record once, as one unit by its kind, id and digest (design D1
  unit `account-record-units`). On a copy of the live record of the incident, the frame fell from 6,666,752 to
  692,463 characters.
- An openness reading chain reads the main capture that its packet bound. In 0.87.0 a `select-main-capture` after a
  recorded finding made the finding stale; the finding now stays current, and a new export reads the chosen capture.
- `export --kind problem-openness` assesses the minimum before it writes anything. A minimum that can only end
  unresolved is refused with `openness_minimum_gaps`; `--accept-gaps` exports with the same forecast as a warning
  (design D3 unit UA1).
- `deliver-attempt` records a link from each accepted result to the review that accepted it, and `record-check`
  verifies the links (design D3 unit UB4).
- One function finds the link between a found record and an exact version of the same work (design D3 unit UA2), one
  table holds the capture classes that each use admits (unit UC2), and one admission checks every reviewer call and
  launch (design D2 unit `call-admission`). These change no result today; later units read them.
- A render of the output views that fails names the path that failed, and a snapshot that leaves out an empty kind no
  longer makes the view model read the store again and again.

## What stays the same

- Every stored record, packet, prompt, request and digest keeps its meaning. A stored search, export, inventory,
  openness packet and repair is read under the rule of the release that wrote it.
- The texts that the agent and the reviewers read (the CLI reference, the skills and most messages) are those of
  0.87.0 for the new behavior; the writer's pass on them comes in the next release.

## What stored records see

- The bytes of `research_harness/review_claude_transport.py` changed: a Claude response now keeps the CLI's
  `rate_limit_event` entries. A Claude route pins the SHA-256 of its transport in its runtime, so every
  `claude_cli_v1` route recorded before 0.88.0 needs a new probe before its next review. A run or a probe on such a
  route is refused with `review_route_runtime_stale`. The stale-route procedure of the route instructions applies:
  record a new route with a new id and the same adapter, model, endpoint and configuration, probe it, and assign the
  role again on it, or, for a slot with history, retire the stale route and continue the slot through a context
  repair. Recorded probes and reviews keep their standing.
- No other file that a reviewer route pins changes.

## Known limits

- An openness finding recorded by 0.87.0 from a packet that 0.87.0 exported after a `select-main-capture` becomes
  stale under the binding of this release, because such a packet has no binding. No research store under the
  exactory workspace holds a `main_capture_selection` record.
- The typed non-approval covers the six-field form of a native review; the three-field form that the reviewer prompt
  asks for is still `invalid_record`. A fix is in progress.
- The paratext exclusion also applies when `acquire` reads an OpenAlex work, which then ends with
  `identifier_mismatch`. A fix is in progress.
- The capacity plan, the carried turns and protocol v6 are not in this release. A review call can still pass the
  output limit at effort `max`, and a long openness chain can still have a final that does not fit.
