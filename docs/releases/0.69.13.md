# Exactory 0.69.13

This patch reads each BibTeX entry by its top-level fields, so that the title and the identifiers of an entry come from
their own fields. It also removes TeX escapes before a URL is percent-decoded.

## Why

The independent review of 0.69.12 found four defects. With an unrelated title, each of the first three cited a work:

- `url={https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv%5C.2609.14520}`: 0.69.12 percent-decoded the URL and then removed TeX escapes,
  so `%5C.` became `.` and the DOI read as the DataCite DOI of arXiv 2609.14520.
- `doi="10.9999/x{"arxiv:2609.14520"}"`: a quote inside braces closed the quoted value. In BibTeX it does not.
- an entry whose `doi` value held `title={...}`: the title was found inside another field.

The fourth was a lost citation: `note="Available at https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14520"` no longer cited the work,
because the URL ran into the closing quote of the field.

## What changed

- Each BibTeX entry is parsed into its top-level fields: a name, `=`, and a value of braced, quoted or bare parts joined
  by `#`. A quote inside braces is part of the value. The parser does not raise on malformed text; it stops at a field
  head without `=`, and an unclosed value takes the rest of the entry.
- The title of an entry is its first top-level `title` field.
- URLs, DOIs, labels and old-style ids are read from each field value separately, so a field boundary ends every
  identifier. A top-level `eprint` value is read as an arXiv id, also without braces or quotes.
- TeX escapes are removed from the raw text of an identifier once. Percent-decoding of a `doi.org` path comes after that
  and removes nothing more.
- In an entry written with parentheses, a `)` inside a quoted value no longer ends the entry.
- A bibliography without BibTeX entries keeps the whole-text reading. Title matching and `find_citation_tokens` do not
  change.

## Upgrade

- Text outside the top-level fields of an entry is not evidence. A field after a malformed field head is not read.
- A quoted field such as `note="Available at https://arxiv.org/abs/..."` cites its work again.
- The notes on BibTeX keys (since 0.69.8), on DOIs in publisher URLs (since 0.69.10), on Markdown link texts (since
  0.69.11) and on `already_cited` items (since 0.69.5) still apply. Publication bundles that are already recorded do not
  change.

## What stays the same

The problem citation projection, the reviewer prompts, the route-pinned transport and cost modules and
`review_state_evaluation.py` are unchanged.

## Checks

The new tests ran first against 0.69.12 and failed as expected (32 failed subtests in 8 tests). One existing expectation
changed: a bare top-level `eprint=2609.14520` now cites the arXiv work. After the change, with
`PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=tests`, the modules `test_research_citation_accounting`,
`test_research_problem_citations`, `test_research_providers`, `test_research_publication`,
`test_research_publication_records`, `test_research_publication_files`, `test_research_problem_delivery`,
`test_math_problems_cli`, `test_codex`, `test_manifest` and `test_research_guidance` (424 tests) passed on Python 3.12.8
and 3.9.6, and the performance cases (10,000 entries, 64 KB fields) took less than one second.
`codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` pass.
