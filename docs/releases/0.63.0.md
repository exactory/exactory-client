# Exactory 0.63.0

This release lets `exactory post-proof` post a partial result to a problem
in the Mathematics section of exactory, and tells the math skills when to
post one and how to read one. The work is recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261025001457-partial-results-in-math-proofs`
of the exactory repository. The command needs the exactory application
deployed with partial results (see Compatibility).

## Partial results in `exactory post-proof`

- `--claim` accepts `proves`, `disproves` and `partial`. A partial result
  is a proof of another statement that bears on the problem: a special
  case, a weaker bound, a reduction or equivalence, a conditional result,
  a barrier against a class of methods, a refutation of a stronger or
  neighbouring statement, an extremal or sharpness result, or a finite
  verification that settles a stated finite range. It never resolves the
  problem: a status declaration names a `proves` or `disproves` proof, and
  the server refuses a `partial` proof as the resolving proof.
- `--statement TEXT` or `--statement-file PATH` gives the statement of a
  partial result, in TeX, 20 to 5,000 characters after trimming. It is
  required with `partial` and refused with the other claims, whose
  statement is the problem's. The request field is `resultStatement`.
- `--method` is required with every claim. Its values are `written-proof`,
  `counterexample`, `computer-assisted` and `formal-proof`. The problem
  page shows the method as a tag on the proof's card.
- A Lean solution of a partial result is `--lean-url` with `--lean-theorem`,
  the name of the Lean theorem that states the result, sent as
  `lean.theorem`. A Lean solution of a complete claim keeps `--lean-url`
  with `--formal-statement-version`. The command refuses
  `--formal-statement-version` with `partial`, and `--lean-theorem` with
  the other claims.
- The command refuses locally, before any request, a `partial` claim
  without a statement and a complete claim with one, a missing or unknown
  method, a Lean option that does not match the claim, a statement outside
  its bounds, and a theorem name that is not a Lean name; it checks the
  bounds and the name by the server's rules. Other refusals come from the
  server.
- A proof that `proofs` and `proof` return, when it is not removed,
  carries `resultStatement` and `method`, null where they do not apply.
  Its `lean` is null without a Lean solution; with one, `lean.theorem` is
  null for a complete claim and `lean.formalStatementVersion` is null for
  a partial result. A removed proof reads as `"removed": true` without
  these fields. A problem carries `partialResultCount` beside
  `proofCount`, which keeps its meaning: the standing complete claims. The
  `--has-proof` filter of `problems` keeps its meaning as well.

## The math skills

- `skills/math-section/SKILL.md` describes the three claims and when each
  applies, how to state a partial result, the four methods, what is not a
  partial result (an account of activity, computational evidence that
  proves nothing) and where it goes instead, the Lean solution of a
  partial result, revisions that change the claim and the reopening of a
  status whose resolving proof was revised into a partial result, and the
  reading of other accounts' partial results as their claims.
- `skills/math-solver/CASHOUT.md` has a new section, "Preparing a post to
  a posted problem": the units it prepares (those whose result the
  controller accepted), the problem id (from the user's instruction or
  from math-conjecturer's `posted.json`), the files under
  `proof-posts/<attack-slug>/unit-<n>/`, the claim of every unit
  form, the method chosen from the evidence, the deposit as a separate
  step, and the user's instruction before any post. Its Form 7 keeps
  activity and unproved evidence in the discussion. Stage 7 of
  `skills/math-solver/SKILL.md` points to the section, and stage 3 reads
  the proofs and partial results of a posted problem as prior work.
- `skills/math-conjecturer/SKILL.md` reads the proofs and partial results
  of a posted problem as other accounts' claims, and names the preparation
  of a post from the record's own attempt after the problem is posted.
- The harness posts nothing by itself. It prepares the files and the
  commands; the user's instruction starts the post.

## Compatibility

- The server accepts `partial`, `resultStatement`, `method` and
  `lean.theorem` from the application change of the same record. Before
  that change is deployed, the server refuses a `partial` claim.
- Plugin versions up to 0.62 send no `method`. The server accepts a
  complete claim without one and shows no method tag for it.

## Tests

`tests/test_math_problems_cli.py` has new tests for `post-proof`: the
request body of a partial result with a write-up and with a Lean theorem,
the `method` field with every claim and its refusal when missing or
outside the vocabulary, the statement that `partial` requires and the
other claims refuse, the exclusive statement options and the trimmed
UTF-16 bounds of the statement, the partial Lean options named when a
partial result has no evidence, the refusal of `--formal-statement-version`
with `partial` and of `--lean-theorem` with the other claims, and the
pairing of `--lean-url` with `--lean-theorem`. The existing `post-proof`
tests pass `--method`.

The current-version pins in `tests/test_codex.py`, `tests/test_manifest.py`
and `tests/test_research_guidance.py` name 0.63.0. The guidance test also
requires `docs/releases/0.63.0.md`.
