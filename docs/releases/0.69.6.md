# Exactory 0.69.6

This patch fixes the manuscript citation check, which could treat an uncited work as cited. It also narrows the link
that 0.69.5 added between problem citations.

## Why

The independent review of 0.69.5 found two defects.

- The manuscript citation check matched an arXiv id after any character that is not a letter or a digit. An entry whose
  only identifier is `doi={10.9999/2609.14520}` therefore cited the work `arxiv:2609.14520v1`. Since 0.69.5 it also
  cited a work that is known only by `doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.14520`. The accounting then let an uncited work pass as
  cited.
- 0.69.5 resolved the arXiv family of a cited work from every alias. A mapped import can store a plain arXiv id as an
  alias of a journal work, so a journal article and its preprint became one citation when their authors agreed. The
  0.69.5 design states that only the DataCite DOI links two works.

## What changed

- An arXiv id in a bibliography matches only after a character other than a letter, a digit, `.`, `/`, `_` or `-`, or
  right after `arxiv.org/abs/`, `arxiv.org/pdf/`, `arxiv.org/html/` or `10.48550/arxiv.`. DOI matching and title
  matching do not change.
- The citation projection of a problem resolves the arXiv family only from the work id and the DOI aliases of the
  work. A journal article and its preprint stay two citations.
- `docs/releases/0.69.5.md` gets a paragraph that states these corrections.

## Upgrade

- Manuscript citation accounting: an entry that cited a work only through the looser boundary no longer cites it. Add
  an accounting item for that work, or cite it by its arXiv id or its DOI.
- Since 0.69.5, a bibliography entry that carries only the arXiv id also cites a work that is known only by its
  DataCite DOI. An accounting item (`cited_as` or `reason`) for such a work is refused as `already_cited`. Remove the
  item before you prepare the publication again. Publication bundles that are already recorded do not change.
- Problem records: a record that cites a journal work with a plain arXiv alias and the arXiv work with the same
  authors gets back the two citations that it had before 0.69.5, and its problem evidence digest changes back. Posted
  problems, stored reviews and exports do not change.

## What stays the same

DOI tokens, the end of an identifier, title matching, the 0.69.5 DataCite rule for problem citations, the reviewer
prompts, the route-pinned transport and cost modules and `review_state_evaluation.py` are unchanged.

## Checks

The new tests ran first against 0.69.5 and failed as expected (an unrelated DOI cited both works, and the plain alias
joined a journal work with its preprint). After the change, with `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=tests`, the
modules `test_research_citation_accounting`, `test_research_problem_citations`, `test_research_providers`,
`test_research_publication`, `test_research_publication_records`, `test_research_publication_files`,
`test_research_problem_delivery`, `test_math_problems_cli`, `test_codex`, `test_manifest` and `test_research_guidance`
passed on Python 3.12.8 and 3.9.6. `codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` pass.

## Corrections in 0.69.7

The independent review of this release found that its boundary does not close the defect. An arXiv id still matched
after other characters that a DOI suffix can hold, inside a BibTeX key, and after an `arxiv.org` path on another host.
The legacy URL `arxiv.org/ftp/arxiv/papers/<yymm>/<id>.pdf` no longer matched. The statement that a journal article
and its preprint stay two citations holds only when a plain arXiv alias is the only link; a journal work whose DOI
alias is the DataCite DOI is linked. Release 0.69.7 replaces the boundary with a list of arXiv contexts and checks
ambiguity over every alias. Releases 0.69.8 and 0.69.9 then read identifiers by kind, because those contexts were
still substrings.
