# Exactory 0.73.3

This release records the real time of a problem post that was made outside the harness. It also corrects two faults
of the post gate that a refresh of the result reviews exposed. The release follows 0.73.2. The work is recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261104074403-harness-problems-closed-for-good` of the exactory repository.

## Why

A research session posted a problem on exactory.ai at 2026-11-04T14:49:22.236Z, the `createdAt` of the server. About
18 hours later, it recorded the post with `problem-post-external` of 0.73.2. The record stored the recording time as
`posted_at`, so `status` showed a wrong time for the post.

The same record had 4 unresolved objections under 0.72.4 and 14 under 0.73.2, measured on copies of its store. Since
0.73.0, each result slot counts only the final of its current series. Both result slots of the record have a
refreshed series without a final, so no current review resolves an objection. This reading is intended, but the CLI
reference did not state it.

A design review of these two observations found two faults in the post gate:

- With an empty current pair, the gate did not compute the evidence digest. An adjudication then did not match the
  digest and lost its effect. No live record has this shape: one live record has adjudications, and its pair is
  complete.
- A pending successor of a parts v1 series or of an ordinary review was reported as `problem_review_pair_required`
  with an empty `review_ids`. A pending staged or parts v2 series was reported as pending work of its slot.

## What changed

- `problem-post-external` takes an optional `posted_at`, the time of the post on exactory.ai. It is a timestamp with
  an explicit timezone, and the record keeps it exactly as given. Without it, the record stores `posted_at` null. The
  record adds `recorded_at`, the recording time in UTC. The harness does not compare `posted_at` with the local clock.
  When the time of the post is known, pass it: a post that 0.73.3 records without `posted_at` cannot get a time later.
- `status` shows an external post that 0.73.0 to 0.73.2 recorded with `recorded_at` set to its stored time and
  `posted_at` null. The stored record, its receipt and the raw output exports keep their bytes.
- The new command `problem-post-time {post_id, posted_at, evidence}` states the time of such an older external post,
  once. It writes an immutable `problem_post_time` record. `status` then shows its `posted_at`, the stored
  `recorded_at`, and the statement under `time_statement`. Outside a problem record it is refused with
  `orchestration_conflict`. Its other refusals are `problem_post_missing`, `problem_post_time_inapplicable` and
  `problem_post_time_already_recorded`. The output views export the statement at `problem/post-times`. The statement
  is not part of the problem evidence, the post gate or the reconstruction of review requests.
- The gate computes the evidence digest whenever it checks the evidence, also with an empty pair. An adjudication
  made against the current evidence and the current material version of its objection keeps its effect while the
  successors are pending. The post gate still requires the pair.
- The gate reports every pending or incomplete current result head as `review_task_pending` or
  `assessment_incomplete`, with `occupied`, `incomplete` and `pending`, for every kind of series. A slot without a
  head still gives `problem_review_pair_required`. The gate now checks a completed peer of a pending head for
  independence, assurances, rubric and coverage. It already did so for staged and parts v2 peers. `slot_outcomes`
  appears where 0.73.2 shows it, and where a head is pending or incomplete. `status` sorts the obligations of a record
  with a pending or incomplete head, and `next` gives the continuation of the pending series.
- `docs/research-cli.md` states the new field and command, the meaning of the `posted_at` of `problem-posted` (the
  time of the recording, normally right after `exactory post-problem` returns), the current-series reading of
  objections, and the pending occupancy.
- `tests/incidents/ledger.json` has five new entries with tests of their real shape.

No route-pinned module changed. No stored record changes, and no migration is necessary.

## For a record with an older external post

To show the time of an external post that 0.73.0 to 0.73.2 recorded:

1. Store the saved response of the server with `artifact`.
2. Run `problem-post-time` with the id of the post, the `createdAt` of the server as `posted_at`, and the artifact
   reference as `evidence`.

Then `status` shows that time as the `posted_at` of the post.

## Checks

- The changed and new test modules, with the CLI, check-mode audit, output views, input validation, guidance,
  posted-problem, review continuation and review parts modules: 605 tests of 34 modules pass on Python 3.12.8 and
  3.9.6, with no skipped test. The three new test modules failed before the change.
- After the texts and the version change, the post, guidance, CLI, check-mode audit, Codex, manifest and
  release-records modules: 159 tests pass on both versions.
- The implementation lane ran the 141 modules that import a changed module. Its first run failed only in three test
  modules whose old expectations this release replaces. After their update, the 75 modules that import the CLI
  (1,565 tests) pass on Python 3.12, and the 15 changed modules (399 tests) pass on 3.9.
- `codex/generate.py --check`, `git diff --check` and the compile check pass. No route-pinned module changed.
- On copies of the nine live problem records, `status` and `next` of 0.73.2 and 0.73.3 give the same output for
  seven records. Both result slots of the other two records hold a pending successor. Their reports differ only in
  three ways:
  - `review_task_pending` with `occupied` 2 replaces `problem_review_pair_required`.
  - The report adds `slot_outcomes`.
  - One of the two records now sorts its obligations, so its `next` names the other result slot first.
- The times of `status` and `next` on these copies differ from 0.73.2 by less than 2 percent.
- On a copy of the posted record, 0.73.2 recorded an external post in the old shape, and 0.73.3 showed it with a null
  `posted_at`. `problem-post-time` then stated the server time, replayed the same request, and refused a second
  statement. On another copy, 0.73.3 recorded the post with `posted_at` and kept the time exactly.
