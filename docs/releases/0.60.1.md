# Exactory 0.60.1

This release repairs one defect of 0.60.0, found by the code review of
0.60.0 after its merge. The work is recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261024093334-problem-choice-after-refutation`
of the exactory repository. The record kinds, prompts, templates and
packet shapes of 0.60.0 are unchanged.

## Grades made before A7 on a posted statement

- 0.60.0 added the admission item A7, direction after a refutation. A
  self-assessment or a result review graded with exactly the earlier
  admission items A1 through A6 is read unchanged.
- For a statement not yet posted, the post gate and `next`, which reports
  the next required action, list one `problem_rubric_regrade_required`
  obligation naming those records. While it is listed, the below-threshold
  shortfalls of those grades are not listed. This behavior is unchanged.
- For a posted statement, 0.60.0 listed no regrade obligation but also
  skipped the threshold checks of those grades. A shortfall that 0.59.0
  listed was no longer listed, including an A1 grade of `fail` in a
  self-assessment recorded after the post.
- For a posted statement, the harness now checks those grades against
  the items they grade, as in 0.59.0. Their shortfalls are listed as
  `problem_rubric_below_threshold`, the obligation for grades below the
  posting thresholds. Its `source` is `self` for a self-assessment and
  `review` for a result review.
- A ready focused adjudication on the current evidence with disposition
  `not_upheld`, which rejects the shortfall, releases the shortfall of a
  review. Its target, `rubric:<review_id>:<item>`, names the review and
  rubric item. It releases only that review's shortfall for that item.
- `skills/math-conjecturer/SKILL.md` now states both cases instead of
  saying that the gate lists shortfalls only for grades that cover A7.
- Read-only copies of the three problem stores, Sidorenko girth-six at
  revision 432, extremal-graph-stability at revision 301 and stretched-LR
  positivity at revision 572, read the same route and assignment states
  and the same post-gate obligations under 0.60.0 and 0.60.1. `next` gives
  the same milestone and obligations. All three statements are posted,
  and 0.60.1 lists no new obligation for any of them.

## Tests

`tests/test_problem_direction_after_refutation.py` has three new tests:

- `test_posted_previous_self_assessment_lists_its_shortfall` checks that
  an A1 grade of `fail` in a self-assessment made before A7 gives a
  shortfall with source `self` for a posted statement, with no regrade
  obligation.
- `test_posted_previous_result_reviews_list_their_shortfalls` checks that
  the A1 shortfalls of both result reviews made before A7 are listed with
  source `review` for a posted statement, with no regrade obligation.
- `test_posted_previous_rubric_adjudication_releases_only_its_review`
  checks that a current focused `not_upheld` adjudication releases the A1
  shortfall of the named review while the other review's shortfall stays.

The current-version pins in `tests/test_codex.py`, `tests/test_manifest.py`
and `tests/test_research_guidance.py` now name 0.60.1. The guidance test
also requires `docs/releases/0.60.1.md`.

The testing log is in
[math-conjecturer-harness.md, section "Release 0.60.1"](../testing/math-conjecturer-harness.md#release-0601).
