# Exactory 0.65.2

This release bounds the reading of one review turn. A turn delivers at most
120,000 characters of new full text, a longer text arrives in parts over
consecutive turns, and a turn whose run ended without output can be replaced.
The work is recorded in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261027085554-bar-source-requests-exceed-route-capacity`
of the exactory repository (brief `prompts/opus-implement-2.md`).

## Why

0.64.2 let a review read more full text than one route input can hold, but it
filled each turn up to the input limit. On 2026-10-28, in the KMP problem
record, the first reading turn of an openness review carried five full texts,
956,141 characters, and a bar continuation carried four, about 600,000
characters. Both requests fit the Codex input limit, and both runs were
stopped at the route's 600-second limit before the reviewer had read the texts
and written its excerpts. The work of one turn, not the input limit, was the
constraint. The bar slot then could not go on: the stopped continuation had no
output, and a new continuation of its parent was refused as a fork.

## Reading budget and parts

- `review_excerpts.TURN_FULLTEXT_CHARACTERS` is 120,000: the characters of new
  full text that one turn delivers at most. Excerpts already carried, passages
  and abstracts do not count.
- A text longer than the budget is divided into consecutive parts of at most
  120,000 characters. A part ends after the last blank line in the last quarter
  of its window, else after the last newline there, else exactly at the budget.
  The parts cover the whole text in order, and the same text always gives the
  same parts. Each part is its own text artifact, with
  `{version_id, part, parts, start, end, characters}`; `start` and `end` are
  character offsets in the stored text. Excerpts and passages are still checked
  against the whole stored text.
- An openness reading turn takes the longest run of the next units (whole
  texts, or parts), at least one, whose characters fit the budget and whose
  fitted request delivers each unit complete within the input limit. A text
  counts as read when its whole text, or every part of it, reached a reading
  turn complete. `reading_turns` and the reading record add `delivered_parts`
  for a turn that delivered parts.
- A bar, slate or result continuation under the excerpt rules answers full-text
  requests in request order within the budget. A source arrives whole when its
  text bodies fit what is left of the turn; a source that would fit a fresh
  turn waits with status `deferred_reading_budget`; the extracted text of a
  longer source arrives in parts, and the original file and other bodies of
  that source are not delivered with its parts. A request stays pending until
  its source or last part has arrived. A part delivery also maps its span of
  the stored text, so a citation inside one part counts as delivered.
  `already_delivered` applies once a version's whole text, or every part of it,
  reached the reviewer complete in turns under the excerpt rules.

## Replacing a failed turn

A continuation of a bar, slate or result session, or a turn of an openness
reading chain, whose recorded attempts all ended without a completed output
(for example at the route timeout) no longer holds the session. A new
continuation of the same parent is accepted and replaces it. The replaced
assignment stays in the record and can be neither run nor continued
(`review_assignment_replaced`). An assignment with a completed output, or one
that has not run, still blocks a second continuation
(`review_source_continuation_fork`). A failed first turn of a reading chain
has no parent; it is replaced by a new export under a new assignment id.

## Prompts

The excerpt rules (`exactory-review-excerpts-v2`) and the reading and final
prompts (`exactory-openness-reading-v2`) state the budget, that a long text
arrives in parts over consecutive turns, what a part delivery and a deferred
request mean, and that a request stays pending until its last part arrives.
The prompts of 0.64.2 to 0.65.1 (`exactory-review-excerpts-v1`,
`exactory-openness-reading-v1`) stay available, so assignments recorded with
them keep authenticating. Bar, slate and result sessions keep their template
ids, as before.

## Interface changes in openness_reading and review_excerpts

No public function of these two modules was renamed or changed its parameters.

- `review_excerpts`: new `TURN_FULLTEXT_CHARACTERS`, `PROTOCOL_V1`,
  `PHASE_PROMPT_V1`, `PHASE_PROMPTS`, `find_fulltext_capture(records,
  version_id)`, `compute_part_spans(value)`, `compute_part_digests(value)` and
  `save_part(artifacts, value, reference, start, end)`. `PROTOCOL` is now
  `exactory-review-excerpts-v2` and `PHASE_PROMPT` states the budget.
  `find_delivered_fulltexts` is unchanged; the harness no longer calls it.
- `openness_reading`: new `PROTOCOL_V1`, `READING_PROMPT_V1`,
  `FINAL_PROMPT_V1`, `PROMPTS` and `list_reading_units(artifacts, texts)`.
  `PROTOCOL` is now `exactory-openness-reading-v2`, and `READING_PROMPT` and
  `FINAL_PROMPT` state the budget. Entries of `reading_plan` add
  `part, parts, start, end`; entries of `verify_chain` and of the reading
  record's turns add `delivered_parts` when a turn delivered parts.

Outside these two modules: `review_protocol.canonical_prompt` adds the keyword
parameters `excerpt_rules` and `reading_protocol`; `review_protocol` adds
`has_ended_without_output` and `find_replacement`; the fifth parameter of
`bar_sources.continuation` is now `delivered_bodies`, a map from body SHA-256
to the turn that delivered it; `review_supplied_evidence` adds
`list_delivered_parts`.

## Compatibility

- The eleven fingerprinted review modules are unchanged against 0.65.1, so
  registered routes stay valid.
- Records, assignments and attempts of 0.60.0 to 0.65.1 stay readable and
  authenticated. A finding recorded from a 0.64.2 reading chain stays current:
  its turns delivered whole texts, so `verify_chain` gives the same entries.
- A chain or session that 0.64.2 to 0.65.1 started continues under the new
  rules from its next assignment. A 0.64.2 reading plan lists whole texts
  without part fields; the next turn divides them into units. Whole texts that
  a 0.64.2 session delivered count for `already_delivered`.
- `complete-visible-v1` result continuations keep their own contract and
  deliver every requested text whole.

## Choices for the owner

- The budget counts every UTF-8 text body of a whole source delivery (for
  example an HTML original next to its extraction); a source above the budget
  is read through the parts of its extracted text only.
- Full-text requests are served strictly in request order: once a request
  waits for the budget, every later full-text request of the turn that needs
  new text waits too.
- A turn holds its session when it has a completed output, when it has not
  run, or while an invocation is pending; only a turn whose attempts all ended
  without a completed output is replaceable.
- For a source that is still partial, the parts are delivered once; after the
  last part the request stays pending with status `partial` and no new text.
- The replay of earlier turns of a bar session still carries their bodies until
  the input limit leaves them out; the budget counts new text only.

## Not in this release

- Refusing an oversized Codex input before dispatch, and the code-mode
  controls: another lane owns them, and they change fingerprinted modules.
- Math-solver result reviews as reading chains: another lane builds them on
  this release.
