# Exactory 0.91.0

This release is the second part of Stage 2 of the capacity, continuity and openness designs. It follows 0.90.0. A
new problem openness chain now starts under the reading protocol v7 when its admission accepts the packet, and
under v6 otherwise. A v7 chain carries the verified reading turns of an earlier chain, replaces a refused turn at
its own position, and reaches a finding when the provider refuses a unit in every sequence. The records, the
designs, the rulings and the reviews are in
`misc/harness-improvement/20261113110022-every-open-record-and-review-capacity` of the exactory repository.

## What changed

The reading protocol v7:

- A new problem openness chain takes the first protocol of the order `(v7, v6)` whose admission accepts its
  packet on its route. A chain of v5 or v6 keeps its protocol. A math-solver chain keeps
  `exactory-openness-reading-math-v1`.
- The inventory and the chain state of a v7 chain are rebuilt from the stored records. Each turn has a stored
  capacity plan, and each request is checked against it before the call.
- A new chain carries the reading turns of an earlier chain of the same statement, reviewer and route whose units
  did not change. Changed and new units are read again.
- `verify_chain` checks a v7 chain: the delivery of every required unit, the required affected notes, the
  capacity replacements, the carried turns and the recorded prompt paragraphs.
- A v7 final shows the list `decisive_work_ids` (or a unit for a long list), the text of the reviewer's own gaps,
  and one index row for each affected note. A passage request accepts the handle of a logical unit. A reading turn
  refuses a `material` answer; an argument is written in the final.

A refused turn and a chain that cannot go on:

- A refused v7 reading turn or final is replaced at its own position from the unchanged state of the refused turn,
  with the notice of its refusal. A refused final whose entries fill its level keeps its level and its material,
  and the notice uses the 45,000 characters that the plan reserves beside every level.
- When the next turn of a v5 or v6 chain cannot fit, or a v7 final no longer fits one request after a denser
  measured ratio, the plan hands off to a new export whose chain carries the verified turns (v7) or reads again
  (v6). When no protocol admits the successor, the head stops with its two answers.
- An output over the cap and a response over the limit on a v7 reading turn step down each level on the same
  route and effort, and the decision stop comes after the floor of 15,000 characters. An input over the limit at
  the floor of a v7 position ends with the decision stop of its size row.
- An outdated v7 packet hands off to export, and its verified turns stay.
- A v7 reading chain on a visual adapter stops before any request with `review_visual_text_route_required`. Its
  answer registers the text route of the same model and runs the chain there.

The final over unread ranges:

- When the provider refuses a v7 reading turn in every permitted sequence at the lowest level, the chain takes its
  final over the ranges that it did not read. The complete set of unread ranges is one stored artifact, cited as
  `x1`. The final shows its first rows, and `list_page` serves the rest. The finding over such a final is
  `unresolved`, with a ground that references the set.

The guards:

- The capacity guard runs the v7 reading chain on every adapter and profile class, with bodies from 0.9 to 30
  times the input limit, 10,000 units, 5,000 and 20,000 targets, 2,000 gaps and 400 affected-note origins. The
  continuity guard runs every outcome of a v7 turn to a recorded finding or a declared stop.
- Every code on which a series can end still has a registry row.

The texts that a reviewer and an agent read in these units were written by the project's writer and reviewed at
effort max.

Incident ledger: 12 entries set to 0.91.0, and one entry that 0.90.0 fixed set to 0.90.0.

## Compatibility

No file of a reviewer route's runtime identity changes, so no route becomes stale. Stored records, packets, prompts
and requests keep their meaning and their digests. A chain of v5 or v6 keeps its protocol, and a recorded prompt
keeps its version. The visual text turn of a v7 chain ships with the transport release 0.93.0, together with the
other changes of route runtime files.
