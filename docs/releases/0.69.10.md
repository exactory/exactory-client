# Exactory 0.69.10

This patch makes the manuscript citation check read each bibliography entry once into whole identifiers, arXiv ids
and DOIs, and compare works with them. An identifier inside another identifier no longer counts, for DOIs as well as
for arXiv ids, and the time that the check takes grows only in proportion to the length of the bibliography.

## Why

The independent review of 0.69.9 found three defects.

- 0.69.9 ended URLs and DOIs at every `]`. With an unrelated title, `url={https://[::1]/?q=arxiv:2609.14520}` and
  `doi={10.9999/x]arxiv:2609.14520}` therefore cited `arxiv:2609.14520v1`. DOI syntax allows `]`.
- The URL scheme pattern had no length limit. A field of 64 KB of repeated `a-` took about 2.4 seconds, and the check
  repeated the scan for each arXiv id of each work.
- DOIs still matched as parts of the text. `doi={10.9999/x/10.48550/arxiv.2609.14520}` cited a work known only by the
  DataCite DOI `10.48550/arxiv.2609.14520`.

## What changed

- `read_bibliography` gives each entry, and a bibliography without entries, one set of identifiers: arXiv families and
  DOIs. The check compares the identifiers of a work with this set.
- URLs and DOIs are found in one pass from left to right, as in 0.69.9. A URL scheme has at most 32 characters. A `]`
  ends a URL or a DOI only where `](` follows, which is the boundary of a Markdown link.
- A DOI counts only as a whole DOI, or as the DOI that a `doi.org` or `dx.doi.org` URL names (percent-encoded
  characters are decoded). The DataCite DOI `10.48550/arXiv.<id>` also names the arXiv id.
- An arXiv id counts only from an arxiv.org URL with an arXiv path, from the DataCite DOI, or from an `arXiv:` label,
  an `eprint` field or an old-style id that overlaps no URL and no DOI.
- Title matching and `find_citation_tokens` do not change.

## Upgrade

- A DOI inside another identifier no longer cites its work. A DOI inside a publisher URL path, for example
  `url={https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/...}`, also no longer cites its work. Add a `doi` field or give the
  exact title.
- An arXiv id or a DOI inside a URL with brackets, or after a `]` inside a DOI, no longer cites the work.
- The notes on BibTeX keys (since 0.69.8) and on `already_cited` items (since 0.69.5) still apply. Publication
  bundles that are already recorded do not change.

## What stays the same

The problem citation projection, the reviewer prompts, the route-pinned transport and cost modules and
`review_state_evaluation.py` are unchanged.

## Checks

The new tests ran first against 0.69.9 and failed as expected (28 failures and 2 errors in 48 tests). After the change,
with `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=tests`, the modules `test_research_citation_accounting`,
`test_research_problem_citations`, `test_research_providers`, `test_research_publication`,
`test_research_publication_records`, `test_research_publication_files`, `test_research_problem_delivery`,
`test_math_problems_cli`, `test_codex`, `test_manifest` and `test_research_guidance` (397 tests) passed on Python 3.12.8
and 3.9.6. Reading a 64 KB field of repeated `a-`, or of repeated `10.1234/`, and checking it against 50 works took at
most 0.02 seconds. `codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` pass.

## Corrections in 0.69.11

The independent review of this release found that every `](` ended an identifier, that its 32-character scheme limit
left a longer scheme without a URL, that URLs stopped at `<`, which SICI DOIs in `doi.org` URLs use, that removing a
trailing `)` corrupted DOIs with balanced parentheses, and that unclosed entries made reading quadratic. The statements
above that no identifier counts inside another and that the time is bounded therefore did not hold in every case.
Release 0.69.11 corrects these points.
