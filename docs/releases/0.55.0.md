# Exactory 0.55.0

This release adds the research half of `math-conjecturer`: the harness creates
open mathematical problems as research before it posts them. A problem record
now holds an exploration with its candidates and their comparison, an attempt
on the exact statement, and a rubric self-assessment, in addition to the
statement, the openness finding, the value evidence, the reviews and the post
decision of 0.53.0. The operations of the Mathematics section that are not
problem creation move to the new skill `math-section`.

## Candidates and the slate

- `exactory-research problem-candidate` records a candidate question with its
  source (`own_record`, `literature`, `computation`, `section` or `user`), the
  observation that produced it, its declared neighbours, its provisional
  potential grades and a provisional disposition. A revised candidate names
  the earlier one as `previous`.
- `exactory-research problem-slate` records the selection decision. Every
  current candidate gets exactly one disposition: `selected`, `deferred` with
  the evidence that raises it, or `rejected` with a reason. The slate names
  two observed independent problem bars and zero to two focused slate reviews
  (template `exactory-problem-review-v2/slate`). A selected candidate needs
  `max(B1, B2) >= 2` and readiness `pass`. An empty selection with a reason is
  the no-post outcome of an exploration.
- `problem-statement` takes an optional `candidate_id`. The post gate requires
  a slate (`problem_slate_missing`), a slate that still covers every current
  candidate (`problem_slate_stale`), and a statement whose candidate the latest
  slate selects (`problem_candidate_unselected`).
- `status` reports the candidate counts and the slate id. The milestones are
  `exploration`, `slate`, `formulation`, `attempt`, `openness`, `value` and
  `post`. A slate that selects nothing reports `problem_no_post` as the next
  step, with its reason.

## The attempt

- `exactory-research problem-attempt` binds the current statement to an
  attempt. A solver attempt names a `native_attempt` record that the
  math-solver controller delivered into the problem record's store, with an
  `applicability` text that states how the native claim's quantifiers,
  assumptions and scope match the statement and the resolution criteria; the
  claim's scope must be `named` (`problem_attempt_scope_restricted`). A
  literature attempt names full readings and a critical assessment. The record
  states what the attempt resolved, the footholds, the unresolved work and the
  scoped obstructions, each with its evidence, and whether a normal limit (24
  moves, 24 runs, 1 worker, 300 seconds) was extended and why.
- A solver attempt binds the snapshot that the attack root's current pointer
  names (`problem_attempt_superseded` otherwise). When the solver delivers a
  later snapshot of the same objective, the post gate reports
  `problem_attempt_stale` with both digests until a new attempt record binds
  the latest one.
- The post gate requires a current attempt (`problem_attempt_missing`,
  `problem_attempt_stale`). A resolution persists: when any attempt of the
  current statement is resolved (a resolved literature attempt, or a solver
  objective whose latest snapshot has an accepted root proof or
  counterexample, before or after closure), `problem_attempt_resolved`
  blocks the post until the statement changes or the solver invalidates the
  snapshot. The candidate is a result, not an open problem.
- Value paragraphs accept a third evidence kind, `result`: an accepted proof,
  reduction or obstruction of the current solver attempt. The readings of a
  literature attempt are delivered to the result reviewers, covered, cited and
  exported like value readings.
- `exactory-math search deliver-attempt` writes the current attempt of a
  math-solver objective, resolved or not, into the common store that the
  proposal's `review_workspace` names: the contract, the state, the
  obligations, the checkpoints, the acceptances, the retreats, the failed
  strategies, the nodes and the totals (moves, runs, the largest admitted
  workers and timeout). Every delivery also sets the pointer record
  `native_attempt_current` of the attack root and objective to the digest
  it delivered, so that the problem record binds the snapshot the solver
  calls current. The section "Common-store delivery" of
  `skills/math-solver/SEARCH.md` gives the record shapes and the refusals.
  Closure delivery now reports `audit_failed` with its failures map.

## The rubric, the self-assessment and the reviews

- `exactory-research problem-assessment` records the harness's own grades for
  every rubric item (admission A1 to A6, significance on the grounds B1 and
  B2, comparative priority, the attempt, accessibility and calibration), with
  an evidence reference and a note per item and the primary account. The post
  gate requires a current assessment that meets the thresholds
  (`problem_assessment_missing`, `problem_assessment_stale`,
  `problem_rubric_below_threshold`, `problem_rubric_attempt_unbound`).
- The problem review templates move to version 2:
  `exactory-problem-review-v2/bar`, `/slate`, `/result` and `/adjudicator`.
  A result review grades every rubric item and names the delivered evidence
  for each grade. A below-threshold grade in a result review is repaired with
  evidence or resolved by a focused adjudication that finds the objection
  `rubric:<review_id>:<item>` not upheld. A version 1 result review must be
  replaced before the post (`problem_rubric_review_required`).
- The result reviewers receive the attempt (its outcomes, proportion and
  applicability, the native contract, state, totals, obligations, failed
  strategies, retreats and nodes, the literature binding, the cited
  acceptances with their claims), the cited results, and every reference of
  the self-assessment resolved into its content (`assessment_evidence`: a
  cited record with the record, a cited source with its full readings, which
  are delivered, covered and cited like value readings), and their coverage
  is checked.
- The material objections of every observed slate review of the current
  candidate set and bars reach the result packets, the post gate and the
  focused adjudication, whether or not the slate cites the review. After the
  candidates or the bars change, the same reviewer is re-assigned the slate
  review and the harness refreshes it in the same session and slot.
- The version 2 bar prompt states level 2 in full (a substantial mathematical
  advance and its relevance) and says that a question without footholds is
  admissible on a level-3 significance case. A slate cites version 2 bars
  only (`problem_bar_calibration_required`); a reviewer whose bar was recorded
  on the version 1 prompt is re-assigned the bar, and the harness refreshes
  it on version 2 in the same session and slot.
- A refreshed bar, slate review or result review gives an
  `objection_findings` entry on every objection of the review it supersedes
  (`review_objection_history_incomplete`); until the refreshed review is
  recorded, the earlier objections stay current. Every problem continuation
  packet, the bar's included, carries `historical_reviews` with the recorded
  reviews of its lineage, so the reviewer sees the objections it addresses.
- A material objection has versions. A `continuing` or `contested` finding
  of a result or bar reviewer whose reason or evidence differ from the latest
  version is a new version that inherits the claim, the resolution condition
  and the candidate applicability; only the latest review of each lineage
  (the same stage, reviewer, session and slot, and for a result the same
  evidence) states versions. The packets deliver the latest version with
  `material_version`, `review_id`, `recorded_revision` and the earlier
  versions in `history`; the post path and `problem_review_currency` follow
  the versions, and an earlier resolution or adjudication does not dispose of
  a later version.
- The adjudicator packet delivers, for the selected version, its stating
  review (`version_review`) and the evidence and source mappings supplied for
  it (`version_evidence`, `version_source_mappings`), apart from the original
  pair's correction evidence; `bar_calibrations` are the adjudicated results'
  original standards, and `current_bar_calibrations` a replacement after a
  context repair; `rubric_definition` is the rubric's anchors. The gate
  verifies that delivery. `version_evidence` holds one entry for every
  reference the version cites: an artifact descriptor, or a cited record
  delivered as the projection the stating reviewer's packet carried for it
  (never the stored record), with the descriptors of its content. A reference
  the packet cannot deliver makes the packet incomplete: the gate names it in
  `undelivered_keys` and does not count the adjudication. A fixed adjudicator
  is refreshed only on a changed material version, a changed evidence digest
  or an earlier packet that lacked a required delivery, and `focused_refresh`
  records the reason.
- Every reviewer role has its own response contract: the version 2 bar
  contract, the slate and result contracts with the evidence grammar (a
  delivered source is cited as `{version_id, source_id, artifact, locator}`
  copied from the delivery) and `rubric_evidence` as text, and the
  adjudicator contract with exactly its five fields.
- Every record that a problem packet projects for a reviewer carries the
  stored record's `id` and `digest` (the claim, the openness finding, the
  value paragraphs, the self-assessment, each formal check and its native
  closure, the attempt and its native attempt, the computation rows; records
  carried inside another record are listed in `record_references`), so a
  reviewer cites it as `{kind: "record", record_kind, id, digest}`. A review
  that cites a record its own packets never carried, or an artifact or source
  whose bytes or passages they never delivered, is refused with
  `review_evidence_undelivered`. Every delivered review payload carries a
  copyable `record_reference` for the same purpose.
- A packet attaches bytes only through its explicit evidence paths, within
  their bounds: the problem evidence, the delivered sources and passages, and
  a material objection's `version_evidence`. The review payloads a packet
  delivers (`bars`, `own_bars`, `historical_reviews`, the adjudicator's
  calibrations) and a material objection's own `evidence` carry their
  artifact references as templates, so a passage that one reviewer received
  cannot reach another reviewer as the whole original through a payload that
  cites it. A result or slate reviewer receives each material objection's
  cited records as content under `material_objections[].version_evidence`
  and its cited passages under `material_objections[].version_source_mappings`,
  and a slate or result packet delivers every historical bar objection the
  reviewer must give a finding on. A record delivered with an objection keeps
  the artifacts that came with it through later citations.
- Every delivered passage mapping carries `citation`, a valid source link for
  exactly that passage, which a reviewer copies to cite it; a mapping without
  a citation (a JSON passage in a bar, slate or result packet) is for reading
  only. A source citation counts only when the artifact was delivered in full
  or the cited locator lies within a delivered passage; a citation beyond the
  passage is refused with `review_evidence_undelivered`, and an adjudication
  whose packet lacks any cited content is incomplete.

## The skills

- `math-section` is new. It holds the operations of the Mathematics section
  that were in `math-conjecturer`: browsing and reading, formal statements,
  proofs, reports of Lean checks, discussion, links, votes, status
  declarations, deposits of write-ups and rule-violation reports. The text of
  those sections is unchanged.
- `math-conjecturer` keeps "Compose and post a problem" unchanged and gains
  the research half: exploration, candidates and the slate, formulation, the
  attempt, openness, value and the rubric, reviews and the decision.

## Upgrade effects

- A problem record from 0.53.0 or 0.54.0 keeps its recorded reviews and
  their history. It does not pass the post gate until it holds a candidate
  and a slate that selects it, an applicable unresolved attempt, a rubric
  self-assessment, two bars on the version 2 prompt and two version 2 result
  reviews. Refresh a version 1 bar by re-assigning the same reviewer: the
  harness refreshes it on version 2 in the same session and slot. Do that
  before any result assignment; a result refresh delivers the current
  calibrated bars, and a validated context repair keeps its assigned slot.
- The expanded openness evidence changes every problem record's evidence
  digest. Earlier reviews, decisions and exports no longer establish
  readiness by themselves: refresh the result reviews, record a current
  decision and rebuild the export before posting.
- A statement recorded before candidates existed has no `candidate_id`;
  formulate it again under a new statement id with a `candidate_id` that the
  slate selects. Re-selecting the old statement unchanged returns
  `record_conflict`. Evidence that still applies is reused after
  reassessment. Recorded posts stay immutable.
- A solver attempt binds a `native_attempt` record delivered into the problem
  record's store, the one that the attack root's current pointer names. A
  digest that exists only in the attack root's own store does not bind. New
  solver bindings require `applicability`, a named scope, and a reason for
  exceeding any of the four normal limits.
- A material objection is bound to the version of its finding, not to its id:
  an earlier resolution or adjudication does not dispose of a later version of
  the same objection, and the fixed reviewers reassess it; superseded reviews
  are history. Result reviews and adjudications produced from packets that
  lacked the calibrated bars, the rubric definition, the openness evidence or
  the version's evidence are reassessed through the refresh path, in the
  reviewer's own session and slot.
- Refresh the version 1 bars through the same reviewers, sessions and slots
  before any result assignment; a refreshed review addresses the objections
  of the review it supersedes. Focused slate reviews stay optional and are
  refreshed when the candidate set or the bars change. A validated context
  repair keeps its assigned slot.
- Follow the role-specific response contracts: a source reference carries the
  complete delivered link, a record reference its kind, id and digest,
  `rubric_evidence` values are text, and an adjudicator response holds
  exactly its five fields.
- Study, draft, verification and math-objective records are unchanged. The
  math-solver controller gains one command and writes nothing new unless
  `search deliver-attempt` is called.
