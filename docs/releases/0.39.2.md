# Exactory 0.39.2

This release repairs three development-round boundaries found during the review
of [0.39.1](0.39.1.md). It enforces the final round decision at deposit, separates
claim history from blind review, and corrects the measurement population.

## Publication requires the final round decision

`exactory-draft deposit` requires an approved `stop` on the exact current bundle.
This applies to initial deposits, `--new-version`, and resumed unfinished
deposits. The publication service checks the bundle and round before each remote
write, including the preview update. A changed bundle stops further writes.

Completed publication receipts remain available. Reconciliation can still read
an uncertain remote outcome before any further write. New versions retain their
prior-record and environment checks and their existing DOI lineage.

## Blind reviewers receive current claims

The manuscript export derives a separate claims file for the reviewer. It omits
`revised` metadata and `superseded` entries. Its evidence map and source/result
closure contain only current claims. The derived file has its own content hash
and exists only in the delivery directory.

The study retains its complete pinned ledger and bundle identity. The round
assessor receives the original ledger with its history. The PDF, abstract,
bibliography and optional source archive retain their exact bytes.

## Measurement uses the three paired reviewers

The measurement pairs a review with its prediction by normalized assessor
identity and exact bundle. Publication-only reviewers remain outside this group.
For measurement scores `[7, 8, 9]` and publication scores `[5, 5]`, the reported
measurement median is now `8`, rather than `7`.

`measurement.complete` is true when exactly three distinct prediction assessors
each have a review. Duplicate or extra predictions make the group ambiguous.
Incomplete or ambiguous groups report counts and null medians and spreads.
The publication gate retains all its existing review rules.

Earlier stored round assessments retain their original measurement values.
Current summaries use the corrected calculation. Raw reviews and predictions
remain unchanged. Measurements remain outcomes, rather than gate criteria.

## Upgrade and resume

The constitution remains Version 3 and the store schema remains version 1.
An upgrade from 0.39.1 requires no store migration or constitution revalidation.

Before a first or revised deposit, obtain an approved stop on its current bundle.
If an unfinished deposit lacks that decision, complete the round review before
resuming writes. Use the normal reconciliation command for an uncertain remote
outcome. Export fresh manuscript packets for future blind reviews.

For each measurement, record the three reviews and predictions with matching
assessor identities. The paired measurement must be complete before its median
can represent an outcome. Historical comparisons must identify the plugin
version that produced each stored measurement.

The [testing record](../testing/round-integrity.md) contains the reproduction
cases and validation results. These repairs establish mechanism correctness.
The contribution and cohort-prediction improvements still require measurement
on real studies under the development-round validation protocol.
