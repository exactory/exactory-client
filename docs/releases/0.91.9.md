# Exactory 0.91.9

A new v7 openness chain carries reading turns of earlier chains. With this release, only two refusals make it
carry fewer turns of an earlier chain. Every other refusal ends the command with its code. Five views of the
records keep the history of the store, which the verification of a carried selection reads. A guard of the test
suite compares each declared call of the packet registry with the source of its caller. The release follows
0.91.8. Its record is `20261113110022-every-open-record-and-review-capacity`, under `misc/harness-improvement/` of
the exactory repository.

## What changed

The refusals of an earlier chain:

- A new chain under the reading protocol v7 carries the verified reading turns of earlier chains. An earlier chain
  qualifies when it has the reviewer, the author, the statement, the formulations and the route identity of the
  new chain. The harness reads each chain that qualifies. The CLI reference calls this work the selection.
- The selection reads an earlier chain up to its latest turn first. When that reading is refused because a turn is
  not ready, the selection reads the chain again up to the turn before.
- From 0.91.0 to 0.91.8, every refusal had this effect. The only exception was a refusal of the artifact store,
  since 0.91.4. When the examination of the packets of an earlier chain was refused, the selection left that chain
  out.
- As a result, a refusal that says nothing about a turn made the new chain carry fewer turns, or none. Two
  examples are `review_route_capacity_plan_violated`, which is a defect of the harness, and
  `invalid_native_review_packet`. No command reported the refusal. The verification of the new chain made the same
  selection, so it accepted the result.
- Now only two refusals send the selection to the turn before. `review_context_unverified` says that a turn has no
  observed, authenticated output in a verified context. `openness_reading_incomplete` says that the turns do not
  verify under the protocol of the chain.
- Every other refusal ends the first assignment of the new chain with that refusal. `review-assignment` and
  `review-assignment --check` then record nothing.
- The report `carried_readings` also examines the earlier chains under another reviewer. When the store cannot
  give an artifact of such a chain, the report holds a row of `unavailable_chains`, as before. Every other refusal
  of such a chain now ends the first assignment. Before this release, the report left the chain out.
- When the admission starts the new chain under v6, the chain carries nothing. The report then states the
  selection of a v7 chain for the same packet (`would_carry`, `passed_over` and `outputs`). When this selection is
  refused, the report holds `refusal: {code, details}`, and the v6 chain starts. Before this release, this applied
  to a refusal of the store only. Another refusal that reached the report ended the first assignment of the v6
  chain.
- The plan of a stopped chain examines whether a new export is admitted. When the admission selects v7 and the
  selection is refused, the plan has the code of that refusal. Before this release, a refusal that says nothing
  about a turn left the handoff `export` in the plan, with fewer carried turns or none.
- A v7 chain whose measured ratio leaves its final without room examines the same admission. When the selection
  is refused there, `review-assignment` and `review-run` now end with that refusal. Before this release, a
  refusal that says nothing about a turn left their refusal `review_chain_projection_over_limit` in place, and
  `review-series` gave the handoff `export`.
- For a carried selection of version 2, the verification of a chain has two parts. The first part makes the
  selection again on the store as it stood before the first assignment. This part follows the same rule now. A
  refusal other than the two ends it with the code of that refusal.
- The second part authenticates each earlier chain up to its last carried turn. In this part, the two refusals
  and a refusal of the store give `openness_reading_incomplete` with the message of the selection, as before.
  Every other refusal now ends the verification with its own code. Before this release, it gave
  `openness_reading_incomplete` too.
- When the verification ends with a code, a finding that the chain recorded is `problem_openness_stale` with that
  code as its `cause`.
- For a selection of version 2, the verification reads the store as it stood before the first assignment from
  the history of the store. When the records hold no history, the verification now ends with
  `claim_reassessment_required`. Before this release, it ended with `openness_reading_incomplete`. The selection
  of a later chain took that code for a turn that is not ready.
- The message of `openness_reading_incomplete` for the first assignment of a chain named three causes. It now
  names the two that remain. The first is a reference that does not match the stored selection. The second is a
  `recorded_revision` that is not the revision at which the history shows the assignment recorded.
- The CLI reference states these rules under "The reading protocol v7". The items are "Carried turns", "The
  report of the carried turns", "The admission of a successor chain", "The verification" and "The verification of
  the carried selection". The row `problem_openness_stale` of its table names the new causes.

The history of the store in views of the records:

- Since 0.91.4, a store outside managed research keeps the history of its records when a review assignment holds
  a carried selection of version 2. The verification of that chain reads the history.
- Five views of the records were plain mappings without the history. `status` builds two of them when a context
  check is refused. A route renewal builds one for its capacity trial. The other two are the binding of the
  capture selections of a packet and the authority of a gate override.
- On such a view, the verification of a chain with a selection of version 2 was refused with
  `openness_reading_incomplete`. From 0.91.4 to 0.91.8, this had three effects in a store with such a chain:
  - When the context check of any assignment was refused, `status` reported the current finding of the chain as
    `problem_openness_stale` with the cause `openness_reading_incomplete`.
  - The condition check `openness_supported_open` of a gate override listed the same obligation for a current
    finding.
  - In a store with a `main_capture_selection` record, which `select-main-capture` writes, the first assignment
    of a later chain carried no turn of an earlier v7 chain. No command reported a refusal.
- Now each of the five views keeps the history. `status` and the condition check report a current finding as
  current, and the later chain carries the turns of the earlier v7 chain.
- For the view of the route renewal, no run showed an effect. A review found its path in the source.

The call rows of the packet registry:

- This part changes no command of the harness and no file of `research_harness`. It concerns a person who
  changes the source of the plugin, and it adds one command for that person.
- The packet registry is the file `tests/fixtures/capacity_packet_schema.json`. It declares the members of each
  reviewer packet, and it declares each call that passes a packet. Such a declaration is a call row: an entry of
  `callee_inventory`, `mapping_sites` or `non_packet_sites` with the digest of the call.
- Before this release, no code compared a call row with the source of its caller. A row stayed in the registry
  when its call changed or left the source. A new call that passes a packet needed no row after a change renewed
  the digests of its module and its function.
- On 0.91.7, six rows named no call of their caller, and 211 calls that pass a packet had no row. The count takes
  each call once for each caller and digest.
- Now the guard `ci/capacity_packet_inventory.py` compares the rows with the source in both directions. Each row
  names a call of its caller. For `mapping_sites`, a row can also name an assignment statement. Each call in a
  function of a certified module that passes a packet has a row. A row of `callee_inventory` whose call reaches a
  function of the plugin names that function.
- The guard reports five kinds of difference: `stale`, `changed`, `unequal`, `target` and `missing`. The message
  of each kind states the repair: the command to run, or the change that a reviewer makes in the declaration.
- `python3 -m ci.capacity_packet_inventory --call-rows` prints the differences as JSON. It exits with status 1
  when the list is not empty.
- With `--write-call-rows`, the command removes each row whose call left the source, adds each row that the source
  determines, and renews `packet_call_digests`. It keeps a row when its caller still holds an undeclared call to
  the callee of the row. It prints that row with the digest and the line of each such call for a reviewer.
- The table `certified_callers` leaves the registry. It repeated the rows in another form, and no production run
  read it. The data-flow walk of the guard now reads the caller edges from the rows.
- On 0.91.7, the repair added 195 rows to `callee_inventory`: 175 from the command and 20 after a review. It
  added 13 reviewed rows to `mapping_sites`, and it gave six rows the digest of their changed call. The registry
  then holds 2,102 rows of `callee_inventory` and 173 rows of `mapping_sites`, where it held 1,907 and 160.

Incident ledger: 3 entries set to 0.91.9.

## Effects and limits

The refusals of an earlier chain:

- A first assignment that this release refuses started under 0.91.0 to 0.91.8, with fewer carried turns. The new
  chain then read the text of the other turns again. For the refusal, follow the stop of its code ("Stops and
  overrides" in the workflow reference).
- Such a refusal ends the first assignment of every new chain whose selection reads that earlier chain. This
  release adds no command that leaves one earlier chain out of the selection.
- A chain under v5 or v6 that has its result is read first with the turn that returned the result. The checks of
  that turn run before the selection goes back to the reading turns. A refusal of these checks that is none of the
  two now ends the selection. On copies of 20 live problem records, the readings of the earlier chains ended
  with `openness_reading_incomplete` seven times and with `review_context_unverified` twice. They ended with no
  other code.
- `openness_reading_incomplete` is also the refusal of a chain that the installed release no longer verifies. The
  selection does not separate this case from a turn that was never ready. It carries the turns before in both
  cases, as before.
- The message of `claim_reassessment_required` asks to reassess a bound record. That step belongs to managed
  research and does not apply to a problem chain. For a problem chain, run `record-check`, and then report the
  refusal with its details.
- After this release, no path of a command is known on which the verification reads such a chain without the
  history. A probe over 209 tests of the modules that build v7 chains found none.

A correction of the note of 0.91.4:

- That note said that every store without the index of its record versions is read as before. This is correct
  for the index. It is not correct for the test that decides whether a store keeps the index.
- Since 0.91.4, each read of a store outside managed research runs one query over its review assignment records.
  A problem record is a store outside managed research. In a store without a selection of version 2, the query
  reads every assignment record.
- Two measurements ran on copies of three live problem stores. In two of the stores, the assignment records hold
  57 and 62 MB. There the query took 0.09 to 0.12 seconds in the first measurement, at a load average of 9 to 10
  on twelve cores. It took 0.17 to 0.49 seconds in the second measurement, at a load average of 53.
- In the third store, the assignment records hold 1.9 MB. There the query took 3 to 7 milliseconds in both
  measurements. The design of 0.91.4 measured this store only, with 4 milliseconds.
- This release does not change the query.

The call rows:

- A change of the plugin source that adds, changes or removes a call that passes a packet now fails the guard
  until the rows agree. The guard runs in `tests/test_capacity_packet_inventory.py` and in the new module
  `tests/test_packet_call_rows.py`.
- The command writes only the rows that the source determines. On 0.91.7, 33 calls needed a classification from a
  reviewer. A row of the kind `changed` stays until a reviewer gives it the digest of its call or removes it.
- The guard also compares a row with the function that its call reaches. This comparison leaves out a call of a
  nested function and a call of a method through `self`.
- The guard takes approximately one second more of process time. On Python 3.12 at a load average of about 31, it
  took 5.0 to 5.6 seconds, where it took 4.2 to 4.5 seconds before.

## Compatibility

No file of the runtime identity of a reviewer route changes, and no reviewer prompt changes. As a result, no route
becomes stale. Stored records, packets, prompts and requests keep their bytes and their digests.

The carried selection:

- A stored selection keeps its bytes. A chain that verified before this release verifies now, with one
  exception.
- The exception is a chain with a selection of version 2 that 0.91.4 to 0.91.8 built after a refusal other than
  the two. The verification of such a chain now ends with that refusal, and a finding that it recorded becomes
  `problem_openness_stale` with that code as its `cause`. A new export starts a chain for a new finding after the
  cause of the refusal is gone.
- No chain of this kind is known. The 30 live stores that the design examined held no carried selection. On
  copies of 20 live problem records, the selection gave the same rows with and without the change. It met only the
  two refusals there, nine times.
- A chain that did not verify before this release does not verify now. The code of its refusal can differ, as
  "What changed" states.
- `status`, the condition check of a gate override and a first assignment now read a chain with the history of
  the store. A finding that they reported as stale without the history is current again. It needs no new record.
- A selection of version 1, which 0.91.0 to 0.91.3 stored, has no first part. Its verification authenticates each
  earlier chain up to its last carried turn only, as before.
- Three outputs have a new form. The `cause` of a stale finding of a v7 chain can be a code other than
  `openness_reading_incomplete`, `artifact_missing` and `artifact_corrupt`. The member `refusal` in
  `carried_readings` of a v6 receipt can hold a code other than a refusal of the store. The message of
  `openness_reading_incomplete` for the first assignment names two causes.
- A stored assignment holds `carried_selection` and not the report `carried_readings`, so no stored record holds
  the member `refusal`.
- These refusals are new. The first assignment of a v7 chain and its check end with a refusal that earlier
  releases read as a turn that is not ready. The plan of a stopped chain has the code of that refusal. After a
  denser ratio, `review-assignment` and `review-run` end with it. The verification of a chain ends with
  `claim_reassessment_required` when the records hold no history.

The call rows:

- The registry loses `certified_callers`, and it gains rows in `callee_inventory` and `mapping_sites`. The list
  `packet_call_digests` changes in 21 certificates. No packet schema, no declared member and no pin of a module or
  of a function changes with these rows.
- A branch that holds an older registry meets the guard when it merges this release. Run
  `python3 -m ci.capacity_packet_inventory --write-call-rows` in the plugin root after the merge, and review the
  differences that the command prints.

## Checks

The new class `CarriedRefusalTests` of `tests/test_openness_carried.py` (8 tests) and the new class
`HistoryViewTests` of `tests/test_openness_carried_integration.py` (4 tests) pass on Python 3.12 and 3.9. On
0.91.6, the base of the lane, 11 of the 12 tests fail. The other test shows a property that does not change: the
two refusals send the selection to the turn before. The six changed modules have the same bytes in 0.91.6, 0.91.7
and 0.91.8. In the lane of this change, 793 tests in 51 modules or classes passed on Python 3.12 and 3.9. A
script ran on copies of 20 live problem records, with 0.91.6 and with the change. For each first assignment of a
reading chain, it listed and read the earlier chains. The 20 records hold 30 such assignments, and ten chains
qualify. The rows were equal, and the readings ended only with the two refusals.

The new module `tests/test_packet_call_rows.py` (28 tests) passes on Python 3.12, 3.9 and 3.14. On 0.91.7, 26 of
its tests do not pass. In the lane of this change, 12 modules (257 tests) passed on Python 3.12 and 3.9. On the
tree of 0.91.7, the new command printed 212 differences with `--call-rows`: 6 of the kind `changed` and 206 of the
kind `missing`. After the repair it prints none, and a second `--write-call-rows` changes no byte of the registry.
The output of the command and the written registry are equal on Python 3.12 and 3.9.

After the corrections of the texts of each lane, tests ran again. For the carried selection, 224 tests of 7
modules and one class passed on Python 3.12 and 3.9. The modules are `test_openness_carried`,
`test_incident_ledger`, `test_research_guidance`, `test_openness_reading_v7_verification`,
`test_series_code_reach`, `test_capacity_packet_inventory` and `test_review_request_parity`. The class is
`HistoryViewTests`. For the call rows, `test_packet_call_rows` and `test_incident_ledger` (35 tests) passed on
Python 3.12 and 3.9, and `test_capacity_packet_inventory` (39 tests) passed on Python 3.12.

On the release branch, after the merges of the two lanes into 0.91.8, the guard of the call rows printed no
difference, and each of the 206 certificates matched its merged source. 185 tests passed on Python 3.12 and 3.9:
the modules `test_codex`, `test_manifest`, `test_research_guidance`, `test_packet_call_rows`,
`test_incident_ledger`, `test_series_code_reach` and `test_capacity_packet_inventory`, and the classes
`CarriedRefusalTests` and `HistoryViewTests`. `codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` passed.

The CI of the pull request runs the full suites on Python 3.9, 3.12 and 3.14, in eight shards for each version.

## Still to come

examines an answer again before a v7 chain builds on it.

Release 0.92.0 takes both changes of this release with its next merge of `main`. There the rule of the two
refusals also applies to the math reading chains.

observes each earlier chain on the content records as they stood before the first assignment of that chain.
