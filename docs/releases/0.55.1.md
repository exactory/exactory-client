# Exactory 0.55.1

This release corrects the delivery boundary of the problem reviews that
0.55.0 introduced, and adds a bounded recovery after a review output that the
harness refuses. The problem commands, the record kinds of 0.55.0 and the
version 2 reviewer prompts are unchanged, and the pinned prompt hashes are the
same.

## Citations a role can record

- A delivered passage mapping (`prior_context`, `version_source_mappings`,
  `supplied_source_mappings` and the mappings under an objection) carries
  `citation` only when the receiving role can record that link as evidence:
  the passage is a text span, and its source has a current full-text reading
  whose inspections contain it. One predicate decides it for the bar, slate
  and result reviewers and for the adjudicator.
- A text passage of an abstract-only reading and a JSON passage arrive without
  `citation`. The prompts already say that such a mapping is for reading and
  cannot be cited. In 0.55.0 a reviewer that copied an offered citation of
  such a passage was refused with `reading_missing` and lost its slot.
- A correction still cites any delivered passage, JSON included. Correction
  input and output evidence are different contracts.

## Delivery of the adjudicator's evidence and of reassessment findings

- `review-adjudication` refuses an adjudicator output that cites content the
  adjudicator did not receive, with `review_evidence_undelivered` and the same
  details as `value-review`: a record that no packet of the adjudicator
  delivered at the cited digest, an artifact that no packet attached in full
  or as passages, or a source link outside a delivered passage. The
  adjudicator's delivery is its own packet lineage: the problem evidence, the
  projected records, the objection's `version_evidence` with its artifacts,
  and the original and version passage mappings, kept through a correction or
  a focused refresh.
- `value-review` applies the same rule to `reassessment.findings[].evidence`
  of a repaired review.
- At assessment time, every current recorded bar, slate and result review of
  a problem is audited for delivery against the frozen delivery of its
  assignment's lineage. A review that cites undelivered content carries the
  obligation `review_evidence_undelivered` with `review_id` and
  `undelivered_references`; the pair is not ready and the post gate reports
  it. The same reviewer refreshes the review in its session and slot (reason
  `evidence_undelivered`).
- A saved adjudication whose evidence was not delivered (a record from
  0.53.0, 0.54.0 or 0.55.0) stays in the record and carries the obligation
  `review_adjudication_evidence_undelivered` with `undelivered_references`.
  It does not resolve its objection, and the post gate is not ready. The same
  adjudicator, route and author reassess it through a focused refresh with
  reason `disposition_unsupported`; a later ready adjudication supplies the
  disposition.

## Recovery after a refused review output

- `value-review` and `review-adjudication` check an observed output against
  the output contract: the shape of the payload, its locators, its citations
  against the delivered content, its bar ids against the delivered bars, its
  findings on the delivered objections, a reassessment against its
  predecessor, and the adjudicator's objection id and disposition. In 0.55.0
  a refused output recorded nothing, the assignment kept its completed
  attempt, and the reviewer and its slot were blocked for every role.
- The same call now writes one immutable `review_output_refusal` record for
  the attempt (`assignment_id`, `attempt_id`, `output_digest`,
  `request_digest`, `code`, `details`, `check`) when the output fails a check
  that reads only the output and the delivered packets. The record is written
  after the output is authenticated against the observed attempt, so a
  provenance failure is never a refusal. A refusal that depends on the store's
  state (a pending source request, a reading that is not current, a conflict
  with a stored review) writes no record.
- `review-run` invokes the same assignment again while every completed
  attempt is refused and each refused output still fails its check: the
  packet, the prompt, the route and the request digest are the same, and the
  reviewer receives nothing about the refusal. A refused output that has
  become recordable is recorded instead; `review_resampling_forbidden` says
  so. After two refused outputs, `review-run` refuses the next invocation
  with `review_slot_exhausted`.
- The findings that a review must give are the material objections of its
  own packet and the objections of the historical reviews and historical bars
  that the packets of its lineage delivered. A refreshed review gives no
  finding on an objection that an earlier packet of the session delivered and
  that is no longer material.
- `status` reports `review_output_refused` with `refused_attempts` (the
  attempt id and the code) while every attempt of an assignment is refused,
  and `usage_report` counts the refused attempts as calls.
- An exhausted initial bar slot can be assigned to a new reviewer; the same
  reviewer stays refused. A slate or result slot and the adjudicator recover
  only through the retry, because the slot keeps its bar continuity and the
  adjudicator is fixed.

## Tests

- Two regression tests: the typed `artifact_missing` error when a
  problem-evidence attachment's object file is missing during the
  adjudication coverage check, and the undelivered entry of
  `describe_version_evidence` for a review recorded before the record-time
  rule of 0.55.0.
- The marker wait of the probe lifecycle tests waits for a complete file
  (already on main since PR #40).
- The two source-request tests that expected a refused review output with
  contradictory source requests to leave the store unchanged now expect the
  refusal record above.

## Upgrade effects

- A problem record from 0.55.0 keeps its reviews, adjudications and their
  history. Its saved adjudications are checked for delivery at assessment
  time; one that cites undelivered content blocks the post until the focused
  refresh above records a supported disposition.
- A packet built by 0.55.0 keeps its stored citations. When the harness
  delivers such a mapping again, it offers the citation only if the role can
  record it, and the record-time rule judges a citation by the passage's
  bounds, not by the stored citation.
- The version 2 prompts are unchanged, so the assignments of 0.55.0 stay
  verified.
- A store with a refused output from 0.55.0 or earlier holds the completed
  attempt without a refusal record. Submit the observed output again to
  `value-review` or `review-adjudication` with its assignment and the
  current revision: the call returns the error as before and writes the
  refusal record, which advances the revision. Read `status` before you
  invoke the assignment again with a new attempt id. Two refused outputs
  exhaust the assignment, so such a first output permits one more sample.
- A recorded review from 0.55.0 whose evidence was not delivered to its
  reviewer (a reassessment finding of a context repair is the case that
  0.55.0 did not check) carries the obligation `review_evidence_undelivered`
  with `review_id` and `undelivered_references` at assessment time. The
  record stays. The same reviewer refreshes the review in its session and
  slot: a new assignment with an empty context is permitted while the
  obligation stands, and its packet carries `problem_refresh` with the reason
  `evidence_undelivered`. A post decision that named the superseded review
  needs a new post decision with the refreshed reviews. If the refreshed
  output fails its output contract, `review-run` continues the same
  assignment through the retry above, and the obligation stays until a valid
  review is recorded.
- Known limitation. A bar packet that 0.55.0 built with a citation of an
  abstract-only passage, whose reviewer copied that citation, holds an output
  that `value-review` refuses with `reading_missing`. This is a failure of
  the saved packet, not of the output, so 0.55.1 writes no refusal record
  and permits no retry for it, and no refresh or replacement reaches that
  slot. 0.55.1 builds no such packet. The repair of a saved packet is planned
  for 0.55.2; until then, keep the old store and continue the problem in a
  new store.
- Study, draft, verification and math-objective records are unchanged.
