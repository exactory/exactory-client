# Exactory 0.92.1

This release repairs three areas that release 0.92.0 left open: the finishing writes of a remote job and of a
reviewer call, the render after a write, and the field classes of a review packet. It also adds tests that drive
the new forms of a review. No command selects these forms yet. The release follows 0.92.0. Its record is
`misc/harness-improvement/20261121234749-switch-to-the-new-review-forms` of the exactory repository.

## What changed

### Remote jobs and reviewer calls

- A Colab launcher reads the first reconciliation snapshot after `DONE` until its deadline. Before, the launcher
  read this snapshot one time, and a busy store at that read ended the command. A busy read does not start the
  release check of the launch loop again. If the store stays busy until the deadline, the launcher refuses with
  `store_busy`. The message names `reconcile-run`, which collects the same job.
- A busy refusal of a reviewer call names its call. When the store is busy, the record of a child process or of a
  capture gives a refusal. The details of this refusal now hold `operation` and `invocation_id`. The message says
  what a reconciliation records for this call.
- `python3 ci/unittest_shards.py run-modules --help` prints a description of the subcommand.
- A sharded CI run builds the published submission template one time for each Python version.

### The render after a write

- The harness builds the same files with fewer reads. The verification of a series in parts lists its series one
  time. The check of a parts turn reads the delivered items one time, at the first statement that needs them.
- Each rendered file has the bytes of 0.92.0. The validation results, the source locations and the refusals are
  the same.
- In a read with 32 whole items, 0.92.0 read the manifest 33 times. This release reads it 2 times.
- The processor time of one write did not change. On a record of 180 MiB, `form-outcome` took a median of 12.43 s
  in 0.92.0 and 12.34 s in this release (three runs each).

### The field classes of a review packet

- Each member of a review packet has a field class. A member without a class gives the refusal
  `review_input_unreadable`. Before, the builder of the frame ended with a Python `ValueError`.
- The identity members of a packet stay in the frame: `protocol`, `role`, `reviewer_id` and `dossier_id`. Before,
  a large input moved these members into units. The reader of the request then refused the second turn of a study
  series of the parts protocol v3. This occurred from 127,000 net input tokens. No command selects this protocol.
- A new capacity plan records the members that its frame keeps. A stored plan keeps its bounds.
- The repair packet of a study adjudicator names the protocol `exactory-focused-review-evidence-v2`. Before, it
  named `exactory-independent-review-sources-v2`. The prompt of this repair used the focused version before.

### Tests of the new forms

- Two reviewer stubs answer from the instructions and the user message of one call. They read no store and no
  validator of the harness. One stub drives a session of the rules v4 and a series of the parts protocol v3. The
  other stub drives the reading turns, the pages, the combine turn and the math final.
- The property coverage fixture holds 59 driven rows and 18 gaps. Each gap has its reason.

## Not in this release

- The new forms stay unselected: the session rules v4, the parts protocol v3, the math protocol v2 and the v7
  final in pages. The constants `NEW_SESSION_RULES`, `NEW_SERIES_PROTOCOLS`, `V7_FINAL_FORMS` and
  `NEW_MATH_CHAIN_PROTOCOLS` keep their values of 0.91.14.
- On 2026-11-22, independent reviews of the repairs of each form still found defects of the first rank. In most
  of them, a plan names a step that the store or the math controller refuses, and the plan then repeats.
- The repairs of these forms are on branches of the plugin repository and are not merged.
- Four more items are on branches and are not merged: the citation rules at the commands that record a citation,
  the payload audit, the history proof, and the findings of the context check and of the carried selection.
- The record `misc/harness-improvement/20261122114001-new-review-forms-not-switched-in-0-92-1` lists each branch
  with its latest commit, its latest review and its open findings.

## Effects and limits

- The repair packet of a study adjudicator has a new packet protocol. The packet digest of a new repair is
  different from the digest that 0.92.0 gives for the same inputs. A recorded repair keeps its packet.
- The peak memory of the validation of a parts turn can be larger than in 0.92.0 by a small amount. In the
  measured case (32 delivered items, one quotation) the peak was 190,352 bytes, 768 bytes more than in 0.92.0. The
  amount increases with the number of delivered items.
- The details of a busy refusal of a child record or of a capture have two more members. A reader that compares
  the full details of such a refusal sees a difference.

## Compatibility

A stored record keeps its meaning. A stored capacity plan replays with the bounds that it recorded. A chain in
progress takes its next turn with the protocol that its record holds. No record form changes.

## Checks

- Each merged change had an independent review before the merge. The reviews found no open finding of the first
  rank for these changes. One finding of the second rank stays open: the 768 bytes of peak memory.
- The test modules of each merged change passed on Python 3.12 and on Python 3.9, on an export of the merged tree.
- The byte tests of the render compare each rendered file with the functions of 0.92.0.
