# Exactory 0.60.0

This release makes the direction of problem selection after a refutation an
absolute principle. It adds the admission item A7, direction after a
refutation, and version 5 review templates. Earlier assessments and result
reviews that lack A7 must be graded again before posting a statement that
is not yet posted.

## Problem selection after a refutation

On 2026-10-24 the user made this principle absolute: when a counterexample
or another proof refutes a conjecture, the harness treats the refutation
as a fact about mathematics and chooses questions about the world that
the refutation reveals. It does not choose questions whose purpose is to
find where the refuted statement still holds. No rubric grade, review or
other consideration outweighs this principle.

The occasion was a reply from Professor Razborov about the posted Sidorenko
girth-six problem. That problem asked whether Sidorenko's inequality holds
for bipartite graphs without 4-cycles after a reported counterexample. It
is of the excluded kind and stays posted.

## Admission item A7

The rubric adds A7, "direction after a refutation", with a grade of `pass`
or `fail`. Every admission item must be `pass` before posting. Its anchor
in `research_harness/problem_rubric.py` is:

> Direction after a refutation: a reported counterexample or another
> reported proof against a conjecture that the question concerns counts
> as a refutation unless the record shows it wrong, and the question
> then studies the world that the refutation reveals, such as the
> mechanism or the structure of the counterexamples, how far and how
> strongly the statement fails, what other results fall with it, or the
> statement that replaces it. A question whose purpose is to find where
> the refuted statement still holds fails whatever its other grades and
> whatever form it takes; its usual forms ask for a restricted class or
> for added hypotheses under which the refuted statement holds, with a
> value case that rests on that survival. The purpose is read from the
> question and its value case, not from its surface form, and an added
> value argument changes neither the purpose nor the grade. A question
> that no refutation concerns passes.

## New assessments and result reviews grade A7

New self-assessments and new result reviews grade A7 with its evidence.
A self-assessment supplies an evidence reference and a note for A7, and a
result review supplies evidence and reasoning for the grade. An A7 grade
of `fail` is a shortfall against the posting threshold.

## Version 5 review templates

- A bar review sets the field's standard before a candidate is shown. New
  assignments use `exactory-problem-review-v5/bar`. Its prompt adds a sixth
  point about refuted conjectures, the works that refute them and the
  questions about the world that each refutation reveals.
- A slate review compares the current candidates. New assignments use
  `exactory-problem-review-v5/slate`. Its prompt adds a sentence requiring
  an objection that names any candidate whose purpose is to find where a
  refuted statement still holds.
- A result review assesses the selected statement and its evidence. New
  assignments use `exactory-problem-review-v5/result`. Its prompt includes
  the A7 anchor, and its response grades A7 with evidence.

The adjudicator keeps `exactory-problem-review-v2/adjudicator`. The
identifier `rubric:<review_id>:A7` names a focused adjudication of the A7
grade of the given review, like the other admission items. The
adjudication receives the A7 anchor and its `pass` threshold.

## Earlier assessments and result reviews

A self-assessment or result review graded with exactly the earlier
admission items A1 through A6 is read unchanged. For a statement not yet
posted, the post gate and `next`, which reports the next required action,
list one `problem_rubric_regrade_required` obligation. Its explanation in
`research_harness/research_decisions.py` is:

> Before the post, record a new self-assessment that grades A7 when
> assessment_id names one, and assign the result role again to the reviewer
> of each review in review_ids; that assignment continues the review on the
> current result template.

The obligation's `assessment_id` names the self-assessment to replace, or
is null when it grades A7. Its `review_ids` names the result reviews to
continue and can be empty. Assigning the result role again to the same
reviewer continues the review on the current template in the same session
and slot. The packet carries `problem_refresh`, which describes the
continuation, with reason `prompt_contract_changed`.

Bars and slate reviews recorded on the version 2 templates
(`exactory-problem-review-v2/bar` and
`exactory-problem-review-v2/slate`) stay valid. Recorded
assignments keep their template identifiers and hashes, and the 0.59.0
prompts render byte for byte as before.

## Version 1 bar refresh messages

The messages for `problem_bar_calibration_required`, which identifies a
version 1 bar without the required calibration, now explain the refresh:
assign the bar again to the same reviewer with an empty `context`. The
harness refreshes it on the current bar template in the same session and
slot.

15 tests:

  self-assessment must supply A7 evidence.
- `test_failed_self_a7_is_a_post_shortfall` checks that a failed A7

- `test_new_result_requires_a7` checks that a new result review must grade

- `test_new_result_requires_a7_evidence` checks that a result review must
  supply A7 evidence.
- `test_failed_result_a7_is_released_only_by_its_not_upheld_adjudication`
  checks the focused adjudication of a failed A7 result grade.
- `test_previous_records_read_and_name_one_regrade_obligation_without_mutation`
  checks earlier records, their authenticated reviews, the single regrade

- `test_previous_records_still_refuse_other_admission_item_sets` checks
  that accepting exactly A1 through A6 does not admit other item sets.

  checks that a partial refresh leaves one combined regrade obligation.

## Not in this release

- The harness does not detect a refutation mechanically. The author and
  reviewers judge it from full readings. A reported refutation counts
  unless the record shows it wrong, and the record reads every refuting
  work in full.
- Bars and slate reviews recorded on the version 2 templates are not
  refreshed.
- Problems already posted are not changed.
