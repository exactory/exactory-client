# Exactory 0.73.6

This release makes a recorded post terminal for review work on the posted statement. The release follows 0.73.5.
The work is recorded in `misc/harness-improvement/20261104074403-harness-problems-closed-for-good` of the exactory
repository.

## Why

Since 0.73.0 a recorded post is terminal for the post gate: `next` shows only `problem_already_posted`, and the other
findings of the record move to `after_post`. These rows kept their `next` actions, and a posted statement still
accepted new review work. On a copy of the posted bunkbed record, `review-assignment --check` accepted a new result
turn built from such an action, and each of the two result slots pointed at another part turn. Such reviews cost
reviewer calls and cannot change the post.

## What changed

- A new `bar`, `result` or `adjudicator` assignment of a posted statement is refused with `problem_already_posted`,
  with the details `post_id` and `statement_id`. The refusal covers the next turn of a series, a context repair, a
  staged upgrade, a focused adjudication, a refresh and a replacement, and the child and the planning of a staged
  review. The check compares the statement digest (the claim and the resolution criteria), so a statement under
  another id with the same claim and resolution criteria is refused too. A statement with another claim or other
  criteria can be reviewed.
- `review-run` of a new attempt, `review-run` of a staged task or controller, and `review-output-correction` are
  refused the same way. A refused operation records nothing in the store and makes no reviewer call. The staged
  reservation and the launch authorization check the post again in their own writes, so a post recorded while a
  staged run is in progress stops the run at one of them. A worker that has started then exits without a reviewer
  call, and a reservation that stays is released with `review-run` and `action: "reconcile"`.
- These operations stay available: the replay of a committed request, the acceptance of a captured output,
  reconciliation, the record of a `value-review` or `review-adjudication` that was already observed, and
  `review-context` events.
- The `after_post` rows of the post gate keep every field except `next`. For a posted statement, `status` and
  `series.json` show no prospective review action: the `next` lists of `review_parts` and of staged tasks are empty,
  and the plan entries keep only `kind` and `state`. Progress, findings and incomplete outcomes keep their content.
- `review-assignment {controller_assignment_id}` now prepares its write as every other `review-assignment` does. It
  requires `--expected-revision`, and its `--check` works.
- Records without a post keep their output byte for byte. For every record, the stored records, authentication,
  request reconstruction and the store fingerprint do not change.
- `docs/research-cli.md` states the rule in "Problem creation".
- The regression ledger has six new entries, one for each kind of review work, with their tests.

No stored record changes, and no migration is necessary.

## Checks

- After the merge with 0.73.5, 11 test modules pass on Python 3.12.8 and 3.9.6 with 152 tests and no skipped test:
  the new module, the five changed ones, and the ledger, parity replay, second post and two CI modules. Before the
  merge, 220 test modules (each module that imports a changed module or names a changed code or field) passed with
  3,428 tests on both Pythons, with no skipped test.
- Seven synthetic records give the same store fingerprint, request reconstruction and authentication with 0.73.3 and
  with this release, with and without a post.
- On a copy of the posted bunkbed record (revision 1549), `status` and `next` differ from 0.73.5 only in the two
  `after_post` rows, which lose their `next`. The `review-assignment --check` of the next result turn, which 0.73.5
  accepts, is refused with `problem_already_posted`.
- On copies of the problem records, `status` and `next` of 0.73.5 and of this release are equal for graph rigidity
  and an unposted copy of bunkbed, which hold no post, and for the posted kmp, unknotting and random cokernel
  records. In the posted quantum codes record, the plan entries of two adjudication assignments lose `workflow`
  (null) and `scope`. The comparison of extremal, sidorenko and stretched-lr runs after the pull request opens.
- `codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check` pass. The eleven protected modules are unchanged.

CI on Python 3.9, 3.12 and 3.14 runs after the pull request opens.
