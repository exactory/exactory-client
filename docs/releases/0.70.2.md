# Exactory 0.70.2

This patch lets the final turn of a problem result series fit when the bodies of its objection evidence are large.
The final counts a body that an earlier turn of its series delivered, and an undelivered body travels first in a part
turn of its own.

## Why

A final turn must attach the bodies of the evidence of its material objections (0.69.3). Part turns leave that
evidence out (0.69.2), so a body that only objection evidence names is delivered in no part turn. A math-conjecturer
record (on a 0.69.20 candidate) met this when its recorded result reviews cited two full texts in their findings. Both
slots delivered every part, and then planning the final failed:

```text
review_input_too_large {"stage": "final", "turn": 8, "input_characters": 1184689, "input_limit_characters": 1300000}
review_input_too_large {"stage": "final", "turn": 7, "input_characters": 1227842, "input_limit_characters": 1300000}
```

The two bodies held about 390,000 characters. In the part turns that listed them, the transport had elided both.

## What changed

In `review_problem_parts.py`:

- `find_credited_objection_bodies`: a final does not attach again an objection-evidence body that an earlier valid,
  observed frame of its series actually delivered. The final carries the earlier-delivery marker that part turns use
  for such a body. Mapping excerpts and source deliveries stay attached, because their citation credit needs them in
  the packet that carries them.
- `plan_objection_body_part`: when a final does not fit and some objection-evidence bodies are undelivered, the
  planner plans a part turn without evidence units. It carries the largest prefix of those bodies that fits, listed
  in `objection_bodies` of the part plan. `has_new_objection_body` makes `observe_series` count such a turn as
  progress when it delivered a planned body that no earlier turn delivered. The next planning credits the bodies and
  plans the final.

These stay as they were: a final that fits is unchanged, every objection descriptor is still sent, a body that no
earlier frame delivered still has to be attached, and an invalid part response gives no credit. The recording and
post-gate delivery checks already credit what the current series delivered. No module that a route pins by its bytes
changes.

## Upgrade

No action is necessary.

## Checks

The tests in `tests/test_problem_result_parts_continued.py` cover the credit, the kept excerpts and source deliveries,
the body-only part, its observation as progress, the following final, an invalid body-only response, and a body that
does not fit. On the record's store, both slots ran a body-only part turn and then a final, and both finals were
recorded. The modules `test_problem_result_parts_continued`, `test_problem_result_parts`,
`test_problem_result_contract` and `test_problem_result_history` pass on Python 3.12.8 and 3.9.6 (110 tests on the
0.69.20 candidate), with `codex/generate.py --check` and `git diff --check`. The full suite runs in CI.
