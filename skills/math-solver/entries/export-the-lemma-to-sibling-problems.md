## Trigger

- The attack has produced a lemma whose hypothesis mentions only generic structure (a family of sets, a tensor, a partition of space, a weight family, a certified-computation pipeline) and none of the problem's specifics.
- Sibling problems exist whose bottleneck has the same shape, including variants of the original along another axis: more colours, a different exponent, the pinned version, a different ring, the next dimension, the dual under an exchange of two structures (measure and category).

## Action

1. Strip the lemma of problem-specific hypotheses and state it in its widest true form: a preservation property becomes the class of constructions that share it; the principle a construction yields becomes an axiom, proved consistent by the same construction.
2. List the sibling problems whose bottleneck it fits, including neighbouring conjectures and the original's own variants.
3. Apply it; port a construction to its sibling case at once, using any numerical characterisation already in hand to select the analogous objects.
4. Prove where the lemma stops: the variant for which its bound is sharp.

## Output form

Theorems on sibling problems, and a sharpness statement for the lemma.

## Failure signal

Every sibling needs a hypothesis the lemma lacks; or the lemma's bound is provably not sharp on the siblings, so the original's specifics were doing the work.

## Typical cash-out

New machinery; quantitative improvement on the siblings; barrier (the lemma's sharpness).
